Here's where I think most people plateau. They build a few good skills and then keep running them by hand, one at a time. The jump is chaining them and putting them on a schedule.

1. Chain the ones you already have. Mine go research, then plan, then write, then distribute, and each step just hands its output folder to the next one. Nothing clever, they just agree on where the files live.

2. Put one on a timer. This is the one that changed things for me. There's a job on my machine that fires every hour, finds the comments I haven't replied to, drafts the easy ones, and drops them in a file. I wake up and the work is already sitting there.

3. Keep everything in draft. Nothing I've built posts on its own. It does the finding and the first draft, I do the deciding. That's the only version of this I actually trust, and it's still most of the time saved.
