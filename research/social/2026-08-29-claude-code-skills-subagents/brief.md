## Cross-platform patterns

- **Travels everywhere: name one artifact, then name the exact behavior it changes.** "one file, 170k
  stars, Claude stops doing these four things" (TikTok 677.3K, 27.65x). "One skill replaces a $100/mo
  subscription" (YouTube 285.8K). "/variate generates multiple design variations" (X 62.4K, 1,464
  bookmarks). Vague "skills are powerful" content does not appear anywhere near the top.
- **Travels everywhere: kill a bill or kill a limit.** Higgsfield replacement 285.8K, the Coding Sloth
  video is 22 minutes built on usage limits at 228.1K, "Paste This Into Claude, Never Hit a Token
  Limit Again" 174.0K with **557 comments** (the highest comment count in the YouTube pull), and two
  of the five most-engaged IG carousels are limit-saving habits.
- **Platform-specific:** comment-gates are Instagram's norm (11/26) and TikTok's exception (2/26).
  Long courses win YouTube (course/guide titles median 285.8K vs 180.6K overall) but that is a
  subscriber-scale game. X pays for the confession and the bookmark.
- **Format read:** TikTok top 10 median **35s**, 15 of 26 under 60s. YouTube top 10 median **28 min**.
  Instagram is carousels. Nothing in between is winning.

## Gaps (make these)
- **What agent teams and subagents actually cost you.** Demand: usage limits are the single loudest
  complaint in the pull (Coding Sloth built a 228.1K video on it, Nate B Jones' token-limit video has
  557 comments, two of the top five IG carousels are limit habits) and the Agent Teams news post did
  106.2K on X. Coverage: only **3 of 26** YouTube videos touch agents at all, and the best English one
  did **0.61x its own channel**. Nobody has put a number on it. Platform: YouTube long-form.
- **Why your skill never fires.** Demand: developer.israel's "a skill without invocation control is a
  skill Claude triggers whenever it wants" and the frontmatter carousel are the #2 and #4 most-engaged
  posts in the IG pull, from a small account, on a feed where everything else is a roundup. Coverage: **zero** videos in the YouTube
  pull cover skill mechanics or failure modes. Everything is "install these 5". Platform: YouTube +
  a short.
- **The skill that replaces a paid subscription, done as a build.** Demand: the single highest-reaching
  English skill video in the pull is exactly this (285.8K, 1.64x) and the TikTok plugin equivalent did
  118.2K. Coverage: it worked once, in July, on one channel, for one tool. The pattern is open.

## Saturated (skip or differentiate)
- **"N Claude skills you must install" lists.** 6 of 26 TikTok items, 35-skill and 12-skill threads on
  X, and the same "All 53 Claude Skills" post copy-pasted across two X accounts. To differentiate:
  one skill, built on camera, with a before and after number.
- **The full Claude course.** 10 of 26 YouTube items. High median (285.8K) but it belongs to 200K-1M
  subscriber channels and runs 1 to 6 hours. Not the lane to enter.
- **Gated PDF/DM lead magnets.** 11 of 26 IG posts and 8 of 26 X posts. Fine as a CTA, dead as a
  hook - and the comment pass shows the failure mode: the largest demand cluster on the top skill
  video is seven people who could not find the file they were promised. If you gate, the retrieval
  path has to actually work, or the comment section fills with "where is it" instead of "what next".
