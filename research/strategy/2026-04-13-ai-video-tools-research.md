## Content Patterns & What's Working

### Formats That Dominate
1. **Comparison videos** - "X vs Y vs Z" gets massive views (393K for Kling vs Veo vs Seedance vs Sora)
2. **Full workflow tutorials** - Step-by-step showing the complete pipeline (93K for UGC workflow)
3. **"Free/no limits" angles** - Free access to Veo (46K), free local video gen (422K)
4. **Tool-specific deep dives** - "Master Kling 3.0 in 25 minutes" (147K)
5. **Hot takes** - "Sora is Dead" (388K on WAN Show), "Seedance destroyed everything" (20K)

### Title Patterns
- "How to Make [RESULT] with [TOOL]" - most common, reliable
- "[TOOL] vs [TOOL] vs [TOOL]" - highest ceiling
- "The NEW Way to [RESULT] in 2026" - urgency + recency
- "FREE [TOOL] (No Limits)" - massive click-through
- "I [DID THING] in [SHORT TIME]" - proof + speed

### Gaps Nobody Is Filling
1. **Claude Code + AI video tools** - nobody connects content automation with video generation
2. **Nano Banana for thumbnails** - everyone uses it for UGC faces, nobody shows it for YouTube thumbnails
3. **Complete content factory** - text + images + video + scheduling in one system
4. **Beginner-friendly Kling tutorials** - only 1 beginner tutorial (926 views), demand is clearly there with 678K on the advanced one
5. **AI graphics for carousels/social** - most carousel content is Canva-based, not AI-gen
6. **Wan for content creators** - all Wan content is for ComfyUI power users, not regular creators
