## Hook DNA (from all 6 transcripts) — the most repeatable thing in the whole dataset
Opens in the FIRST 5 SECONDS, no "hey what's up guys". The shared recipe:

1. **THE "most people" reframe (the #1 device — in nearly every hook):** position against what most people do.
   - "Most people use Claude like a fancy Google search" (Dan Martell)
   - "Most people build fancy skills for a cool video, but businesses want 6 boring ones" (Nate Herk)
   - "Almost everyone is prompting Claude Code wrong" (Austin Marchese)
   - "99% of people type basic prompts into Claude" (Sabrina)
2. **Credibility flash, woven in fast:** "400 hours in Claude Code" (Nate), "I build AI products every day at Martell Ventures" (Dan), "I hit 30M views last month" (Sabrina), "Anthropic engineers" (Austin).
3. **Specific numbered promise:** 16 hacks, 7 codes, 6 skills, 4 rules, $10K website, "$61K/mo recreated in 20 min."
4. **Accessibility:** "no technical experience needed," "whether you're new or experienced."
5. **Open loop / tease:** "the last code will change how you use Claude forever" (Sabrina), "stick around for chapter 7" (Metics).
6. **Free asset bribe:** "free swipe file with all 7 codes" (Sabrina).
7. **"Let's get into it"** — straight in.

**Steal this exact frame:** "Most people [do X wrong/basic]. [Fast credibility]. Here are [N specific things] that [outcome], no [barrier] needed. [Tease the best one]. Let's go."
