## What the winners have in common

1. **Cost or comparison in the title.** "10x your Claude Code," "50% cheaper," "vs," "Will Jev replace LLMs." Every build video over 100K this week is one of these.
2. **Under 20 minutes wins.** Of the 14 videos over 50K/day, 9 are under 25 min; the 7-minute Caleb explainer did 565K. The 2h Berman stream did 92K on day 1 from a 1M+ sub base.
3. **Speed to the news.** Every GPT-6/Opus video is 1 day old and already 50K to 163K. The window is 72 hours.
4. **The RoboNuggets title is the one to study.** "Jev will 10x your Claude Code (Here's How)": named tool + your audience's tool + a multiplier + a promise. 435K in 2 days at 11 minutes. That is your title shape, and it proves the Claude Code audience is alive when the hook is a cost win, not a setup.

## What is NOT working

- Setup / "fundamentals" / "for beginners" on a tool that launched more than 2 weeks ago. Hermes is cooling (249K across 12, top 20K/day). Your 057 (456 day 1, 8.5% ret) is consistent with that.
- The OpenAI Agents API lane has no audience yet (6K across 5 videos). 058 is a bet on being first, not on demand.
- Antigravity, Cursor, n8n, "AI coding agents" as search terms: nothing this week.
