### 1. "Claude Code tutorial" - saturated at the generic level, gap for a real engineer

- Zinho Automates - "Claude Code Tutorial: Beginner to Advanced in 20 Minutes" - 140,787
- AI Foundations - "FULL Claude Code Tutorial For Beginners in 2026! (Become a PRO)" (1hr) - 81,451

Title patterns that win: time-boxed ("in 20 Minutes", "in 11 Minutes"), "Beginner to Advanced", "for Non-Coders", "2026" stamp, "FULL / Complete."
Saturated: generic "beginner tutorial" and "full course" from generalist channels. The non-coder angle is being done by generalists (Kevin Stratvert), not by an actual engineer who can go one layer deeper and still keep it simple.
**Tyler's edge / gap:** the honest engineer who builds the hard version at a Fortune 500 but teaches the version anyone can start with. Nobody in this set owns "non-coder, but taught by someone who actually ships."

### 2. "Claude Cowork" - wide-open lane, one creator owns it

# RANKED VIDEO IDEAS

## (a) Standalone videos

1. **Claude Cowork on My Phone: 5 Tasks That Now Run While I Sleep**
   Angle: the new mobile/web + background cloud tasks, shown as real recurring jobs firing with the laptop closed.
   Why it works: Cowork is wide open (only Tina Huang + official), the mobile/background feature is brand new, and "runs while I sleep" is honest, not hype. Rides the 300k-395k Cowork demand.

2. **I Connected Claude to My Whole Business (No Code, Just Connectors)**
   Angle: Cowork/Code connectors to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, ClickUp - the tools not the theory - and one real task per connector.
   Why it works: the MCP middle is empty (official intro vs 3.5hr build). Non-dev "connect the tools that matter" is the unowned slice, and it is exactly Tyler's Arcade framing applied to his own stack.

3. **The Honest Version of "Claude Runs My Business"**
   Angle: a direct, no-hype counter to the $1M / zero-employees / 30-days videos. What actually got automated, what still needs a human, real runs.
   Why it works: 880k of proven appetite (Olgaard) but the whole lane is hype. An engineer who names the limits is the trust play nobody in that set offers. No dollar amount in the title, on purpose.

4. **Claude Code for Non-Coders, Taught by an Engineer**
   Angle: the non-coder on-ramp, but from someone who ships production code, so it goes one layer deeper without getting scary.
   Why it works: "for Non-Coders" pulls views (Kevin Stratvert 28k) but is done by generalists. The engineer-who-keeps-it-simple positioning is Tyler's whole brand and is unowned here.

5. **Which MCP Servers Are Actually Worth It (I Tried a Bunch)**
   Angle: an honest curation - the handful of MCP connections that earn their context cost, and the ones to skip.
   Why it works: mirrors the proven "I tried 100+ skills, these are best" format (Tech With Tim 43k) but for MCP, where no curation video exists yet.

6. **Build Your Own Claude Skill for the One Task You Hate**
   Angle: pick a single repetitive task, build one SKILL.md live in about ten minutes, run it. Non-dev friendly.
   Why it works: skill roundups are all dev-only. "Build your OWN for YOUR task" is the doable, teach-a-friend angle. Reuses the live 2-minute skill build Tyler already has (040).

7. **Cowork vs Claude Code vs Chat: Which One to Actually Open**
   Angle: the plain decision rule - a quick answer, an outcome you would hand an assistant, or building the machinery.
   Why it works: definitional "X vs Y vs Z" formats over-index (Apna College 391k). Tyler can do the useful version tied to real jobs, not a vocabulary lesson.

8. **I Gave Claude My Inbox for a Week (Here Is What Happened)**
   Angle: Cowork/agent triaging Gmail, drafting replies in his voice, in draft/safe mode. The wins and the misses.
   Why it works: inbox is the universal pain, the Microsoft 365 + Gmail write-tools are newly capable, and "here is what happened" honesty fits the voice. Draft-not-send is the trust beat.
