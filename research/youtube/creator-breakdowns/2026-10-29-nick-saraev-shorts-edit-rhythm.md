## What he does (from the frame sheets)

- **Captions:** bold white caps, 2-3 words, lower third. One keyword per line turns
  yellow or orange, sometimes with an underline. There's no fade: each line snaps in.
- **Cutaways:** b-roll and stock on the noun he's saying (money, a family, bankruptcy).
  An image or stamp slams in, like the red "BANKRUPT" stamp or "1 HOUR" sliding across.
- **Transitions:**
  - orange/warm light-leak flash (7.4s, 35.8s);
  - zoom-through blur into b-roll (14.2s, 33.0s);
  - RGB/slice glitch into a talking-head cut (12.0s);
  - push-in punch on a talking-head jump cut.
- **Graphics:**
  - a white quote card with a green quote mark ("Money doesn't make you happy");
  - a glowing green number ("$48,164");
  - "1 HOUR / 1 MONTH" kinetic text sliding across;
  - red redaction bars over stock faces.
- **Framing:** a tight talking head, with jump cuts that alternate the zoom.
