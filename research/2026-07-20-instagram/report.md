Sources: 5 keyword popular-reel searches (claude code, claude ai, ai automation, ai agents, ai tools for business), 4 competitor profiles (30 days of posts), 8 hashtag intel pulls. 179 unique posts analyzed, 46 from the last 30 days. Covers for the top 14 posts saved in `covers/`.

## What's working this week

**Reels**
- The comment-keyword CTA is the single dominant pattern. Nearly every top performer opens the caption with "Comment JARVIS / agent / BLITZ and I'll send you X". It drives comment velocity (the strongest ranking signal) and builds a DM lead list at the same time. chandlerintelligence pulled 3.45M plays in 13 days with "Comment JARVIS for the full guide".
- Claude content is now a mainstream Instagram niche, not a corner. meech_ward: "You need to know these Claude Code features" 11.4M plays. thevarunmayya: moving memory to Claude, 3M. imjonathanacuna: "Illegal Claude Tips Part 37" 2.6M. stevenshoaf: Claude skill giveaway, 2.4M. Tyler is unusually qualified for exactly this lane.
- Series branding works: "Illegal Claude Tips Part 37" means 36 prior episodes of a repeatable format. Viewers binge series; the algorithm feeds them the next part.
- Recent 30-day reel median across all sources is 26K plays; the winners above that line are almost all Claude or agent-workflow topics.

**Carousels**
- Carousels are the engagement (saves and comments) format for mid-size accounts, not the reach format. chase.h.ai posts a reel and a carousel on the same topic days apart, both gated with "Comment agent for my Claude Code guides".
- Top carousel: chase.h.ai "Comment Jarvis to get my exact Fable 5 OS setup" (score 30.3K, 18 days). aiwithanushka's playbook-style carousels ("Comment SEO for the full playbook") still perform on her account.

**Statics**
- Designed static images are essentially absent from the winners (1 of 179). The static play is static REELS (flash-video style), not feed images. Carousels own the designed-image slot.

## Visual patterns (from the actual covers)

- Real terminal or product UI screenshot on top + talking head below + short bold title in a white rounded pill ("Claude Code 5 best tips"). Authentic UI beats designed graphics for credibility.
- Logo-stack covers: big ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini logos on black, tiny talking head. Instant recognition at feed size.
- JARVIS aesthetic: face + glowing blue dashboard screens. The Iron Man fantasy is the top emotional hook in the agent niche right now.
- Lo-fi UGC selfie + small pill title + Claude logo pinned on top ("Illegal Claude Tips Part 37"). Low production, high relatability, series-branded.
- Every cover has 5 words or fewer of on-image text. All readable at thumbnail size.
