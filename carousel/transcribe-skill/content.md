## Slide 2 - Pain
**Studying other creators is slow**
You watch a 15-minute video at 1.5x speed. Take messy notes. Rewind to catch what they said. Miss the structure entirely. And you still don't have the actual words.

## Slide 3 - Solution
**Now I type: /transcribe [youtube-url]**
It downloads the audio, runs it through OpenAI Whisper, and saves a clean text transcript I can actually work with.

## Slide 4 - How It Works
**What /transcribe actually does:**
1. Downloads audio from any YouTube URL
2. Transcribes with OpenAI Whisper
3. Saves to ~/content/scripts/transcript_[id].txt
4. Works on local video files too
5. Full text, ready for analysis

## Slide 5 - Results
**I transcribe 2-3 reference videos per topic**
Then I feed them into /yt to study hook structure, pacing, and what competitors actually say. It's like having a cheat sheet for every video in your niche.
