## Slide 1 - Cover
**How I Generate YouTube Thumbnails with AI**
No Photoshop. No designer. One command.

## Slide 2 - Pain
**Thumbnails used to be my biggest bottleneck**
Open Canva. Find a background. Cut out my face. Add text. Try 5 layouts. Hate all of them. Spend 45 minutes on something that should take 5.

## Slide 3 - Solution
**Now I type: /thumbnail [concept]**
It generates multiple thumbnail variants using Kie.ai, with my likeness, custom text, and styles optimized for YouTube CTR.

## Slide 4 - How It Works
**What /thumbnail actually does:**
1. Takes your concept or description
2. Generates variants via Kie.ai API
3. Uses your likeness for consistency
4. Creates text overlays that pop
5. Saves options to ~/content/youtube/thumbnails/

## Slide 5 - Results
**Multiple options in under a minute**
I generate 3-4 variants per video, pick the best one, and move on. No design skills needed. The AI handles composition, color, and layout.
