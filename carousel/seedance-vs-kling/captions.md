## Instagram
I ran the same image and same prompt through Seedance 2 and Kling 3.0. The results were completely different.

Save this so you know which one to use for what.

Seedance 2 wins for UGC style content. The motion feels natural, the facial expressions are more human, and the lip sync is better. If you need someone talking to camera or holding a product, Seedance is the pick.

Kling 3.0 wins for product shots and VFX. The lighting is cinematic, the camera movements are smooth, and it handles complex scenes without breaking down.

They're not competing, they're complementary. Use the right tool for the right job.
