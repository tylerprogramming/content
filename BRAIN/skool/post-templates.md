# Skool Post Templates

17 templates from Evelyn Weiss's engagement planner. Each has Title / Body / Media slots. Replace `[community]` with the community name. Follow `brain.md` mindset rules and `../tyler-voice.md` for wording (no em dashes, no hype).

## 1. Encouragement

### 1.1 Motivational encouragement
- Title: `I just wanted to tell you`
- Body: [acknowledge the struggle] + [reframe it positively] + [end with encouragement]
- Example: "Just because a project seems tricky and you have one setback after another doesn't mean it's not meant to be. Sometimes it just takes longer and looks different from what we thought it would. You got this!"

### 1.2 Collective encouragement
- Title: `A word of encouragement`
- Body: Do you have some encouragement for your fellow [community] members? Any words or quotes that keep you going when you don't feel like it?
- Media: motivational image or GIF

## 2. Educational

### 2.1 Answer a member question with Loom
- Title: `[The question as a headline]?`
- Body: @[Member] thank you for the great question, my answer is here.
- Media: Loom, 2-5 min

### 2.2 Tutorial with member expertise
- Title: `[Topic]: [specific tutorial title]`
- Body: Hi [community] members, [why this matters]. @[Member] helped me [do the thing]. We recorded it so you can do the same.
- Media: tutorial video(s)

### 2.3 Notes and learnings
- Title: `Notes from [event / experience]`
- Body: [personal context] + [what you learned] + [why it matters] + "Check it out and let me know what you think"
- Media: video breakdown or written notes

### 2.4a Share YouTube (podcast / feature)
- Title: [descriptive title of the content]
- Body: [greeting] + [context, why it matters] + [link] + "Let me know if you have any questions"

### 2.4b Share YouTube (your own content)
- Title: [tutorial / topic title]
- Body: Hi [community] members, a lot of you have been asking about this. Here's my [resource]. Let me know if you have questions.
- Media: embedded YouTube video

### 2.5 Live Q&A with timestamps
- Title: `[Topic] Live Q&A`
- Body: [YouTube link] + timestamps
- Media: embedded YouTube video

## 3. Discussion

### 3.1 Hot topic / controversial
- Title: `[Provocative question]?`
- Body: Asking for a friend / What do you think? / Let's discuss
- Example: "Can you show me your flex of an ad that converts that was made WITHOUT ads?"
- Media: optional

### 3.2 Accountability check-in
- Title: `Did you [do the thing] yet?`
- Body: Who implemented the [resource / course] already? Would love to see your work or hear your ideas. Let's check out each other's [work] and give feedback.
- Media: GIF or meme

## 4. Transparency

### 4.1 Progress update
- Title: `[Topic] update` or `[Milestone] numbers update`
- Body: [what you did] + [what changed] + [results with data] + "I'd also like to hear from other [members] who've tried [the thing]"
- Media: screenshots, before/after, data

### 4.2 Behind the scenes
- Title: `Behind the scenes: [project / challenge] day [X]`
- Body: [bullet list of updates, real and raw, wins and struggles] + "In the video I walk you through this"
- Media: Loom walkthrough

## 5. Collaboration

### 5.1 Collaboration offer
- Title: `Can I [do thing] with/for you?`
- Body: I'm thinking about [project]. If you want to be featured, drop a [comment / info]. Here are examples of what it would look like.
- Media: examples / mockups

### 5.2 Member visibility
- Title: `Do you have a [thing]? Link it!`
- Body: Hi [community] members, if you've got a [thing] going, share it with the community. Drop a comment and link below.
- Media: optional

### 5.3 Marketplace / collaboration hub
- Title: `[Opportunity type]: [title]`
- Body: Drop the details below using this template:
  - What's the opportunity: [summit / bundle / freebie swap]
  - Who created it: [name]
  - What we need to do: [action]
  - What we get out of it: [benefit]
  - Link or CTA: [share]

### 5.4 Tone-setting / how to collaborate
- Title: `[Value statement]: tap into the power of [community benefit]`
- Body: [remind them of the opportunity] + [set expectations: give to receive] + [list the collaboration posts] + "Let me know if you want more collaboration posts. Ideas welcome."
- Media: community-themed image

## 6. Product development

### 6.1 Offer feedback / help me help you
- Title: `[Product name]... help me help you`
- Body: As I'm working on a resource to help you [outcome], why am I doing this without asking you for feedback? I'd love to hear:
  - a) First impression?
  - b) What's important for you to have?
  - c) What's important that this is NOT?
  - d) Anything missing?
  [outline of what you're building]
- Media: SOS / help image or GIF

## 7. Personal connection

### 7.1 Holiday / celebration
- Title: `Happy [holiday / event]!`
- Body: [personal message] + "Show us your pictures"
- Media: personal photos

### 7.2 Weekend / life check-in
- Title: [short personal update]
- Body: How was your weekend? What were you up to?
- Media: personal photo

## Worksheets (the planner's Part 5)

- Daily routine: when I check notifications / respond / post
- Activity allies tracker: community, owner, last engaged
- Content ideas bank: member question → best post type
- Weekly planner (x4): day, post type, topic, posted? + what worked / what to improve
- Monthly reflection: top posts, best types, best topics, ideas, goals
