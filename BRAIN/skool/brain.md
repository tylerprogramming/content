# Skool Brain

Playbook for community engagement and posting in Tyler's Skool communities (free + paid). Read before writing any Skool post. Source: Evelyn Weiss, "Skool Community Engagement Planner & Posting Guide" (Grow With Evelyn), filed 2026-10-28. Rules below are hers, adopted as-is; stamp `[confirmed YYYY-MM-DD]` once Tyler's own data backs one up, retire rules that stop being true.

Companion: `post-templates.md` (17 post templates with title/body/media slots).

## Mindset (how to show up)

- You are the party host, not the professor. Make introductions, start conversations, keep energy up until it runs on its own. Be the first one dancing.
- Go first, always. Share your struggle before asking for theirs, post progress before asking for check-ins, celebrate small wins before asking them to.
- Appreciation, not entitlement. Nobody owes attention or engagement. Returns are delayed, disproportionate, and compound.
- Lower your status on purpose. Share what you're still figuring out, ask real questions, ask for help, admit what didn't work, be a student alongside.
- Transparency, not trauma dumping. Share what's happening plus the insight; the reader leaves empowered. Never seek sympathy or make the reader responsible for your feelings.
- Trust before teaching. Evelyn's data point: full workshop post got 15 likes / 5 comments; a transparency post about personal struggle got 38 likes / 17 comments. Educational content lands only after people trust you.

## Activity allies

- 2-3 other community builders in complementary (not competing) niches whose members overlap with yours. You engage in theirs, they engage in yours. Genuine, not spam.
- Be an ally in 2-3 communities; recruit 2-3 allies for your own.
- Rules: respect their space, contribute in ways that help the owner, never drop value posts to profile yourself.
- Why: a community with one poster is a ghost town regardless of content quality.

## YouTube reverse organic

- One short video per week (3-5 min, Loom quality is fine) whose topic comes from what's top of mind in the community. It solves a live conversation, not another course to consume.
- Flow: find idea in community threads → package first (title + thumbnail) → brain-dump recording → AI script formula → record walkthrough → 5-min CapCut edit.
- Transcripts accumulate into a knowledge base for offers, tool libraries, and mini courses.

## Post categories (full templates in post-templates.md)

| # | Category | Job | Types |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Encouragement | Emotional connection | motivational, collective |
| 2 | Educational | Expertise and trust | Loom answer, member tutorial, notes/learnings, share YT (feature / own), live Q&A with timestamps |
| 3 | Discussion | Comments | hot topic, accountability check-in |
| 4 | Transparency | Trust | progress update with data, behind the scenes |
| 5 | Collaboration | Member bonds | collab offer, member visibility, marketplace, tone-setting |
| 6 | Product development | Co-create offers | "help me help you" feedback |
| 7 | Personal connection | Human level | holiday, weekend check-in |

Mix rule of thumb: lead with transparency and personal early (trust), layer educational once engagement exists, use discussion and accountability posts to follow up on anything you shipped.

## Operating rhythm

- Daily routine: fixed times for (1) check notifications, (2) respond to comments, (3) post. Write them down.
- Weekly planner: 7 days x post type x topic, then "what worked / what to improve".
- Monthly reflection: top posts, which types and topics won, ideas and goals for next month.
- Keep a running content ideas bank: member question → best post type.
