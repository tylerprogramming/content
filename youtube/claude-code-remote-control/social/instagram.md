## Caption

Most Claude Code Remote Control tutorials only show you the happy path.

Here's what they skip: your terminal has to stay open. You can't start a session from your phone - only continue one already running on your machine. And if your internet drops for ~10 minutes, the session ends.

Still worth using. Once it's running, you can approve tool calls, continue conversations, and run any local skill from your phone - because it's tunneling to your actual machine, not cloud.

Setup takes 30 seconds: /rc to start, /rc again for a QR code, scan from the Claude app or any browser.
