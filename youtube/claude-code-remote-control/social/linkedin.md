# LinkedIn Post — Claude Code Remote Control

---

Most tutorials on Claude Code Remote Control only show the happy path.

Here's what they skip.

Remote Control is a tunnel to your LOCAL machine - not cloud infrastructure. That means your files, your MCP servers, your project configs all stay available when you connect from your phone. That's what makes it actually useful.

But there are real limits worth knowing before you rely on it.

👉 You cannot start a session from your phone - only continue one already running on your machine

👉 If your terminal closes or your internet drops for about 10 minutes, the session ends

👉 Setup is genuinely fast: /rc to start, /rc again for a QR code, scan from the Claude app or any browser

👉 Once connected, you can approve tool calls, continue conversations, and run local skills remotely - anything your machine can do

👉 It's available on all plans now, not just Claude Max

The best use case: you start something on your laptop, step away, and handle the "approve this?" prompts from your phone without having to run back to your desk.
