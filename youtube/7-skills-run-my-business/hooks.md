# Hooks: 7 Claude Code Skills (revised)

**Revision goal:** Lead with PROOF the viewer can see. Make it un-skippable in the first 5 seconds. Show the result, then make the promise.

---

## Hook A (RECOMMENDED) — Proof First, Then Promise

**[SHOW: Screen capture — Blotato content calendar with 32 posts scheduled this week, color-coded by platform. Quick scroll. Then cut to terminal cycling through all 43 skill names.]**

> *(VO starts at 0:03)*
> This is 32 pieces of content going out across YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, and Skool this week. There's no team. No VA. No editor doing my social.
>
> *(at 0:10)*
> Just me, a full-time job, and these 7 Claude Code skills.
>
> *(at 0:14)*
> I'm going to show you all 7, the prompts I use, and the exact Monday morning workflow that connects them. Then I'll show you how to build your own.

**Why it works:**
- **Proof in the first 3 seconds** — the calendar is the receipt
- **Specific numbers** — 32, 7, 7 platforms (verifiable on screen)
- **"Just me + full-time job"** — credibility through constraint
- **Three concrete promises** at the end — what they'll see → makes them stay

---

## Hook B — The Constraint

**[SHOW: Tyler at desk, full-time job calendar open on second monitor, terminal on main, content schedule on third]**

> I work a software engineering job 8 to 4. I have a wife and kids. I post 32 pieces of content a week anyway. Here's the entire system — 7 Claude Code skills — and the prompts I use.

**Why:** The constraint (full-time job + family) is the credibility moat. The numbers verify it's real.

---

## Hook C — The Receipt

**[SHOW: SQL query running in TablePlus — `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM content_pipeline WHERE week='2026-05-17'` returning 32]**

> 32 pieces of content this week. The query you just watched is from my actual content tracker. Here are the 7 Claude Code skills that put them there.

**Why:** SQL receipt is hard to fake. Maximum verifiability. Best for technical audience.

---

## Hook D — The Side By Side

**[SHOW: Two halves of screen — left side blank, right side full of files appearing rapidly]**

> Left side — a creator without skills. Right side — me with 7 skills. Same hour of work. Watch what comes out.

**Why:** Visual contrast hook. Sells the system in 5 seconds without explaining it.

---

## Recommendation

**Use Hook A.** It's the most aligned with the AntiGravity pattern that works for Tyler — show the result, then make the promise. Test Hook C as a swap if Hook A underperforms on technical audiences.

**Avoid Hook B as cold-open** — too much "about me" before proof. Use it as the 30-second framing AFTER the hook lands.
