# Prospect Call - Jenna Rivera (Small Creator)

Date: 2026-06-11
Attendees: Tyler Reed, Jenna Rivera (creator, wellness + productivity niche)

## Summary

Intro call with Jenna Rivera, a small creator in the wellness and productivity space. She has been making content for about eight months, posts mostly Reels and a few YouTube videos, and is sitting somewhere around 1,200 followers across platforms. She joined the free Skool community after a friend recommended it and booked this call because she is at the point where she is wondering whether to keep going or quietly let it fade. So this was as much a gut-check call as a sales conversation.

Her two big questions were honest and a little vulnerable. First, she asked whether it is even worth it, or whether she is just too late - she sees huge accounts everywhere and feels like the window closed before she started, and she wondered if her channel is too small to bother investing in a system. I pushed back on that pretty directly. Small is not too late, small is normal, and the people who win are the ones who build a system and stay in the game while everyone else quits. The accounts she is intimidated by were all small once. The second question was the consistency one - she asked how I actually stay consistent enough to post every day, because she does great for a week and then life happens and she vanishes for two. I walked her through how the system removes the daily decision-making, batches the work, and turns "what do I post today" into a repeatable command instead of a fresh panic each morning.

She left visibly more encouraged. She is not ready to commit to anything paid yet and wants to keep using the free community and try implementing a couple of things on her own first, which I respect. Good potential fit down the line if she sticks with it.
