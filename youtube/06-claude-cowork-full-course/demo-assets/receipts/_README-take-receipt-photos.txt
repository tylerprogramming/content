RECEIPTS DEMO (Module 2B - Build a document from raw inputs)

Before filming, take 5-6 phone photos of real business receipts and drop the
image files in THIS folder. Good mix that makes the demo land:

  - a coffee / lunch receipt
  - a software subscription receipt (e.g. a SaaS tool)
  - a SECOND software subscription that overlaps the first one
    (so Cowork can flag "two tools that do the same thing" - the wow line)
  - gas or a rideshare
  - a hardware/tool purchase
  - one more of anything

On-screen safety: these are real receipts, so before recording glance for
anything you do not want public - full card numbers (usually masked already),
home address, etc. Crop or blur in edit if needed.

The script's prompt (Demo 2B) asks Cowork to read each photo, build an expense
spreadsheet (vendor, date, amount, category), and write expenses-summary.md with
the total, biggest category, and any duplicate/forgotten subscription.
