## Top 3 Recommendations

### Pick 1: "Claude Cowork Full Course: Build Your AI Employee From Scratch"
- "Full Course" nails the format and the contested high-intent search term Tech With Tim and KJ Rainey are competing for.
- "Build Your AI Employee From Scratch" is the transformation promise (echoes KJ Rainey's proven "Zero To Working AI Employee" at 310K) without copying it.
- "From Scratch" signals complete and beginner-safe, which matches the no-coding audience.
- Strong SEO stack: "claude cowork" + "full course" + outcome.

### Pick 2: "Claude Cowork: The Complete Course (Everything You Need in 1 Hour)"
- Owns the "Complete Course" and "Everything" framing in one title.
- "Everything You Need in 1 Hour" sets the runtime expectation up front, which protects retention by attracting people who actually want the long version.
- Reads as the definitive reference, which is exactly the evergreen and Skool-classroom job this video is built for.

### Pick 3: "Claude Cowork Full Course: 10 Modules to an AI That Works While You Sleep"
- "Full Course" plus "10 Modules" signals depth and structure (great for the course and SEO).
- "Works While You Sleep" is the scheduled-tasks payoff, the most shareable and curiosity-driving promise in the whole video.
- Best browse-traffic option of the three because the back half is a benefit, not just a format label.
