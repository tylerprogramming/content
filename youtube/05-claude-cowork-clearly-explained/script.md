# Claude Cowork Clearly Explained - Script

**Target runtime:** ~18 min (matches the 966K model video)
**Format:** Total-beginner walkthrough. Empathy intro, 3 escalating live demos, honest limitations, soft CTA.
**Voice:** Calm, warm, "I've got you." You are the real engineer who makes this simple. Never rushed, never salesy.
**Filming note:** Every demo is REAL. Run it live, let it finish on camera, open the result. Do not fake or speed-lie. If a task takes 4 minutes, say "I'll speed this up" and cut, but show the genuine before/after.

> So what actually is Cowork, and why is everyone losing their minds over it?
>

## 6. DEMO 1 - Organize a messy folder (EASY) (~2:30)

> Demo one, the easiest possible start. We are going to take a messy folder on my computer and have Cowork clean the entire thing up.
>

It cannot wander around the rest of your computer. So I am going to point it at my Downloads folder, which, like everybody's, is an absolute disaster.

[B-ROLL: open the folder picker, select Downloads, the "allow Claude to change files in this folder" confirmation]

> When I pick the folder, it asks me to confirm, allow Claude to make changes in this folder. Yes. That is me handing it the keys to this one room and no others.
>
> Over here on the right I can pick my model, and I can leave thinking on. Then I just tell it, in plain English, what I want.

[ON SCREEN, type the prompt:] "Go through this folder, rename every file so the name actually describes what it is, and sort everything into clean subfolders so I can find things later."

> No fancy prompting. I am just telling it the goal, like I would tell an assistant.
>
> And now I walk away. You can watch it work on the right, it scans everything first, catalogs what is there, then starts renaming and filing. I am going to speed this up, but in real time this took about four minutes.

[B-ROLL: progress panel on the right, then cut to result]

> Think about how long that would take you by hand. Open each file, look at it, rename it, decide where it goes. Nobody does it, that is why your Downloads looks like mine. And here, done. ## 10. HONEST LIMITATIONS (~1:30)

> Now I am not going to pretend it is perfect, because that is not fair to you. It is still a research preview, which means it is the worst it will ever be and it is already this good, but there are real limits today.
>
> First, it does not have memory across sessions. Normal Claude remembers your past chats. Cowork does not, yet. So if you want it to remember things, you set that up yourself by keeping notes in your folders.
>
> Second, it lives on your desktop only. There is no mobile version, you have to be at your computer.
>
> And third, the app has to stay open while it works. This is the big one. If you close the app or your laptop goes to sleep, the task stops. So when you give it a long job, leave it running.
>
> None of that is a dealbreaker. But you deserve to know it going in, so you are not confused when you hit it.
