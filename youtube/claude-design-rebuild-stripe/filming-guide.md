# Filming Guide — Rebuild Stripe in Claude Design

## Pre-recording prep

- [ ] Fresh Claude Design account with weekly usage at 0% or near it (need ~20-25% of Pro for a recreation this complex)
- [ ] Take a clean screenshot of Stripe.com hero section (1440px wide, PNG, no browser chrome) — save to `~/content/youtube/claude-design-rebuild-stripe/stripe-hero-reference.png`
- [ ] Take 3-4 additional screenshots of Stripe sections (pricing, testimonials, feature grid, footer) for side-by-side comparisons later in the video
- [ ] Close all browser tabs except Stripe.com and claude.ai/design
- [ ] Hide bookmarks bar, zoom to 125%
- [ ] Camtasia: 1080p, screen + camera tracks

## Shot list

1. **Open:** screen recording of Stripe.com home + your Claude Design blank canvas (for split-screen hook)
2. **Why it's hard:** slow-scroll Stripe.com with pauses at hero, pricing, testimonials, footer
3. **Screenshot trick:** drag the saved PNG into Claude Design, show the prompt being typed
4. **Plan-mode Q&A:** screen record all 5 questions + your answers
5. **First generation:** full-screen scroll of the generated page
6. **Honest review:** zoom into weak spots (soft gradient, missing globe, testimonial height)
7. **Iterations:** record all 3 iteration moments live — tweaks, comment, draw
8. **Reveal:** synced split-screen scroll of Stripe vs your rebuild
9. **Usage:** close-up of Claude Design usage dashboard
10. **Decision slide:** pre-built graphic, narrated over
11. **Outro:** talking head + Skool preview

## Key visual moments to get right

- **Hook side-by-side** — needs to be both sites scrolling in near-sync. Record each separately then edit together.
- **Draw-mode globe fix** — the moment you circle the flat map and write "3D rotating globe" is the most satisfying beat. Don't rush it.
- **Final reveal split-screen** — let it breathe. 10 full seconds of scrolling match, not 2.

## On-camera tips

- Keep voice confident. The result is the payoff — let it land.
- When something breaks (and it will), show the fix calmly. This is a trust-builder video.
- Don't hype the result. Let viewers decide if 90% is good.

## Target runtime: 16-17 minutes
