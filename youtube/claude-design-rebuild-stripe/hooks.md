# Hooks — I Rebuilt Stripe's Homepage in Claude Design

Three options. Pick one. All borrowed from Chase/Nate proven patterns.

---

## Hook 1 ⭐ Recommended — Proof Open (Chase-style urgency + visual reveal)

> Stripe's homepage is one of the best-designed sites on the internet. I just rebuilt it in Claude Design in 20 minutes with one prompt and three iterations.

*[SHOW: Split-screen — left is Stripe.com live, right is the Claude Design canvas rendering the rebuild in real-time]*

> No layers. No design system imports. No Figma. Just Claude Design and a screenshot.

*[SHOW: Quick cut to final rebuilt page scrolling]*

> I'll show you the full process, the exact prompts, and the one iteration trick that got me 90 percent of the way there in five minutes.

*[SHOW: Tyler on camera, confident]*

> Let's go.

**Technique:** Pattern matches Chase's "updates never stop" opener but specific. Shows the end result in the first 5 seconds. Names the "one trick" curiosity spike.

---

## Hook 2 — Challenge Open (Nate-style energy)

> All right, so Claude Design just launched and everyone's using it to build generic SaaS landing pages. I wanted to see if it could actually hold up against real top-tier design work. So I gave it 20 minutes to rebuild Stripe's homepage.

*[SHOW: Timer animation starting at 20:00 over Stripe.com homepage]*

> Here's what happened.

*[SHOW: Cut to final rebuild with confetti-style reveal]*

**Technique:** Nate's "they just dropped this" urgency + challenge setup. Makes the stakes feel real.

---

## Hook 3 — Specific Screenshot Open

> This is Stripe's homepage. This is what Claude Design built in 20 minutes from a single screenshot prompt. They're almost identical.

*[SHOW: Side-by-side static comparison for 3 full seconds so viewers can register both]*

> Let me show you exactly how I did it, what I had to fix, and which parts Claude Design nailed and which parts it got wrong.

**Technique:** Extreme specificity. No setup. Proof-first with honest-review framing (nailed vs got wrong).

---

## Selection guidance

- **Hook 1 is the pick.** Instant proof visual, names the "one trick" curiosity, 22-25 seconds on camera.
- Energy: confident but not hyped. The Stripe comparison does the selling.
- Keep the Stripe.com live shot onscreen for at least 2 solid seconds at the open — viewers need to register it's the real Stripe site.
