One idea on the thumbnail, 3-5 words, Tyler's face + a real expression. Title and thumbnail must NOT repeat each other - they stack. ## Packaging principles (reuse on any low-search idea)
1. Promise a RESULT or NUMBER, not the process. "$7 server" beats "I automated my hosting."
2. One idea per thumbnail. 3-5 words max. It reads in under a second or it failed.
3. Title and thumbnail stack, they do not repeat. Thumbnail shows the shock, title gives the context.
4. Use a real face expression (shock, smirk, sleep). Generate with /thumbnail + tylerai.png.
5. A dollar figure or a clean number in the title is the single highest-leverage CTR move for Tyler.
6. If the topic is niche (Pinterest, LinkedIn), bury the platform and lead with the outcome.
