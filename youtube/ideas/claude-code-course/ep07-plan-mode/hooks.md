# Episode 7 — Hook Options

## Hook A (RECOMMENDED)

> Here's the biggest mistake I see beginners make with Claude Code. They jump straight into building. They type a prompt, Claude starts editing files, and ten minutes later they realize it built the wrong thing. There's a better way. Plan Mode lets you explore your codebase and map out exactly what Claude will do — before it touches a single file. Let me show you how it works.

## Hook B

> What if Claude could think through your entire feature before writing a single line of code? That's exactly what Plan Mode does. You toggle it on, ask Claude what you want, and instead of editing files it reads your code, thinks about it, and gives you a step-by-step plan. You review the plan, tweak it if you want, then flip Plan Mode off and let it build. It's the difference between hoping it works and knowing it will. Let me show you.
