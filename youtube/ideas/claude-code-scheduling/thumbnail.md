# Thumbnail Analysis & Brief

## Reference Thumbnail 1: Brock Mesarich — "Claude is Now a 24/7 Employee"

**Layout:** Two-column split. Left ~60%: giant text. Right ~40%: Claude Cowork logo (orange rounded square with asterisk icon).

**Color scheme:** Dark charcoal/near-black background. Orange (#E8622A) for "EMPLOYEE" text and the logo. White for "24/7". Orange border frame around entire image.

**Typography:** "24/7" in massive white bold condensed sans-serif — fills almost half the height. "EMPLOYEE" below in same weight but orange. No subtitle. Two words total.

**Visual elements:** Just the Claude Cowork app icon — no screenshots, no clutter, no face.

**Composition:** Extremely minimal. Maximum ~5 elements total. High contrast reads perfectly at small size.

**What makes it clickable:** The "24/7 EMPLOYEE" phrase is aspirational and immediately clear. The Claude logo signals "this is about Claude." Zero cognitive load — you get the concept in under a second.

## Reference Thumbnail 2: WorldofAI — "Claude KILLED OpenClaw"

**Layout:** Busy — text dominates left 60%, floating UI screenshots on right. Mascot characters bottom-center.

**Color scheme:** Dark background with orange particle/wave texture. Bright orange for "CLAUDE", hot pink/red for "KILLED", white for "OPENCLAW". Orange border frame.

**Typography:** Three-color treatment on the main text. Very large, aggressive. Comic-style energy.

**Visual elements:** Two cartoon mascots (Claude bear vs. OpenClaw robot), code editor screenshots floating on the right, starburst graphic top-left, orange glow effects.

**What makes it clickable:** Drama/conflict framing. "KILLED" is an extreme word. The mascot characters add personality and the vs. dynamic creates tension.
