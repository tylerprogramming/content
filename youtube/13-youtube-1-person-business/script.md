# Script: How I Run a 1-Person YouTube Business with Claude Code (Full Course)

**Target:** 60-90 min flagship course. Riff each module, do not read verbatim. Every module = a chapter (signpost so viewers can skip).
**Structure source:** Patrick Dang 416K (hook + "pain point → skill" + free-skill lead magnet + 30-day plan) layered over Albert Olgaard 530K full-course pacing (vision-first open, concept → setup → methodology → hands-on build, aggressive chapter signposting).

> CTA rule: ONE primary CTA - the funnel (free.tylerai.dev/youtube/). Give away the capstone skill as the lead magnet. No em dashes, spoken or written.

---

## MODULE 0 - COLD OPEN + THE PROMISE (0:00-3:00)

[SHOW: fast montage of the system running - terminal cycling through /yt-search, /yt, /thumbnail, /shorts; Studio analytics; a scheduled upload; the email funnel.]

Open on the payoff (see hooks.md, Hook 1). Land: one person, whole channel, Claude Code doing the heavy lifting. State the promise: "by the end you'll have built your own first skill." Expectation-set: "this is a system, not get-rich-quick - it compounds."

[NOTE: retention-critical 30s. Open ON proof, not setup.]

---

## MODULE 1 - WHY NOW (3:00-7:00)

[SHOW: talking head + simple stat overlays.]

The opportunity: the creator is now a one-person media company. AI makes the whole pipeline runnable solo. Contrarian framing (Patrick Dang): everyone says build an app or start a faceless channel - the better play is running a real channel as a business where AI does the heavy lifting. Why YouTube specifically: owned audience, compounding library, funnels to email + offers.

Signpost: "Here's the whole map." [SHOW: the pipeline node diagram.] "Chapters are in the description - skip to any piece."

---

## MODULE 2 - SETUP + WHAT A SKILL IS (7:00-15:00)

[SHOW: Claude Code install, an empty SKILL.md, running a skill.]

For non-devs: what Claude Code is, install it, and what a skill actually is - a markdown file with a name, a description, and instructions. No SDK, nothing to install.
[SHOW: run one existing skill end to end so they see the payoff before the theory.]
The description field is the part that matters (it's how Claude knows when to use the skill). Briefly: skills vs MCP vs plugins, one sentence each.

Signpost: "If you've used Claude Code, skip to 15:00."

---

## MODULE 3 - THE RESEARCH ENGINE (15:00-25:00)

[SHOW: `/yt-search` live on a real topic; the ranked long-form + shorts report; thumbnails downloading.]

Prompt shown: `/yt-search <topic>`. Show it returning proven videos ranked by views, split long-form vs shorts, with thumbnails. Then `/yt-deep-research` for a deeper dive on the top 2. Point: you're not guessing what to make - you're starting from proven demand.

[NOTE: use a real, safe topic. React to one insight you wouldn't have spotted.]

---

## MODULE 4 - LEARN FROM WINNING VIDEOS (25:00-32:00)

[SHOW: `/transcribe <competitor URL>` → transcript appears.]

Pull a top competitor's transcript to model structure and hooks. Point: you can reverse-engineer what's working without watching 10 videos. (Meta moment: "I did this exact thing to plan this course - I transcribed two 1-person-business videos.")

---

## MODULE 5 - PLAN THE FULL VIDEO (32:00-42:00)

[SHOW: `/yt <transcript>` generating the full package - analysis, titles, hooks, script, filming guide.]

The core planning skill. Show it asking you questions and generating titles, hooks, a script, and a filming guide. Then `/seo` to optimize the title/description/tags, and `/save-idea` to bank extra angles. Point: research-to-script in minutes, grounded in the reference, not a blank page.

---

## MODULE 6 - THUMBNAILS + MOTION GRAPHICS (42:00-52:00)

[SHOW: `/thumbnail` generating variants (Nano Banana); then `/ai-video` rendering a kinetic-text or terminal overlay.]

AI thumbnails with your likeness, multiple variants for A/B testing. Then motion graphics with `/ai-video` (Remotion) for shorts overlays and b-roll. Point: the visuals that usually need a designer/editor, done from the terminal.

---

## MODULE 7 - POST-PRODUCTION (52:00-58:00)

[SHOW: `/chapters` on a finished mp4 → accurate timestamps written into the description.]

After the edit: `/chapters` extracts audio, transcribes, and writes real timestamps. Mention captions (Submagic) briefly. Point: the tedious finishing work, automated.

---

## MODULE 8 - ONE VIDEO INTO A WEEK OF SHORTS (58:00-66:00)

[SHOW: `/shorts` and `/repurpose` turning one long video / research into multiple YouTube Shorts scripts.]

Turn one long-form into a week of YouTube Shorts. Show the scripts being generated. (Keep it YouTube - Shorts, not cross-platform.) Point: one filming session feeds the whole week.

---

## MODULE 9 - UPLOAD, SCHEDULE, OPTIMIZE (66:00-74:00)

[SHOW: `/youtube` uploading + scheduling a video with title, description, tags, thumbnail; then `/analytics`.]

Upload and schedule straight from the terminal (real tags, custom thumbnail, publish-at). Set up a title/thumbnail A/B test. Then `/analytics` to read views, CTR, retention, and close the loop - what to make next. Point: the optimization loop is part of the system.

---

## MODULE 10 - ENGAGEMENT ON AUTOPILOT (74:00-79:00)

[SHOW: `/yt-replier` finding unreplied comments and drafting replies.]

Reply to YouTube comments at scale - the monitor finds unreplied comments, drafts keyword-aware replies (with your funnel link where it fits), you approve and post. Point: engagement that usually eats an hour a day, handled.

---

## MODULE 11 - TURN VIEWERS INTO AN EMAIL LIST (79:00-86:00)

[SHOW: the funnel page free.tylerai.dev/youtube/, then `/email` building a drip.]

The money module. A video view is rented attention - email is owned. The play: a free skill as the lead magnet → funnel captures the email → `/email` runs the welcome drip and broadcasts. This is exactly the funnel in this course's own description. Point: turn the channel into a list you own.

---

## MODULE 12 - CAPSTONE: BUILD YOUR FIRST SKILL + 30-DAY PLAN (86:00-92:00)

[SHOW: building a brand-new skill live from an empty folder, then running it.]

Build a simple, useful skill from scratch on camera (talk to Claude, it writes the SKILL.md, run it). This is the skill you give away as the lead magnet.
Then the 30-day start plan (Patrick Dang style):
- Days 1-7: install Claude Code, build your first skill, pick your channel topic
- Days 8-14: research + plan your first 2 videos
- Days 15-21: film + use the thumbnail/shorts skills
- Days 22-30: upload, set up the email funnel, read analytics, iterate

CTA: "Grab the free skills and get on the newsletter at free.tylerai.dev/youtube/. Tell me in the comments which skill you'd build first." (Secondary, brief: Skool community.)
