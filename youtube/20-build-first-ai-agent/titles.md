# Titles - Build Your First AI Agent (The Easy Way, No Code)

Scoring key: Clarity / Curiosity / CTR pull, each 1-10. Total out of 30.
CTR pull = how hard the average beginner who is curious about AI agents thumb-stops on it.

## Scored options

1. **Build Your First AI Agent (The Easy Way, No Code)**
   Clarity 9 / Curiosity 7 / CTR 8 = 24
   The anchor. "First," "easy," "no code" all speak straight to the beginner who thinks this is out of reach. Clear promise, low intimidation.

2. **How to Build an AI Agent in 10 Minutes (No Code)**
   Clarity 9 / Curiosity 8 / CTR 8 = 25
   Time promise plus "no code." "10 minutes" makes it feel doable in one sitting. Very clickable, slightly more tutorial-flavored.

3. **AI Agents Are Easier Than You Think (I'll Prove It)**
   Clarity 7 / Curiosity 9 / CTR 8 = 24
   Belief-flip plus a challenge. High curiosity, "I'll prove it" promises a live demo. Loses a point on clarity (doesn't say you build one).

4. **You Don't Need a CS Degree to Build an AI Agent**
   Clarity 8 / Curiosity 9 / CTR 8 = 25
   Speaks the exact objection out loud. Strong for the beginner who feels locked out. Slightly long for a thumbnail pairing.

5. **I Built AI Agents at Fortune 500 Companies. Here's the Easy Version.**
   Clarity 8 / Curiosity 9 / CTR 9 = 26
   Leads with the moat (real authority) then hands over the easy version. The credibility gap is the click. Strongest CTR, needs the thumbnail to carry the "easy."

6. **Build a Real AI Agent Today (Beginner Friendly)**
   Clarity 8 / Curiosity 6 / CTR 7 = 21
   Clear and safe but flat. "Beginner friendly" is a weaker hook than "no code" or the credibility line.

7. **Everyone Makes AI Agents Sound Hard. They're Not.**
   Clarity 7 / Curiosity 9 / CTR 8 = 24
   Pure belief-flip, matches the cold open word for word. Great curiosity, doesn't promise the build clearly.

8. **Your First AI Agent, Built in Plain English (No Code)**
   Clarity 9 / Curiosity 7 / CTR 8 = 24
   "Plain English" is the mechanism and the reassurance in three words. Clean, honest, on-brand.

9. **I Made an AI Agent That Does My Research For Me**
   Clarity 8 / Curiosity 8 / CTR 8 = 24
   Showcase framing tied to the actual agent we build. Concrete outcome. Good B-option, slightly narrows the promise to research.

10. **Stop Overcomplicating AI Agents. Build One in 10 Minutes.**
    Clarity 8 / Curiosity 8 / CTR 8 = 24
    Command plus time promise. Punchy. Slight risk of sounding preachy without the warm delivery.

## Top 3 recommendations
1. **I Built AI Agents at Fortune 500 Companies. Here's the Easy Version.** (26) - highest CTR, leads with the one thing no competitor can copy (real authority), then disarms with "easy."
2. **How to Build an AI Agent in 10 Minutes (No Code)** (25) - cleanest doable promise, best search fit ("how to build an AI agent"), lowest intimidation.
3. **You Don't Need a CS Degree to Build an AI Agent** (25) - names the exact belief blocking the beginner, high curiosity.

## FINAL PICK
**Build Your First AI Agent (The Easy Way, No Code)**

Why the anchor wins as the primary: this is the niche TEST video, and the job of the title is to pull the broadest possible beginner-agent audience with the lowest possible intimidation. "First," "easy," "no code" remove every reason to not click, and it reads clean at a glance next to the thumbnail. It also nails search for the huge "build your first AI agent" / "AI agent no code" queries, which is where the untapped audience actually lives. The sharper credibility line (#5) is stronger on raw CTR but is doing a different job (it sells authority), so it becomes the A/B challenger and the thumbnail carries the Fortune-500 proof either way. Keep the title low-friction, let the thumbnail carry the intrigue.

## A/B plan (YouTube Test & Compare)
- **A (ship first):** Build Your First AI Agent (The Easy Way, No Code)
- **B (challenger):** I Built AI Agents at Fortune 500 Companies. Here's the Easy Version.
- Run both through native Test & Compare from launch. Keep the thumbnail identical across A and B so the test isolates the title.
- Watch: does B's credibility framing lift CTR above channel baseline? If yes, "authority-first" becomes the template for the whole agents pillar. If A wins, low-friction beginner framing is the lane.
- Because this is the niche test, log the winner and the CTR delta against the Claude-Code showcase baseline in the video_learnings post-mortem. That comparison is the real deliverable of this video.
