# Thumbnail briefs — AI Agent remakes (050-054)

For the thumbnail designer. Five videos. Each has a recommended concept + one A/B backup.

## Brand rules (apply to all five)
- **Tyler's face** in every one — calm, confident, a little impressed. NOT shocked/clickbait-face. The honesty is the brand.
- **ONE big short phrase**, 3-4 words max, huge, readable on a phone at ~320px wide.
- **Orange #D97757 accent on exactly ONE word or one element** per thumbnail. Never spread it. Everything else white.
- **Near-black / dark background**, high contrast. Face on one third, text on the other — never overlapping.
- No money amounts, no hype words.

---

## 050 — How To Build AI Agents in 2026 (5 Steps, No Code)
**Big text:** `AI AGENT` (white) / `IN 5 STEPS` — the **5** huge in orange.
**Visual:** Tyler left third, slightly turned, confident. Right two-thirds: the big text, with a faint node diagram behind it (Claude in the middle, thin orange lines out to a Gmail icon and a calendar icon) so it reads "connects to real apps."
**Backup (A/B):** `IT DID MY INBOX` (INBOX in orange) over a brief/inbox card with an orange check and 5 flagged emails; Tyler pointing at it.

## 051 — Scrape ANY Website With This AI Agent (2026)
**Big text:** `SCRAPE ANY WEBSITE` (two lines, **ANY** in orange).
**Visual:** Tyler left 60%, slight lean-in "watch this" look. Right 40%: a busy/chaotic website with a bold orange arrow sweeping into a clean spreadsheet filling with rows. Mess in → order out. Small orange "2026" chip in a corner.
**Backup (A/B):** center orange arrow between a small website (left) and a full spreadsheet (right); Tyler smaller, bottom-left, pointing up at the arrow. Text: `INTO A SPREADSHEET` (SPREADSHEET orange).

## 052 — 100% Local AI Agents in 2026 (Free, Private, No API Keys)
**Big text:** `100% LOCAL` (100% white, **LOCAL** orange), stacked, top-left.
**Visual:** Tyler right half, slight lean-in, one hand toward the text. Behind him a dark desk: laptop with a terminal + a red-slashed cloud icon (data NOT going up). Small orange `NO API KEY` tag, bottom-right.
**Backup (A/B):** `WIFI OFF` (orange, with a struck-through wifi icon) and a smaller `STILL WORKS` above; Tyler pointing at a laptop showing airplane mode + a terminal mid-run. (The "how does it work offline?" curiosity angle.)

## 053 — How To Use AI Agents To Do ALL Your Work (Claude Code)
**Big text:** `AGENTS DID IT ALL` (**ALL** in orange). If too small on two lines, drop to `DID IT ALL`.
**Visual:** Tyler left, chest up, looking slightly off-camera at the screen, mildly impressed. Right: a phone/screen with an inbox visibly emptying / labels stacking, glowing. A small orange check or arrow sweeping across it = "the work is done."
**Backup (A/B):** `WHILE I SLEPT` (SLEPT in orange); Tyler with a relaxed "wasn't me" gesture; a dark dashboard at 6:00 AM with a briefing doc + green checks, small orange `6:00 AM` tag.

## 054 — I Fully Automated My Instagram With AI Agents
**Big text:** `MY INSTAGRAM` (white) / `RUNS ITSELF` (orange).
**Visual:** Tyler right, arms slightly out, calm "look, no hands" expression. Left/behind: a faint Instagram grid dissolving into terminal text (Instagram + code at a glance). Subtle agent/robot chip badge over the grid, not cartoonish.
**Backup (A/B):** `I DIDN'T` (white) / `POST THIS` (orange) over a real IG post mockup with an auto-generated caption + reply; Tyler in the corner, dry half-grin "wasn't me." (Keep the face dry, not overacted.)
