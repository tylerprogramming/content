read off the actual images in Studio rather than assumed.

## The channel's own two eras, side by side

**Legacy (10 to 15% CTR):** `SPLAY TREE EXPLAINED`, `RADIX SORT`, `SKIP LISTS`,
`FIRECRAWL FULL COURSE`, `CREWAI NO CODE`. Huge text filling the frame, two or
three words, on flat bright green or orange. No face. One idea, readable at
thumbnail size.

**Current (0.9 to 3.6% CTR):** small text, a neutral face at the right, three or
four competing elements - product logo, icon grid, a badge, a duration - on a
dark background that blends into YouTube's dark UI.

## The 0.9% thumbnail

`Claude Opus 5 Cut My API Bill in Half` is the worst CTR on the channel and the
odd one out visually: **mostly empty white, a small orange sunburst, and the
words "Is Opus 5 the Best?" set tiny at the top.** No face, no contrast, no
subject. It received 19,334 impressions - the most of any video - and converted
0.9% of them.

Every other recent thumbnail at least has a face. This one is closest to blank,
and it has the worst number. That is as clean a natural experiment as the
channel is going to produce on its own.

## What is wrong with the current set, concretely

1. **Text is too small to read in the feed.** On a phone a thumbnail renders
   about 210 pixels wide. `17 SKILLS` survives that. `Is Opus 5 the Best?` does
   not. The legacy thumbnails work because two words fill the frame.
2. **Dark backgrounds vanish.** YouTube's UI is dark. A dark thumbnail has no
   edge. Every high-CTR video on this channel sits on flat bright green or
   orange.
3. **Too many elements.** Face plus product logo plus icon grid plus badge is
   four things competing at a size where one thing barely reads.
4. **The face is neutral.** BRAIN already recorded this on 2026-06-21: *"Real
   expressive face (genuine smile/pointing) OR clean command/concept hero. Flat
   faces and AI-cartoon characters = ~3% CTR."* The channel has been sitting in
   that 3% band ever since, which is the rule being confirmed rather than
   applied.

## The rule that falls out

**One idea, three words maximum, filling the frame, on a colour that is not
dark.** Add a face only if it is doing something - pointing, reacting - not
just present.

The legacy thumbnails were not better because they were about data structures.
They were better because they obeyed that rule, and the current ones do not.

## Order of work

1. **Opus 5** - 19,334 impressions already earned, 0.9% conversion, and the
   thumbnail is nearly blank. New thumbnail before anything else gets filmed.
2. **I Built a YouTube Business That Runs Itself** - published 08-12, sitting at
   1.9%. Fresh enough that a swap still catches the initial push.
3. **I Automated My Entire Content Workflow** - 6,164 impressions at 2.4%.
4. Leave `I Automated My Life with Claude Routines` (3.5%) alone for now. It is
   the best of the recent set; changing it risks the one thing working.

## Test one variable

Change the thumbnail only, keep the title, on Opus 5. It has enough impressions
that a real signal arrives in days. Changing both at once means learning
nothing, and this channel has been guessing for months.
