## Thumbnails, which is the real gap

Sabrina, every single one:

- **Two or three words, set enormous** - `WINNER?`, `7 AGENT SKILLS`,
  `AI FOR LAWYERS`, `HIDDEN DANGER`, `UNLIMITED VIDEOS`, `NO ONE KNOWS`,
  `CLAUDE WRITES 90%`, `$300,000`, `2.6M FOLLOWERS`. Text takes 40 to 60% of
  the frame.
- **Yellow on nearly every one.** It is her signature and it is the single
  brightest thing in a feed.
- **Her face, expressive, usually pointing at the text.** Not present - acting.
- **Saturated backgrounds** - orange glow, blue-purple, teal. Never dark grey.
- **One idea.** No logo grid, no badge, no second concept.

Tyler AI, recent:

- Small text, often a full phrase (`Is Opus 5 the Best?`) that is unreadable at
  210px wide, which is how a thumbnail actually renders on a phone.
- Dark backgrounds that disappear against YouTube's dark UI.
- Face present but neutral, doing nothing.
- Three or four competing elements: face, product logo, icon grid, badge.
- The 0.9% one is nearly blank white with a small sunburst and no face at all.

## What this means

The gap is not effort, topic, or title writing. On titles the two channels are
within a few percentage points of each other. **The gap is that her thumbnails
are readable at thumbnail size and his are not.**

That is a solvable, mechanical problem, and it is the whole ballgame at 3% CTR.

## Do this, in order

1. **New thumbnail on Opus 5.** 19,334 impressions already earned, 0.9%
   conversion. Three words, huge, yellow or orange, his face reacting. Change
   nothing else so the result is readable.
2. **Rebuild the recent set to one rule:** three words maximum, filling half the
   frame, on a bright background, face doing something.
3. **Remake the 4.7% video's format.** `How to Create Your First X in 2 Minutes`
   is the only thing that has worked in a year and it produced a quarter of the
   channel's new subscribers from a single 2m55s upload.
