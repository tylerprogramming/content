**Audience rewards SHORT + SPECIFIC + single-outcome ("X in N minutes"). Bails on LONG + broad "course/full."**

## Action playbook
**CTR (top of funnel):**
- Apply thumbnail rules: expressive face / real command, 3-4 word text, accent color, composited Claude mark.
- Use YouTube Test & Compare for clean A/B (not manual swaps).
- Titles: specific outcome + number, not description.

**Retention (the hold):**
- Proof-first cold open - hit the payoff in the first 15-30 sec, no slow intro.
- Aggressive chapter signposting on anything long.

**Format strategy (the big one):**
- Lead the channel with the proven "X in N minutes" single-outcome demos - they're the retention + Shorts engine.
- For the flagship courses (1-person business, Blotato): release the MODULES as standalone short videos in the proven format; keep the full course as evergreen/funnel, not the day-one growth bet. If publishing the monolith, mitigate with a hard cold open + chapters.
