## The one number that settles it

**Opus 5 got 19,334 impressions - more than any other video on the channel this
period - and converted 0.9% of them.**

That is not a reach problem. YouTube took a chance, put it in front of nearly
twenty thousand people, and 99.1% scrolled past. Reach was granted and wasted.
Every other recent long-form sits between 2.4% and 3.6% against a 4.3% channel
average.

## The honest caveat

Do not read the legacy CTR as proof that "EXPLAINED" titles are better. Those
videos are served in **search** to people who typed "radix sort" - high intent,
so a high click rate is expected and does not transfer to browse or suggested.

The fair comparison is recent-vs-recent, and it is still bad: four current
long-form videos, all below channel average, none above 3.6%.

## What the pattern actually is

Every underperforming title is a **statement about Tyler**: "I Automated My
Life", "I Automated My Entire Content Workflow", "I Don't Edit Videos Anymore".
Every high performer is a **promise to the viewer**: "Crash Course in 9
Minutes", "NO CODE AI Agent Builder Course for Beginners", "How To Use AI Agents
To Do ALL Your Work".

"Is it Worth It?" is the weakest of all at 0.9%, because it asks a question the
viewer has no reason to care about the answer to yet.

This matches what BRAIN already recorded on 2026-08-02, from a different angle:
*"A status claim ('I Don't Edit Videos Anymore') is not a payoff. Needs time
saved, $ result, or a specific number."* The CTR data is that rule, measured.

## What to do

1. **Re-title Opus 5 first.** It has proven distribution - 19,334 impressions -
   and the worst conversion on the channel. It is the single highest-leverage
   change available, and it needs no new filming.
2. **Stop opening titles with "I".** Four of four recent underperformers do.
   The catalogue's winners open with the outcome or the tool.
3. **Put the number in the title.** BRAIN already has this confirmed twice. Of
   the recent four, only one carries a number, and it is buried behind "Is it
   Worth It?".
4. **Shorts are carrying the channel and should not be the plan.** Six Shorts
   took 3.8K views against 9.9K for six long-form, and produced 8 subs to 69.
   They reach; they do not convert. Keep them as distribution, fix long-form.
