## What the numbers say

**The channel is being carried by videos Tyler no longer makes.** Search is the
single biggest source at 34.5%, and nine of the top twelve search terms are
legacy: data structures (radix sort, splay tree) and the CrewAI/AutoGen/Atomic
Agents era. Only two terms (`claude routines`, `opus 5`) belong to current
content. That traffic is real and it is free, but it is not the growth engine
and it never converts to subs on new work.

**Suggested video is 5.9%.** That is the number that decides whether a channel
compounds. Under 10% means YouTube is not confident enough to put the videos in
front of people who did not search for them, and it points at packaging -
titles and thumbnails - rather than content.

**Retention has one clear winner and it is short.** The 08-08 Opus 5 comparison
holds **59.3%** at 487 views. Nothing else in six weeks is close; the median
recent upload sits near 19%. The 08-09 workflow video pulled 270 views at 6.7%,
which means people arrived and left almost immediately.

**Subscribers are not coming from new uploads.** Ten uploads since 07-01
returned 12 subs. In the same window, `How To Use AI Agents To Do ALL Your Work`
gained 8 on its own, `CrewAI NO CODE Builder` 7, `Atomic Agents Crash Course` 5.
All legacy. Net +60 for the period is mostly the back catalogue working.

**External URL at 20.7% is the social distribution paying off.** That is the one
current-era lever clearly working, and it is worth protecting.

## What to do

1. **Fix packaging before making more.** 5.9% suggested is the ceiling on
   everything else. Re-title and re-thumbnail the two recent videos with real
   view counts but poor conversion - 08-07 (958 views, 0 subs) and 08-09 (270
   views, 6.7% retention). Both got traffic and failed to hold or convert it.
2. **Study the 59.3%.** The 08-08 comparison video retained three times the
   channel median. Same-screenshot, same-prompt, side-by-side. Work out whether
   it was the format, the length, or the topic, and repeat the one that holds.
3. **The legacy search terms are a demand signal, not just a tail.** `atomic
   agent`, `crewai`, `autogen`, `smolagents` still pull 350+ views a month
   between them. An agent-framework video in the current style would meet demand
   that already exists rather than creating it from scratch.
