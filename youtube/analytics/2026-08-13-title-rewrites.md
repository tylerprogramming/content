## Start here: the Opus 5 title was right, then changed

`041-claude-opus-5/titles.md`, locked 2026-07-28, scorecard and all:

> **Claude Opus 5 vs Fable 5: I Built the Same App Twice**

What is live now:

> **Claude Opus 5 Cut My API Bill in Half - Here's the Catch**
> 19,334 impressions, **0.9% CTR**

The package planned an A/B: *"swap to 'Half the Price' at 48-72h if CTR is
soft."* The swap happened. Nobody swapped back.

Then on 2026-08-11 BRAIN recorded, from TikTok, on this exact topic, same week:
**versus framing 1,262 views, price framing 262. 4.8x.**

So the head-to-head frame was chosen deliberately, replaced by a price frame,
and the price frame was later measured as 4.8x worse on the same topic. The
0.9% is that decision showing up on YouTube.

**Action: revert to the locked title.** It has never actually been tested here.

## What Sabrina does that these titles do not

Her three outliers: `10 Secret Codes for Claude AI & ChatGPT` (6.2x),
`I Built $1M Marketing Team with 1 AI Agent (7 Skills)` (3.6x),
`Every Prompt to Make Unlimited Free AI Videos` (2.8x).

Four transferable moves:

1. **A parenthetical second number.** `(7 Skills)`, `(3 Real Use Cases)`. The
   headline promise plus a concrete count of what is inside.
2. **Quantified completeness.** `Every Prompt`, `10 Secret Codes`. Implies you
   are not getting a sample.
3. **Two named tools in one title.** `Claude AI & ChatGPT` doubles the search
   surface for one video.
4. **Free or dollar framing in the promise**, not the topic.

Her failures are the same shape as this channel's: `Loop Engineering for AI
Agents` (0.4x, jargon) and `Is AI Making Us Dumber?` (0.4x, abstract question).

## The three not yet published, which matter more

Fixing a live title recovers a fraction. Getting these right before they ship is
where the leverage actually is.

### Arcade video 1 (045) - currently "Give Claude Code Access to Your Real Apps in 7 Minutes (Arcade MCP)"

This is already close to the only format that has worked on this channel in a
year. `How to Create Your First Claude Routine in 2 Minutes` hit **4.7% and 22
subscribers** on 22,511 impressions. Same shape: how-to, first, time bound.

- **Claude Code Can Finally Use My Gmail (7 Minutes, No Code)**
- Connect Claude Code to Gmail, Slack and Calendar in 7 Minutes
- I Gave Claude Code My Real Apps in 7 Minutes (No OAuth)

Keep the 7 minutes. Add the named tools - that is Sabrina's search-surface move.

### Arcade video 2 (046) - currently "Build a Real AI Agent in 7 Minutes: Claude Code + Arcade"

Already carries a number, a time bound and two named tools. Strongest title on
the slate. Only addition worth testing is a parenthetical count.

- **Build a Real AI Agent in 7 Minutes (Claude Code + Arcade)**
- The 4-Step AI Agent That Reads My Inbox (7 Minutes)

### Blotato, filming tomorrow, sponsored

No package exists yet. Sponsored means the title has to earn the click on its
own merits, because the audience is less forgiving of a tool video that looks
like an ad.

- **I Post to 8 Platforms From One Command**
- One Video, 8 Platforms, Zero Scheduling (Here's the Stack)
- I Stopped Scheduling Posts Entirely (8 Platforms, 1 Command)

Avoid naming the sponsor in the title. Named tools help when the viewer is
already searching for them; for a tool with low search volume it just reads as
an ad and costs the click.

Disclosure belongs on camera and in the description regardless.

## The rule this whole audit produces

**Every title needs a number and a payoff the viewer gets, and the channel's
one 4.7% video had both plus a time bound.** Nothing published in the last year
has all three.
