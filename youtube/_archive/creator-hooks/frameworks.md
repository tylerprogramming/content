## Framework 1: "What Actually Triggers (Bad Thing)"
**Hook score:** +466 | **Example views:** 222K
**Example:** "What Actually Triggers an IRS Audit"
**Thumbnail:** Face close-up + "THIS TRIGGERS [X]" in bold white bottom text

**Why it works:**
- Negativity — fear of the bad thing
- Curiosity — "Actually" implies insider info, opens a loop
- "What Actually" promises the real answer vs. what you think you know

**Templates:**
- What Actually Triggers [Bad Thing]
- What Actually Causes [Problem You Have]
- What Actually Makes [Thing] Fail

## Framework 2: "Why 90% of [Entities] Are [Bad] (The '[Label]' Lie)"
**Hook score:** +756 | **Example views:** 295K
**Example:** "Why 90% of New Homes Are Garbage (The 'Builder Grade' Lie)"
**Thumbnail:** Aerial/object shot + "DON'T BUY [X]" red badge + arrow

**Why it works:**
- Negativity + drama + conspiracy
- Counterintuitive (new = good, not garbage)
- Parenthetical names the "villain" — makes it specific

**Templates:**
- Why 90% of [Common Thing] Are [Bad] (The "[Fancy Label]" Lie)
- Why Most [Things] Are a Scam (The "[Industry Term]" Trap)

## Framework 3: "I Tried 500+ [Things], These N Will [Achieve Goal]"
**Hook score:** +992 | **Example views:** 936K
**Example:** "I Tried 500+ AI Tools, These 9 Will Make You Rich"
**Thumbnail:** Face centered + logos/items floating + "Just Copy Me" in yellow box

**Why it works:**
- Experience signal — credibility through effort
- List — tangible, you know what's in the video
- Desire — "Will Make You Rich" is aspirational
- Curiosity — which ones?

**Templates:**
- I Tried [N]+ [Things], These [N] Will [Goal]
- I Tested [N]+ [Things], Here's What Actually Works
- I Read [N]+ [Things], These Are The Only [N] You Need

## Framework 4: "[N] [Things] That Will Make You ADDICTED to [Activity]"
**Hook score:** +1930 | **Example views:** 433K
**Example:** "10 books that will make you ADDICTED to reading!"
**Thumbnail:** No text — person holding the thing, warm lighting, natural setting

**Why it works:**
- Desire — the audience wants to love this activity
- "ADDICTED" is an extreme word — makes outcome feel irresistible
- List format — tangible

**Templates:**
- [N] [Things] That Will Make You ADDICTED to [Activity]
- The [Thing] That Made Me ADDICTED to [Topic]
- [N] Features That Make Claude Code ADDICTIVE

## Framework 5: "(Authority): What You Aren't Being Told About (Topic)"
**Hook score:** +335 | **Example views:** 546K
**Example:** "Ex-CIA: What You Aren't Being Told About Iran | Mike Baker"
**Thumbnail:** Two faces + bold white "WHAT NO ONE NOTICED ABOUT [X]" overlaid

**Why it works:**
- Authority — credibility from the source
- Curiosity + FOMO — "aren't being told" implies hidden info
- Timeliness if topic is trending

**Templates:**
- (Authority/Identity): What You Aren't Being Told About (Topic)
- What No One Is Telling You About [Topic]
- What [Platform/Company] Doesn't Want You to Know About [Feature]

## Framework 6: "How Much [Unwanted Thing] Can You Remove Before It Ruins [Entity]?"
**Hook score:** +429 | **Example views:** 163K
**Example:** "How Much Sugar Can You Remove Before It Ruins a Cookie?"
**Thumbnail:** The subject labeled with % amounts — no face

**Why it works:**
- Curiosity — asks a question you want answered
- Desire — people want less of the bad thing
- Experiment framing — implies a definitive answer

**Templates:**
- How Much [Bad Thing] Can You Remove Before It Ruins [Good Thing]?
- How Little [Thing] Do You Need Before [Goal]?

## Framework 7: "N Things [Specific Audience] Get WRONG About [Action]"
**Hook score:** +670 | **Example views:** 230K
**Example:** "5 Things Golfers Get WRONG About Hitting Irons"
**Thumbnail:** Object/action close-up + "NEVER DO THIS" + red arrow

**Why it works:**
- List — tangible
- Negativity — fear of being wrong
- Specific audience callout — speaks directly to them

**Templates:**
- N Things [Audience] Get WRONG About [Action]
- N Things [Audience] Should NEVER Do When [Action]
- What [Audience] Get WRONG About [Topic]

## Framework 8: "The [Topic] Never Made Sense — Until Now"
**Hook score / views:** 1.6M views
**Example:** "The Black Death Never Made Sense - Until Now"
**Thumbnail:** Face (confused/curious) + scene + "How Did We Miss THIS!?" in red

**Why it works:**
- Peak curiosity — implies a revelation
- "Until Now" signals recency — something just changed
- Speaks to people who already know something about the topic
- The thumbnail's "HOW DID WE MISS THIS!?" amplifies the mystery

**Templates:**
- The [Topic] Never Made Sense — Until Now
- [Common Thing] Never Made Sense — Until Now
- [Feature/Tool] Never Worked — Until Now

## Framework 9 (Subscribr): "So [Adjective] They Beg To [Action]"
**Source:** Subscribr newsletter (bryan@subscribr.ai) — Mar 3, 2026
**Hook score:** N/A (sales/brand content focus)
**Example:** "How to Build a Personal Brand So Magnetic They Beg to Buy"

**Why it works:**
- Flips sales dynamic — them chasing you, not you chasing them
- "Beg" is extreme — removes anxiety, creates aspiration
- Before/after contrast built in

**Templates:**
- How to Build [Thing] So [Adjective] [Audience] Beg to [Action]
- Make Your [Thing] So [Adjective] People [Desired Outcome]
