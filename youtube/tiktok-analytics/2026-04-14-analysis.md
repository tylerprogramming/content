## What Worked (Hook Patterns)

### Top formula: "[specific small thing] = [specific big result]"
- "12 characters = full competitor research report" (996 views)
- "carousels without canva... $1.50 total" (1,086 views)
- "thumbnails without photoshop. 9 cents each. 30 seconds" (809 views)
- "7 platforms. 30 seconds. from my terminal" (770 views)

### What flopped: vague/motivational framing
- "Content used to ruin my entire day" (286 views)
- "I automated my entire content system with one tool" (401 views)
- "stop watching tutorials" (446 views)

## Format Insights

- **Screen recording demos** get highest avg watch time (13-21s)
- **Short text/list videos** get highest full watch % (27.7% for "5 skills")
- **Best follower conversion:** "built in 5 min" at 1.7% (also highest search traffic at 6.5%)
- **Best comment driver:** "12 characters" with 10 comments (people asking "how?")

## Traffic Source Breakdown
- For You page dominates at 86-97% across all videos
- Search ranges 1.5-9.4% - highest on "12 characters" (9.4%) and "built in 5 min" (6.5%)
- Personal profile visits are minimal (0.4-2.5%)

## Strategic Takeaways

1. **Hook with specific numbers** - dollar amounts, time savings, character counts
2. **"Without [expensive tool]" framing** crushes - positions Claude Code as the replacement
3. **Screen recording demos** hold attention longest - show the actual tool
4. **Short list videos** for completion rate - TikTok rewards full watches
5. **Front-load "claude code"** in captions for search discoverability
6. **Make results seem unbelievable** so people comment asking "how?"
7. **Avoid vague motivational hooks** - they consistently underperform
