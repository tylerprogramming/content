# Analysis - I Built an AI Agent That Answers WhatsApp (ElevenAgents)

**Runtime target:** 12-16 minutes
**Format:** Horizontal, talking head + screen recording (ElevenLabs dashboard) + a phone showing the live WhatsApp thread
**Sponsor:** ElevenLabs (paid ambassador) - #ad / #ElevenAgentsPartner - $200 per 1000 views
**Brand fit:** Perfect. This is the "AI agents and automation" lane. Tyler built agents at Fortune 500 companies; this is the accessible, no-code version a solo builder or a small business owner can ship in an afternoon. It also opens a real client-services angle (build this for a local business).

---

## The one-sentence promise
You build one ElevenAgent - a system prompt, a voice, a knowledge base - connect it to a WhatsApp Business number, and now a real AI answers WhatsApp messages and voice notes for a business, 24/7, in one thread the customer already uses every day.

## Why this video works
1. Result-first cold open that feels real. We open on a phone: a customer texts a business on WhatsApp, and the AI replies in seconds, including answering a voice note. Everyone has WhatsApp-messaged a business and been ignored. Seeing an AI answer instantly is a visceral "I want that" moment.
2. It is a tangible, buildable thing. Not a vague "agents will change everything." By the end the viewer has copied a system prompt and a knowledge base and knows the exact WhatsApp connection steps.
3. The builder / opportunity angle. Tyler's audience is not just hobbyists. "You can build this for yourself, or build it for a local business that's drowning in DMs" hits the solopreneur and the agency-curious viewer. No income claims, just the obvious use case.
4. Honesty equals trust equals the brand. The setup has real friction (WhatsApp Business account, Meta approval, per-message costs outside the 24h window). Naming those upfront is exactly what makes a paid video credible, and it is the reason people trust this channel.

## Target viewer
- Solo builders / automation people who want a real agent project they can finish.
- Small-business owners (salons, clinics, shops, agencies) who lose leads in unanswered DMs.
- Freelancers / agency builders looking for a productizable service to sell to local businesses.

## The demo spine (what we actually show)
1. Showcase - the finished thing: message a business on WhatsApp, it answers text plus a voice note, live.
2. System - build it from scratch on screen:
   - Create the agent in ElevenAgents.
   - Write the system prompt (the receptionist's personality + rules).
   - Pick a voice (for voice-note / call replies).
   - Add a knowledge base (a one-page FAQ: hours, services, prices, address) and enable RAG.
   - (Mention tools as the next-level step, e.g. a real booking action, but keep the filmed build to prompt + voice + knowledge for reliability.)
3. Connect to WhatsApp - import the WhatsApp Business account, authorize through Meta, assign the agent, enable calls.
4. Payoff - go back to the phone, message it fresh, it answers. Send a voice note, it answers.
5. Opportunity - this is what businesses pay for: a receptionist that never sleeps, support triage, booking questions. Build it for yourself or for a client.
6. Honest limits - the trust beat (accounts, approval, costs, zero-retention).

## The example business (keep it concrete and relatable)
A fictional local service business - "Maple Street Barbershop" (or a salon / dental office / bakery - pick one and stay consistent). Knowledge base = hours, services, prices, address, booking policy. The agent answers "are you open Sunday," "how much for a fade," "where are you," and a voice note asking the same. This is deliberately a business anyone can picture paying for the automation.

## "System on one screen" visual anchor

Same agent also works on the website and phone line - one brain, every channel. Return to this graphic at the intro, after the build, and at the recap.

## The tangible takeaway (lead magnet)
follow-along.md -> a public Google Doc + pinned comment. Contains the exact system prompt, the full FAQ knowledge-base text, the recommended voice/LLM settings, and the WhatsApp connection checklist. Said out loud in the cold open, linked in the description and pinned comment. This doubles as the newsletter/Skool lead magnet (free.tylerai.dev/youtube).

## Compliance guardrails baked into the script
- "ElevenAgents" spoken in the first 5 seconds.
- Verbal paid-sponsorship disclosure in the first ~20 seconds.
- PartnerStack CTA at intro, mid, and end.
- Live product use, not description.
- Honest-limits beat is mandatory and non-negotiable.
- No competitors, no puffery, no money claims, no em dashes.

## Risks / things to get right
- Meta approval lag. Get the WhatsApp Business account authorized days before filming so it is live on camera. (Flagged verify-live in BRIEF.md.)
- Naming. It is ElevenAgents, not ElevenCreative / Flows. Different product, different campaign, different hashtag.
- Do not overclaim the setup. There is a real authorization flow; show it or honestly summarize it, do not imply one click.
- Costs. Always pair a capability with its cost note (ElevenLabs usage + Meta outbound charges).
