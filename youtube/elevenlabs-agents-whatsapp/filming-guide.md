# Filming Guide - ElevenAgents + WhatsApp

Horizontal, talking head + screen recording + a phone on camera for the live WhatsApp thread. This is the paid ElevenLabs video, so the brief production rules are mandatory.

---

## Brief production rules (mandatory - payment depends on it)
- Film HORIZONTAL.
- Smart-casual wardrobe (no pajamas, no distracting logos).
- Good, soft, front-ish light. No harsh backlight, no blown-out window behind you.
- Clean, relevant background (a desk / workspace reads well for an agents build).
- Great audio, but NO microphone visible in the shot. Use a lav hidden under a shirt, a boom just out of frame, or clean the frame around a desk mic. Do an audio check BEFORE you film the whole thing.
- Say "ElevenAgents" in the first 5 seconds. Say the paid sponsorship out loud within the first ~25 seconds.
- No em dashes on any on-screen text.

---

## DO THIS DAYS BEFORE FILMING (the account is the long pole)
1. Confirm the WhatsApp integration is enabled on your ElevenLabs plan tier. (Verify live.)
2. Set up / have a WhatsApp Business account managed in Meta's WhatsApp Manager (business.facebook.com), and complete Meta's authorization so it's live on film day. This approval can take time - do not leave it to the morning of.
3. Add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager if you want to demo/enable calls.
4. Build the agent once as a rehearsal and message it, so you know it answers cleanly on camera. Test the voice-note reply specifically (audio-vs-text reply behavior) so the payoff lands.
5. Decide the example business and keep it consistent everywhere: name, hours, services, prices, address. (Package uses "Maple Street Barbershop.")
6. Confirm the exact partner hashtag (#ElevenAgentsPartner) and the PartnerStack code in the dashboard.

## Shot list

### 1. Cold open (0:00-0:25)
- [PHONE] Screen-record the WhatsApp thread answering a text ("Are you open Sunday?" -> instant reply). This is the money shot; get a clean take where the typing dots and the reply are clearly visible.
- [CAM] Deliver the cold open lines. Energy up. "ElevenAgents" lands early. Say the sponsorship. Name the free doc.

### 2. What it is + who it's for (0:25-2:25)
- [CAM] The explanation and CTA #1.
- [B-ROLL] The ElevenLabs dashboard, Agents section, hovering the WhatsApp option.
- [TEXT] "ElevenAgents = the AI agent platform | WhatsApp = where it now answers customers"

### 3. The build (2:25-6:30)
- [B-ROLL] Screen-record the full build: create agent -> paste system prompt -> upload knowledge doc + enable it -> pick voice + preview -> glance at the model.
- Keep the recording clean: no other tabs, no personal info, hide any account emails/billing.
- [TEXT] "Knowledge base + RAG = it answers from your real info, not made-up info"

### 4. Connect WhatsApp (6:30-9:30)
- [B-ROLL] Import account -> Meta authorization flow -> assign the agent -> Profile tab -> Call settings tab.
- IMPORTANT: blur or avoid showing your real Meta business ID, phone number, and any tokens. Use a lower-third or crop if needed.
- [TEXT] "You need: a WhatsApp Business account + Meta authorization (do this first, it can take time)"

### 5. Payoff - message it live (9:30-11:15)
- [PHONE] On camera or clean screen-record: send a fresh text, get the answer. Then hold the mic and send a voice note, get the answer.
- This must feel real and unedited. If you must retake, keep it honest (same question, same answer).
- [TEXT] the one-screen flow graphic: "[ Customer on WhatsApp ] -> [ Your ElevenAgent: prompt + knowledge + voice ] -> [ Instant reply, text or voice ]"

### 6. Opportunity + limits + CTA (11:15-15:15)
- [CAM] The opportunity beat (no income numbers), the honest-limits beat, the recap, the final CTA.
- [B-ROLL] Short montage of the agent answering 2-3 different questions.
- [TEXT] "Free doc + partner link in the description | #ad #ElevenAgentsPartner"

- Outro chip: "Free doc + partner link in the description | #ad #ElevenAgentsPartner"

## Privacy / safety checklist for screen recording
- [ ] Hide account email, billing, and any API keys/tokens.
- [ ] Blur/crop the Meta business ID and the real phone number if you don't want them public.
- [ ] Close unrelated tabs and notifications (do not disturb on).
- [ ] Use the fictional business's fake address/prices, not anything personal.
