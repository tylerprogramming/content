## Product truth (verified from ElevenLabs docs + blog, July 2026)

Sources:
- Dubbing overview (ElevenCreative products): https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/products/dubbing
- Dubbing capability docs: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/dubbing
- Dubbing Studio product page: https://elevenlabs.io/dubbing-studio

**What it is:** ElevenLabs Dubbing translates audio and video across **90+ languages** while preserving the **emotion, timing, tone, and unique characteristics of each speaker.** It separates each speaker's dialogue from the soundtrack so you can recreate the original delivery in another language.

**Three tiers (name them accurately):**
1. **Automatic Dubbing (v2, Alpha)** - the fast, one-click path. Up to 2 GB and 180 minutes per file.
2. **Dubbing Studio (V1 model)** - manual/granular control: transcript editing, speaker reassignment, per-clip regeneration. Up to 1 GB and 45 minutes. NOTE: docs say Dubbing Studio is currently "in maintenance mode and receives critical bug fixes only" - VERIFY LIVE before demoing it heavily; lead with Automatic Dubbing v2.
3. **Human-verified dubs via ElevenLabs Productions** - human-in-the-loop service tier (mention only, do not demo).

**How it works (workflow to demo):**
1. Upload a video/audio file OR paste a URL (YouTube/TikTok). **Tyler must use his OWN content.**
2. Pick the target language(s).
3. (Advanced) Adjust **Cloning strength** (default 7). Higher = closer to the original voice but can sound less natural in phonetically different languages; lower = more natural delivery, less vocal resemblance.
4. Review the cost confirmation, then Generate. It transcribes, detects/separates speakers, translates, and generates dubbed audio in the cloned voice with timing aligned to the original.
5. Download the finished dub from your project list.

**Voice preservation:** builds a voice model of the original speaker (pitch, pacing, emotional inflection, style) and applies it across every target language. Detects multiple speakers, even with overlapping speech.

**Credits/cost:** Automatic Dubbing v2 is priced per minute; failed jobs are not charged; Dubbing Studio credits are auto-refunded on cancelled/failed jobs. Say plainly that dubbing costs credits - it is not free.

**API:** the Dubbing v2 API is NOT yet live ("coming soon"). Do not demo or promise it.
