# Title Options

## 1. Google AntiGravity in 12 Minutes — Build Your First App (Recommended)
Specific time + clear outcome. "12 Minutes" sets a fast expectation. "Build Your First App" is the promise. Works for search and browse.

## 2. AntiGravity for Beginners: Zero to Working App
Classic beginner framing. "Zero to Working App" implies the full journey. Clear and searchable.

## 3. I Built an App Without Writing Code (Google AntiGravity)
Outcome-first, curiosity-driven. The parenthetical names the tool for search. Broad appeal beyond existing AntiGravity users.

## 4. The Free AI Tool That Builds Apps For You
Doesn't name AntiGravity in the title — casts a wider net. "Free" and "builds apps for you" are strong hooks. Risk: less searchable for people already looking for AntiGravity.

## 5. Google AntiGravity: The Only Tutorial You Need to Get Started
Authority positioning. "Only tutorial you need" is a bold claim that works if the content delivers. Good for search: "AntiGravity tutorial."
