# Test Prompts: Nano Banana Pro vs GPT Image 2 (run all on BOTH, off-camera first)

> Same prompt to both models, save the pairs side by side. Mix of creator-real use cases + proven "gotcha" tests that expose the difference. Sources: Futurepedia 145K test set, AlloyPress/OpenArt 2026 comparison tests. Web confirms the split: Nano Banana = realism/faces/aesthetics, GPT Image 2 = text rendering.

## PRO TIP (over-deliver early, ~Setup): the "photorealism" keyword
Add the literal word **"photorealism"** to a prompt - it noticeably improves realism on both models. Show before/after. (From the Futurepedia test - a quick, genuinely useful tip.)

## Round 1 - YouTube thumbnail (your use case) + BUDGET WILDCARD
Run this on ALL THREE: Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, and **Seedream 4.5 (Kie.ai)** - the cheap one (~$0.025/img). Question: can the budget model even make a usable thumbnail?
> "A bold YouTube thumbnail, 16:9: a shocked man on the right pointing to the left at two glowing AI-generated images, huge bold text reading 'NOT DETHRONED' in white and orange, dark high-contrast studio background, dramatic rim lighting, photorealism."
*(Tests text + composition + face in one - your real need.)*

## Round 2 - Face / likeness consistency (use your own photo)
> Image 1: "Put this exact person in a clean modern podcast studio at a desk with a microphone, looking at the camera, soft key light, shallow depth of field, photorealism."
> Image 2 (consistency test): "Same person, now standing with arms crossed, smiling, same studio and lighting."
*(Nano's strength - judge whether it's still clearly YOU across both.)*

## Round 3 - Text on an image (GPT's turf - concede if it wins)
> "An Instagram quote graphic, cream background, bold modern sans-serif text reading exactly: 'Consistency beats intensity.' centered, a small minimalist line icon below, lots of negative space."
*(Exact-text rendering - the web + Futurepedia both say GPT leads here.)*

## Round 4 - Brand / style consistency across a set
> "A 3-image carousel in ONE consistent flat-illustration style, warm cream and terracotta palette. Slide 1: a laptop with a rocket launching from the screen. Slide 2: a bar chart trending up. Slide 3: a person celebrating with arms raised. Identical style and palette across all three."
*(Creators need a consistent set - Nano's aesthetic edge.)*

## Round 5 - Speed + COST in a real workflow (not a prompt - a race) + WILDCARD shines here
Generate the Round 1 thumbnail three ways and time + price each:
- Nano Banana Pro via your `/thumbnail` skill (Kie.ai, in Claude Code)
- GPT Image 2 manually in ChatGPT
- **Seedream 4.5 via Kie.ai** - the budget option (~$0.025 vs flagship pricing)
Call out the **cost per image** for each - creators care, and nobody else shows it. This is where the cheap Kie.ai model makes its case: "good enough" thumbnails for pennies, inside your Claude Code workflow.

## Round 6 - Hard "gotcha" tests (GPT's turf - concede)
> A) "A clean modern infographic comparing 3 AI image models - Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, and Midjourney - with a short feature list and a starting price under each, accurate readable text, organized layout."
> B) (bonus fine-text test) "A movie-poster style image titled 'NANO BANANA PRO vs GPT IMAGE 2' with small legible fine-print credits at the bottom reading 'Tested by Tyler AI - 2026'."
*(Dense + fine text - where GPT pulls ahead. Conceding these makes your creator verdict credible.)*

## Optional spice (classic stress test, if time)
