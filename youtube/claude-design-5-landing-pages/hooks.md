# Hooks — 5 Landing Pages in One Afternoon with Claude Design

Three options.

---

## Hook 1 ⭐ Recommended — Proof Montage

> In the last 4 hours I shipped 5 completely different landing pages to 5 different live URLs. A SaaS. An agency. A restaurant. A creator site. A local business.

*[SHOW: 5-panel grid of finished landing pages, each getting a checkmark as named]*

> No designer. No developer. No hand-off meetings. Just Claude Design, Claude Code, and about 8 bucks in API credits.

*[SHOW: 5 live URLs flashing on screen, each scrolling briefly]*

> I'll show you the full process, the 5 prompt templates I used, and the one workflow trick that made this even possible in an afternoon.

> Let's go.

**Technique:** Nate-style energy + visual montage proof. 5 different industries covered in first 10 seconds signals breadth.

---

## Hook 2 — Agency-replacement

> A typical agency ships 4 to 6 landing pages per month for their clients. I just shipped 5 in one afternoon by myself using Claude Design.

*[SHOW: Side-by-side — agency invoice ($12,000) next to your actual cost ($8)]*

> If you run an agency, freelance, or just need to test a bunch of concepts fast, watch this.

*[SHOW: Quick cut through all 5 finished pages]*

**Technique:** Agency-replacement frame. Narrows viewer but attracts higher-value viewer.

---

## Hook 3 — Challenge format

> I gave myself 4 hours to ship 5 different landing pages to 5 live URLs using only Claude Design and Claude Code.

*[SHOW: Timer starts at 4:00:00]*

> Here's what happened.

*[SHOW: Time-lapse montage of the 4-hour build compressed to 8 seconds]*

> And here are all 5 live.

*[SHOW: 5 URLs scrolling, one per second]*

**Technique:** Challenge + timer creates immediate tension. Higher drop-off risk but strong CTR.

---

## Selection guidance

- **Hook 1 is the pick.** The 5-industry spread in the first sentence is the strongest differentiator — most volume videos on YT stick to one niche (5 SaaS pages). Yours shows breadth.
- Keep it under 30 seconds.
