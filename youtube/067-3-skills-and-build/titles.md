## Why The Hybrid Works

Showing 3 skills first earns the right to drop the "it's just a markdown file" reveal. Without the showcase, the reveal lands flat. With it, the simplicity is the punchline. That's the screenshot moment.

This structure also gives the video two payoffs — the demos (impressive) and the build (empowering). Either pulls the click. Both keeps people watching.
