## Director notes
- Fastest cut wins. The first 3 seconds must show CONTENT VOLUME, not Tyler talking. Earn the face reveal.
- Hook 1 is the lead recommendation - clearest result-first structure and it explicitly promises the steal-able system (serves both audiences).

- Do NOT open with any money claim or hype number that isn't real. The volume (32/week) and the time (1-2 days) are the proof - they're enough.
