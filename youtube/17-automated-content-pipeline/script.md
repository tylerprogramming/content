## [1:45-3:30] THE SYSTEM REVEAL

[Visual: whiteboard-style or simple on-screen diagram: ONE kickoff at the top, arrows fanning out to all the outputs. Keep returning to this graphic.]

- "Okay so here's the whole thing on one screen. Everything starts with one research kickoff. From there it flows through a chain of what Claude Code calls skills."
- Quick, plain-English definition of a skill (serves the builders AND the non-technical): "A skill is just a workflow I taught Claude Code once. I wrote down the steps - how to research, how to write a script, how to format a post - and now I can trigger that entire workflow with one command. Build it once, run it forever."
- Walk the chain, fast, one line each. Put the command on screen as you say it:
  - "/yt-search - it researches what's actually working on YouTube for a topic right now."
  - "/transcribe - it pulls the transcript from the best reference videos so I'm learning from proven stuff, not guessing."
  - "/yt-package - this is the big one. It writes the full video package. Titles, hooks, the script, the description, even a filming guide."
  - "/yt-seo - it optimizes the title and description against what's ranking."
  - "/shorts - it turns that research into five short-form scripts plus two Instagram carousel outlines."
  - "/content - it writes the LinkedIn post, the X posts, the Instagram caption, the YouTube community post."
- Land the scope: "Put that all together and one to two content days turns into about 32 pieces of content for the week. That's the pipeline. Now let me actually run it so you can see it's real."

## [3:30-9:00] LIVE SKILL DEMOS (the payoff - keep it moving)

General direction: these are REAL, run them live, talk to Claude Code on camera. Speed-ramp or cut the "thinking" time so it never drags. Narrate what you're seeing. Do not fake output.

### Demo 1 - /yt-search (research kickoff) [3:30-5:15]
- "Let's start where I always start. I'm going to research what's working." Type it live: `/yt-search claude code automation`.
- While it runs, narrate: "It's going out to YouTube, pulling recent videos on this topic, splitting them into long-form and Shorts, ranking them by views. This is the part that used to take me an hour of scrolling."
- When it lands, scroll the report on screen. Point at 2-3 real things: "Look - it found the top videos, the view counts, the angles that are working. Now I know what to make instead of guessing."
- The honest beat: "This isn't magic, by the way. It's using the same YouTube data I could pull myself. It's just doing it in thirty seconds instead of an hour, and it never gets bored and quits halfway."

### Demo 2 - /yt-package (the full video package) [5:15-7:30]
- "Now watch this. I'm going to take that research and turn it into an entire video package." Run `/yt-package` on the research.
- Narrate the pieces as they generate: "It's doing web research first - it actually goes and reads around the topic. Then titles, scored. Hooks. The full script. The description. A filming guide so I know exactly what to shoot."
- Open a couple of the real files on screen: show the titles.md, scroll the script.md. "This is a real script I could film today. I'm not reading you a fake one - this is the actual output."
- Critical honesty beat (this is the trust moment): "Here's what I want to be clear about. I do not just film whatever it hands me. I read it, I rewrite the parts that don't sound like me, I cut what's weak. Claude Code gets me to a strong first draft in minutes instead of hours. The judgment is still mine. That's the deal, and that's why it works."

### Demo 3 - /shorts or /content (the multiplier) [7:30-9:00]
- Pick the one that films best. "The last piece is the multiplier - taking one idea and spreading it across every platform."
- Run `/shorts` (or `/content`). Narrate: "From that same research it's writing five short-form scripts - one for each day - plus two Instagram carousel outlines. Then /content handles the LinkedIn post, the tweets, the Instagram caption."
- Show a real short script and a real caption on screen.
- Land it: "That's the whole trick. I'm not writing 32 different things from scratch. I'm making one strong thing and letting the pipeline reshape it for every platform, in my voice, using rules I set once."

## [10:30-11:45] HONEST LIMITATIONS (do NOT skip - this is the brand)

[Visual: Tyler straight to camera, no B-roll. This is the sincerity beat.]

- "Now let me tell you what this does NOT do, because I'm not going to sell you a fantasy."
- It does not film for me. "I still have to sit down and record. The camera doesn't run itself."
- It does not replace my judgment. "Bad input gives you bad output. If I feed it a lazy topic, I get lazy content. Garbage in, garbage out - that never changed."
- It is not set-it-and-forget-it. "I review everything. I approve everything before it goes out. Nothing auto-posts without me looking at it."
- It took work to build. "These skills didn't appear. I built them over time, tweaking them every week when something didn't sound right. The payoff is real, but it's leverage, not a lottery ticket."
- The honest close on the section: "So what did I actually buy myself? Time. The boring, repetitive 80% is handled. I get to spend my energy on the 20% that actually matters - the ideas and the judgment. That's the whole game."
