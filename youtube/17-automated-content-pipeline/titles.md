## FINAL PICK
**I Automated My Entire Content Pipeline With Claude Code**

Why the anchor wins as the primary: his #1 cohort video used almost this exact title and it is the one datapoint we trust most. Don't out-clever a proven pattern on the video that is deliberately running it back. It nails search ("automated," "content pipeline," "Claude Code"), it's honest, and it holds both audiences. Use the sharper numbers-based lines (#4, #3-list) as the thumbnail text and the A/B challenger.

## Thumbnail concepts
Shared visual system (matches channel): black background, Tyler's face (real reaction, slight lean-in / mild disbelief), huge bold text, single orange accent, one real-stat proof element. NO dollar amounts anywhere.

**Concept 1 - The number slam**
- Left: Tyler's face, looking at the viewer, eyebrows up.
- Right, stacked huge: "32 PIECES" (white) / "1 AFTERNOON" (orange).
- Small corner tag: "Claude Code" lockup or the terminal glyph.
- Reads in under a second. The 32-vs-1 contrast is the whole click.

**Concept 2 - The one-to-many spine**
- Left third: Tyler pointing at a single glowing terminal prompt ("/yt-search").
- Right two-thirds: a fanned-out grid of content thumbnails/platform icons (YouTube, TikTok, IG, LinkedIn, X) cascading out of it, black bg, orange connecting lines.
- Overlay text, top: "1 KICKOFF ->" / bottom: "A WHOLE WEEK" (orange on "WEEK").
- Visually tells the "one input, many outputs" story before a word is read. Strong for this specific video.

Recommendation: test Concept 1 first (faces + big numbers historically win for this channel). Hold Concept 2 as the retention-safe alternate.
