# Grilled Cheese Sandwich

**Servings:** 2
**Prep Time:** 2 min
**Cook Time:** 6 min

## Ingredients

- 4 slices bread
- 4 slices cheddar cheese
- 2 tbsp butter, softened
- 1 tsp dijon mustard (optional)

## Instructions

1. Butter one side of each bread slice.
2. Spread mustard on the non-buttered side if using.
3. Place 2 cheese slices between each pair of bread, butter side out.
4. Cook in a skillet over medium heat, 3 minutes per side, until golden and cheese is melted.
5. Slice diagonally and serve.
