## Title Testing Plan

1. Publish with **Title 1** ("Clearly Explained") — broad appeal, Eliot's proven formula
2. If CTR under 6% after 24 hours, swap to **Title 2** (reaction hook)
3. If CTR still weak after 48 hours, swap to **Title 3** (dollar hook) — narrower but higher-intent audience
4. Keep the thumbnail consistent for all 3 — only change title text

## Hooks In Titles To Avoid

- "Killed n8n" / "Destroys Zapier" — saturated, Nick already took this angle
- "Mind-blowing" / "You won't believe" — algorithmic penalty on clickbait
- Vague "The Future Of AI" framing — no specificity
