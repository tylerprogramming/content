# Tweets: Claude Code for Content Creators

---

## Standalone Tweets

**Tweet 1 (Hot Take)**
Everyone saying Claude Code is only for developers is leaving a massive content creation use case on the table. The terminal is the most powerful content scheduling tool most creators have never tried.

---

**Tweet 2 (Practical Tip)**
Before you plan your next video, run a YouTube search and pull the top 10 performers in your niche from the last 30 days. The gap between what's getting views and what you feel like making is usually where the best ideas live.

---

**Tweet 3 (Hot Take)**
The content creators who will win the next 2 years aren't going to be the most talented. They're going to be the ones who figured out that research and distribution are systems, not tasks.

---

**Tweet 4 (Practical Tip)**
LinkedIn posts with hashtags and bold markdown perform worse than plain text. X posts with hashtags get buried. Instagram needs exactly the same 5 brand hashtags every time. Your AI tool needs to know the difference or it's making you look off-brand on every platform.

---

**Tweet 5 (Insight)**
32 posts per week from 2 filming days sounds impossible until you realize you're only responsible for the ideas and the face on camera. Everything else is a system problem.

---

**Tweet 6 (Insight)**
A CLAUDE.md file is just a text file with your rules in it. But it's the difference between an AI that forgets your setup every session and one that already knows your posting schedule, your brand voice, and where to save files before you type a single word.

---

**Tweet 7 (Practical Tip)**
If you're writing LinkedIn posts, X posts, Instagram captions, and YouTube descriptions separately for every video - you're doing the same job 4 times. One good repurpose command should handle all of it in one pass.

---

## 3-Tweet Thread

**Tweet 1 of 3:**
The mistake most creators make with AI tools: they use them like a fancier search engine. Ask a question, get an answer, move on. No memory. No context. No system.

**Tweet 2 of 3:**
The better approach: build a config file that tells your AI your rules once. Platforms, schedule, hashtag rules, brand voice, file locations. Now every session starts with Claude already knowing how you work. You just say what you want.

**Tweet 3 of 3:**
Result: research, writing, repurposing, and scheduling in about 20 minutes. The other 4 hours and 40 minutes of a typical "content day" are either filming or getting your life back.
