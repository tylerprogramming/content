# Banana Pancakes

**Servings:** 4 (about 12 pancakes)
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 10 min

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1.5 cups flour
- 1 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- Butter for the pan
- Maple syrup

## Instructions

1. Mix mashed bananas, egg, milk, and sugar in a bowl.
2. Add flour, baking powder, and cinnamon. Stir until just combined.
3. Heat a buttered skillet over medium heat.
4. Pour 1/4 cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form, about 2 minutes.
5. Flip and cook another 1-2 minutes until golden.
6. Serve with maple syrup.
