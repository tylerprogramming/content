# Scrambled Eggs

**Servings:** 2
**Prep Time:** 2 min
**Cook Time:** 5 min

## Ingredients

- 4 large eggs
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp milk
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Chives, chopped (optional)

## Instructions

1. Crack eggs into a bowl. Add milk, salt, and pepper. Whisk until combined.
2. Melt butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat.
3. Pour in eggs. Gently push eggs from edges to center with a spatula.
4. Cook 3-4 minutes, folding slowly, until just set but still soft.
5. Remove from heat and top with chives.
