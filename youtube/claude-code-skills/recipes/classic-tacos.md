# Classic Beef Tacos

**Servings:** 6
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 10 min

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 12 taco shells
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 cup diced tomatoes
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- Salsa

## Instructions

1. Brown ground beef in a skillet over medium-high heat. Drain fat.
2. Add taco seasoning and 1/2 cup water. Simmer 5 minutes.
3. Warm taco shells according to package directions.
4. Fill shells with beef and top with lettuce, cheese, tomatoes, sour cream, and salsa.
