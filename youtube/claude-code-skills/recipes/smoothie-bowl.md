# Smoothie Bowl

**Servings:** 1
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 0 min

## Ingredients

- 1 frozen banana
- 1/2 cup frozen mixed berries
- 1/4 cup milk
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- Toppings: granola, sliced banana, coconut flakes, chia seeds, honey drizzle

## Instructions

1. Blend frozen banana, berries, milk, and peanut butter until thick and smooth. Keep it thicker than a normal smoothie.
2. Pour into a bowl.
3. Arrange toppings on top in rows.
4. Eat with a spoon.
