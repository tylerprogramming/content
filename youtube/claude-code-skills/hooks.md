# Hook Options (First 30 Seconds)

## Hook 1: The Demo Montage (Pattern Interrupt)

> I resize images for every social media platform with one command. I transcribe YouTube videos in seconds. I journal every day without opening an app. And I built each one of these in about a minute. They're called skills. Let me show you how they work and how to build your own.

**Technique:** Pattern interrupt. Opens with rapid-fire capability demos instead of a thesis statement. The viewer sees outcomes before hearing any explanation. Fast cuts on screen sell the speed.

---

## Hook 2: The Before/After (Curiosity Gap)

> Every time I needed to resize an image, I'd open Photoshop, set the dimensions, export, repeat. Every time I needed a transcript, I'd find a website, paste a link, wait, copy the text. I was doing the same work over and over. Then I found a feature in Claude Code that fixed all of it. It took me about a minute to set up. And I haven't gone back.

**Technique:** Curiosity gap. Builds relatable pain first, then teases the solution without naming it. Viewer has to keep watching to find out what the feature is.

---

## Hook 3: The Bold Claim (Direct)

> Skills are the single most useful thing in Claude Code and almost nobody uses them. Today I'm going to show you three of mine, explain what they actually are under the hood, and then build one from scratch right in front of you. By the end you'll know how to build your own.

**Technique:** Bold claim + roadmap. Tells the viewer exactly what they'll get. No mystery, just a clear promise. Works well for beginner audiences who want to know what they're committing to.
