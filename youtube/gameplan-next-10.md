Three hard rules the numbers prove:

The two levers that are broken: **CTR** (stuck at 2-4%, needs 4-8% to trigger promotion) and **first-60s retention**. Thumbnails and cold opens are the whole game.

## The winning cold-open formula (reverse-engineered from the #1 video)

The actual opening lines of "I Automated My Entire YouTube Workflow":
> "This video that you're watching right now, I put together using four commands in my terminal... I didn't open up a browser. I didn't open up Google Docs. I did it all right here in my terminal using Claude. And I'm going to show you exactly how I did this."

The formula, in order, all inside the first 40 seconds:
1. **Proof-of-result in sentence one** - the thing they're watching IS the output.
2. **The negative contrast** - "I didn't open a browser, I didn't open Google Docs." Names what you DIDN'T do.
3. **The promise** - "I'm going to show you exactly how."
4. **The pain they feel** - name the manual grind so the payoff lands.
5. **The free resource** - "grab the whole thing free in the link below."

No slow intro, no "hey what's up guys," no channel throat-clearing. Payoff first.

## The copy-paste resource = the lead magnet

The AntiGravity and YouTube-workflow videos both had a Google Doc / GitHub people could copy from. That's what made them feel "clearly better." Going forward EVERY video gets a companion resource (public GitHub repo or Google Doc with the exact skills, commands, and prompts).

Wire it to the funnel: the resource link -> a landing page that captures email -> delivers the doc -> invites to the free Skool. One asset, three jobs: makes the video better, feeds the newsletter, feeds Skool. This is the free giveaway from the traffic conversation.

## Thumbnail upgrade system (non-negotiable, every video)

- **Real expressive face**, anchored to `~/assets/identity/tylerai.png` (genuine smile or pointing with intent). Flat/deadpan = the 3% CTR trap.
- **One real proof element**: the actual terminal with the real command, or the multi-platform result grid. Show the thing.
- **3-4 huge words, one in orange (#D97757).** Composite the real Claude spark mark (diffusion garbles it).
- **Title + thumbnail complement, never repeat.** Thumbnail = the proof/what; title = the payoff/stakes.
- **Use YouTube Test & Compare** (native A/B) for every launch instead of manual swaps.
- Build one reusable template so they're consistent and branded.
