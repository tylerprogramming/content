# Hooks - "I Automated My YouTube Comments With Claude Code"

Five cold-open variants. Each is 12-20 seconds, spoken word-for-word, designed to open a loop and earn the next 30 seconds. All open on real proof, not a talking head monologue. No em dashes. No fake claims.

## Hook 1 - The Wall (recommended)
[On screen: scroll a real wall of unreplied comments across recent uploads. Then cut to one getting a real reply posted, green check.]

"This is every comment on my channel I never replied to. For months I told myself I would get to them, and I never did. Watch this."

[Beat. Cut to terminal. Claude pulls the unreplied ones, drafts replies, Tyler approves, they post live.]

"I did not hire anyone. I did not fake a single reply. I built one system with Claude Code that finds the comments I missed and helps me actually answer them, and I am going to show you exactly how it works."

## Hook 2 - The Confession
[Tyler to camera, honest.]

"I have to be honest with you. I am terrible at replying to my comments. And if you run any kind of channel or page, you probably are too. It is not that we do not care, it is that tracking what you already answered across every video is genuinely miserable."

[Cut to the inbox file populating.]

"So I automated the tracking part, not the caring part. Claude finds what I missed, drafts a reply, I approve it, and it posts through the official YouTube API. Let me walk you through the whole thing."

## Hook 3 - The Outcome
[On screen: notification-style stack of "replied" checkmarks filling up fast.]

"I reply to every comment on my channel now, and I am not spending hours in the Studio tab to do it. No bots blasting Great video to everybody. Real answers, to real people, still written by me. The only thing I automated is the part nobody wants to do, and I will show you how to copy it."

## Hook 4 - The Contrast (differentiator-first)
[Split screen: left, a gray generic "Great video!!" bot reply with a red X. Right, a specific real reply with a green check.]

"There are two ways to automate your YouTube comments. This one, where a bot spams the same fake reply to everyone and slowly kills your channel. And this one, where a system tracks what you missed, drafts a real answer, and you approve it before anything posts. I built the second one with Claude Code, and today I am handing you the blueprint."

## Hook 5 - The Cost
[Tyler to camera.]

"Not replying to your comments is quietly costing you subscribers. The algorithm watches engagement, and your community notices when you go quiet. I knew that, and I still could not keep up. So I stopped trying to do it by hand and built a human-in-the-loop system instead. Claude drafts, I approve, it posts through the real API. Here is exactly how."

## Notes for the edit
- Whichever hook is used, the wall-of-unreplied-comments visual should appear in the first 3 seconds. It is the single strongest cold-open image.
- Say "I did not fake anything" or "real replies, still written by me" inside the first 20 seconds no matter which hook. That line is the trust anchor.
- Do not promise money or scale in the hook. The opportunity layer comes at the end, earned.
