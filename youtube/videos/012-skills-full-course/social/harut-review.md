# Harut conversion review - Claude Code Skills launch sequence

## Verdict
The sequence already follows Harut's framework: value-first content, honest tone, one free deliverable as the lead magnet, a final nudge on Friday. Copy is on-strategy. Biggest leverage is operational (Skool setup), not the words.

## Harut's principles applied here
1. ONE primary CTA per asset, make it clear. "If you never ask, you don't have a brand."
   - Emails: primary ask is Watch (button). Kept. Starter-pack line is the same destination, not a competing ask.
2. Sell the outcome, not the community. Name the deliverable.
   - Applied: launch email now names "the actual /standup skill we build plus 5 more you can copy" instead of a vague "starter pack."
3. Value-first, low friction. The posts teach; the click is a natural next step. Good.
4. Final nudge in the back half of the week. Friday email = "this weekend is the time." Good.

## Copy changes made
- Email 1 (Sun): starter-pack line now names the concrete deliverable (sell the outcome).

## Left as-is (already on-strategy)
- LinkedIn: value-first + question CTA + link in first comment. Clean, no salesy asks (matches both Harut and LinkedIn reach rules).
- X: one CTA reply with the link. Good.
- Emails 2-4: each has a single Watch CTA + a reply prompt for engagement.

## Bigger conversion unlock (operational - beyond copy, worth doing in Skool)
These are where Harut says the real lift is:
1. Membership questions on the free Skool: require email + 1 qualifying question (e.g. "how many automations do you run?") + 1 goal question. Filters and captures email.
2. Auto-DM welcome: turn on Skool auto-DM, point new members straight to the starter-pack classroom module. Speed-to-lead matters.
3. Pin the starter-pack module in the classroom as the clear first step.
4. Personalized Looms to high-fit joiners in the first few minutes (Harut: converts massively).
