## MONDAY (skills vs MCP vs plugins)

I mixed these up for way too long.

Skills, MCP, and plugins. If you use Claude Code, you have probably blurred them together too.

So let me give you the version I wish someone gave me.

A skill is know-how.

It teaches Claude how to do something, your way, and it repeats it for you over and over.

MCP is access.

It connects Claude to something it cannot reach on its own, like your Gmail or your database.

A plugin is the box.

It bundles skills and connections so you, or your whole team, can install all of it in one command.

That is the whole thing.

Know-how. Access. The box.

Once that clicked for me, everything else about Claude Code got easier.
