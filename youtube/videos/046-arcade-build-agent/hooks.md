# Hooks - 043 Arcade Build Agent

Three word-for-word cold opens. Pick one, shoot it fast, get right into it. No preamble.

---

## Hook 1 - Result-first (RECOMMENDED)
**Technique:** Show the finished thing working, then promise to build it from scratch.

[SHOW: the finished agent's output on screen. A clean morning brief: today's calendar, the three emails that need a reply, and drafts already written.]

"This is an AI agent I built. It just read my calendar and my inbox, told me what my day looks like, flagged the three emails that actually need a reply, and drafted all three for me.

Let me build this whole thing from scratch in about seven minutes. Starting now."

---

## Hook 2 - Pain-first
**Technique:** Name the exact wall people hit, then remove it.

"Everyone wants an AI agent that touches their real apps. Their email, their calendar. And almost everyone gives up at the same spot: the auth. Wiring up OAuth and tokens and scopes.

I've built these integrations at Fortune 500 companies. The auth is the hard part. So I'm going to skip it entirely and build a working morning brief agent, live, in about seven minutes."

---

## Hook 3 - Direct challenge / promise
**Technique:** Set a concrete bar on screen and race it.

[SHOW: a 7:00 timer, paused, in the corner of the frame.]

"A real AI agent needs two things: tools, and permission to use them. Most tutorials stop at a chatbot that can only talk.

I'm going to build one that actually does the job. Reads my Gmail and my Calendar, plans my day, drafts my replies. And I'm putting seven minutes on the clock. Let's go."
