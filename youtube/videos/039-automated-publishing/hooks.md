# Hooks - I Automated Publishing My Content With Claude Code

Cold-open rule for this video: proof of RESULT in sentence one - "I didn't publish this video, Claude did." Then the negative contrast (all the apps he didn't open), then name the pain (the posting grind), then the promise, then the free doc. Every variant is 10-20 seconds, fast, no throat-clearing, no "hey what's up guys." Payoff first.

## Hook 1 - The "I didn't publish this" reveal (RECOMMENDED)
[Visual: this video's own YouTube page, then a fast fan-out into a short, LinkedIn posts, Pinterest pins, an email queue - all scrolling by. Then cut to Tyler.]
"This video you're watching right now, I didn't publish it. Claude did. It went up on YouTube, got cut into a short, turned into three LinkedIn posts, four Pinterest pins, and a week of emails, and I didn't open a single app to do any of it. So most creators spend more time posting the content than making it. You finish the video, and then you're uploading it, writing the description, cutting the short, writing the caption for TikTok, then Instagram, then LinkedIn, and by the time you're done you don't even want to make the next one. That was me. So I built a system in Claude Code that takes one finished video and pushes it everywhere. I'm going to show you exactly how it works, and you can grab the whole thing free in the link below."

One finished video goes in, and all of that comes out, scheduled. So here's the thing nobody warns you about, right? Making the content was never the hard part. ## Director notes
- Hook 1 is the lead recommendation. It's the provided cold open refined into Tyler's voice, it opens on the strongest proof (this video published itself), names every platform concretely, hits the exact pain, and promises the free doc. Use it.
- The first 3 seconds must show the RESULT (this video fanning out across platforms), not Tyler talking. Earn the face reveal.
- Whatever variant is chosen, land on a "watch it work live" promise so the viewer stays for the demo.
- Kill the auto-poster objection early. If the open makes it sound like a bot blasting spam, viewers bounce. Hook 3 handles this best ("nothing goes out until I say go") - if the comments on 17 showed skepticism, lead with Hook 3 instead.
- Do NOT open with any money claim or hype number that isn't real. Seven platforms and "never open an app" are the proof - they're enough.
