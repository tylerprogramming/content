# Reels — 039 Automated Publishing

Cut from the 039 footage. Demo-forward: lead with the fan-out / the queue filling, keep talking to voiceover. Target 45-90s. Priority: 1, 2, 4.

---

## Reel 1 — "I didn't publish this video" (flagship)
**Hook:** "This video you're watching right now? I didn't publish it. Claude did."
- **Show:** this video's YouTube page, then a fast fan-out — a short, LinkedIn posts, Pinterest pins, an IG carousel, a TikTok slideshow, the queues filling.
- Say: one finished video, uploaded, cut, and reshaped for eight platforms, and I didn't open a single app.
- **Payoff:** "I built the machine once. Now the machine does the posting."

## Reel 2 — "Watch one video fill every platform" (the money shot)
**Hook:** "One finished video. Watch every platform fill up."
- **Show:** the payoff montage — YouTube scheduled (thumbnail set), then TikTok, IG Reel, LinkedIn, X, four Pinterest pins, Bluesky and Threads, each ticking to *scheduled*.
- Say: a full week of presence across eight platforms.
- **Payoff:** "And all I did was finish one video and hit approve a few times."

## Reel 3 — "The platform everyone sleeps on" (Pinterest)
**Hook:** "Everyone sleeps on this platform, and it sends me traffic for months, not hours."
- **Show:** the Pinterest step running — pulling pins from the video with keyword-first titles, assigned to the right boards.
- Say: it's basically a search engine, and I used to skip it because it felt like too much work.
- **Payoff:** "Four pins, ready. A whole channel of traffic I'd never touch by hand."

## Reel 4 — "It's not a spam cannon" (approval / trust)
**Hook:** "No, I'm not blasting the same caption everywhere like a bot."
- **Show:** the per-platform drafts side by side — TikTok's caption, Instagram's hashtags, LinkedIn with no hashtags, X's punchy line — then you approving each.
- Say: same clip, reshaped per platform, and nothing goes out until I read it.
- **Payoff:** "I'm the approve button. That's not a limitation, that's the feature."

## Reel 5 — "Automate the one platform you hate" (accessible CTA)
**Hook:** "Don't automate all eight platforms. Automate the one you always skip."
- **Show:** building one small skill from plain English (the platform you hate posting to most).
- Say: you don't build the whole machine on day one, you build one piece.
- **Payoff:** "If you can describe how you post to one platform, you can turn it into a skill. Start there."
