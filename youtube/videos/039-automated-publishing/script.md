## [10:45-11:45] HONEST LIMITATIONS (do NOT skip - this is the brand)

[Visual: Tyler straight to camera, no B-roll. The sincerity beat.]

- "Now let me tell you what this does NOT do, because I'm not going to sell you a fantasy."
- It doesn't make the content. "This system publishes. It does not create. The video still has to exist, the short still has to be cut. That's a whole other part of my setup, and honestly it's the video I made before this one. This is just the distribution half."
- It does not auto-post blindly. "I said it earlier and I mean it. Nothing goes out without me approving it. If you set something like this up to just fire on its own, one day it posts something dumb to your whole audience while you're asleep. I approve everything. That's not a limitation, that's the feature."
- It does not make the algorithm like you. "Scheduling a post everywhere doesn't mean any of it pops. It just means you actually showed up on every platform instead of running out of steam after YouTube. Consistency is the win here, not virality."
- Each platform still needs its own connected account and a little babysitting. "APIs change, a token expires, a platform updates something. Every few weeks I have to go fix a small thing. It's real, it's just not two hours a week anymore."
- The honest close: "So what did I actually buy myself? Time, and consistency. The boring, repetitive posting is handled. I get to spend my energy on making one thing genuinely good instead of clicking upload buttons for two hours. That's the whole game."
