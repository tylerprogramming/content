## [2:00-8:00] THE LIVE DEMO - two tools

General direction: REAL, run it live, talk to Claude Code on camera. Speed-ramp or cut every upload wait and thinking moment so it never drags. Narrate what you see. Do not fake output.

### Piece 1 - Blotato: setup + everything, everywhere [2:00-7:00]

- "Let's start with Blotato, because it is doing most of the work. I wired it into Claude Code, so I drive the whole thing from right here."
- The one-time setup, quick: "Before any of this, you connect your accounts to Blotato once. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, Bluesky, Threads. You click connect, you authorize each one, and you are done. You only ever do this once, then you forget about it." [SHOW: the Blotato accounts screen with all eight platforms connected.]
- Kick it off live: hand Claude Code the finished long-form and the two reels. "I am handing it the long-form video and the two reels. Watch what it does."
- Narrate the drafting, fast, grouped:
  - **YouTube:** "It uploads the long-form to YouTube. The real custom thumbnail, the full description with the links, and it sets the exact publish time, so it goes live when I want and I am not sitting here at 9am. It also cuts the two reels into Shorts."
  - **The reels, everywhere:** "Then the two reels go out as reels. TikTok, Instagram, Bluesky, Threads, each with its own caption. And these are not identical, that is the whole point. TikTok gets its caption, Instagram gets its hashtags, LinkedIn gets no hashtags because that is what works there, X gets a punchy standalone line."
  - **The text and the pins:** "It writes three tweets and a couple of replies for X. It writes three LinkedIn text posts, because on LinkedIn a plain text post that teaches something beats a video. And it pulls three Pinterest pins with keyword-first titles, because Pinterest is basically a search engine and it sends traffic for months, not hours."
  - **The visual formats:** "Then it builds two Instagram carousels and a TikTok slideshow out of the key points, because a lot of people scroll carousels and slideshows way more than they watch a clip. That is free reach I would be leaving on the table."
- The approval beat, say it clearly: "And here is the important part. Every single one of these, I see before it goes. Blotato shows me each draft. I read them, I fix anything that does not sound like me, and THEN I schedule. Nothing posts on its own. I am the approve button."
- [SHOW: the real Blotato queue filling up across all eight platforms, scheduled.]
- Say plainly: "Blotato is just the tool I happen to use. If you use something else, the idea is the same. One set of content, reshaped per platform, scheduled, and approved by a human."
- The accessible version: "And you do not even have to touch Claude Code for this. Blotato has its own dashboard. You can write a post and schedule it to all eight platforms right there in the browser, no code at all." [SHOW: drafting and scheduling a post directly inside the Blotato platform, picking the platforms, hitting schedule.] "I drive it from Claude Code because publishing is one piece of my bigger pipeline. But if all you want is the posting handled, Blotato on its own does it. Because nobody wants to do this by hand, eight times, every week."

### Piece 2 - Resend: the email [7:00-8:30]

- "The one thing that does not go through Blotato is email. And email is the piece most creators completely skip. Your list is the one audience the algorithm cannot take away from you. So every video becomes emails too."
- Run it live: Claude drafts the sequence and schedules through Resend. "It is writing a short sequence off these videos. A few emails spaced across the week that pull people back to watch, and point them to the free stuff. Then it schedules them in Resend."
- [SHOW: the real emails and the Resend schedule.]
- The approval beat again: "Same rule. I read every email before it sends. These go to real people on my list, so I am not letting anything out I have not looked at. But I am not writing them from a blank page anymore either."

## [9:15-10:15] HONEST LIMITATIONS (do NOT skip - this is the brand)

[Visual: Tyler straight to camera, no B-roll. The sincerity beat.]

- "Now let me tell you what this does NOT do, because I am not going to sell you a fantasy."
- It does not make the content. "This system publishes. It does not create. The videos still have to exist, the reels still have to be cut. That is a whole other part of my setup, and honestly it is the video I made before this one. This is just the distribution half."
- It does not auto-post blindly. "I said it earlier and I mean it. Nothing goes out without me approving it. If you set something like this up to just fire on its own, one day it posts something dumb to your whole audience while you are asleep. I approve everything. That is not a limitation, that is the feature."
- It does not make the algorithm like you. "Scheduling a post everywhere does not mean any of it pops. It just means you actually showed up on every platform instead of running out of steam after YouTube. Consistency is the win here, not virality."
- It needs a little babysitting. "APIs change, a token expires, a platform updates something. Every few weeks I go fix a small thing. It is real, it is just not hours a week anymore."
- The honest close: "So what did I actually buy myself? Time, and consistency. The boring, repetitive posting is handled. I get to spend my energy on making three things genuinely good instead of clicking upload buttons. That is the whole game."
