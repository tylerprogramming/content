# Filming Guide - I Automated Publishing My Content With Claude Code + Blotato

Target runtime: 13-15 min. Sponsored Blotato video, demo-heavy showcase. Blotato is the ONE tool on screen (creates, publishes to all 8 platforms, reads engagement back). No Resend / no email. The energy and the "watch it populate" payoff carry this video. Shoot it so the edit can stay fast.

## The one rule that makes or breaks it
Speed-ramp or cut EVERY wait. Uploads take real minutes, Claude "thinking" takes real seconds. On screen that is death. Any progress bar, spinner, or thinking indicator gets ramped to 1-2 seconds or cut with a clean jump. The viewer should feel like it's instant even though it isn't. Capture extra handle on both sides of every wait so the edit has room.

## What to shoot (in order)

### A. Talking-head INTRO (0:00-0:40)
- Deliver the word-for-word intro from `script.md`. Do 3-4 takes; pick the one where "I never opened a single app to do it" lands with a little grin, not a flat read.
- Straight down the barrel, full energy from word one. This is the thumb-stopper. Result-first, no warmup energy.

### B. Talking-head SYSTEM (0:40-1:40)
- The one-screen stack overview: video + 2 reels → Claude Code → Blotato (one tool, all 8 platforms). Confident, "look how simple this actually is."
- Say "nothing posts without my approval" here with intent - it's the objection-killer.

### C. Talking-head LIMITATIONS / SHARFITMAMA / CTA (9:30-13:45)
- The limitations beat (9:30) is the sincerity moment. Slow down, drop the energy, look right at the lens. This is the only slow part of the whole video - let it breathe.
- sharfitmama (10:45) and the CTA (12:30) go back up to energetic.

## Screen-record checklist (the live demo) - all Blotato, all REAL
Record each clean, full-screen, high resolution. Narrate live so audio and screen match. Everything on screen must be REAL - no mockups.

- [ ] **Two ways in (1:40-2:50):**
  - *Create it:* screen-record Blotato's create-a-video / Create Visual - build a short video or a slideshow on brand from the key points. Keep it quick.
  - *Create from source:* paste a YouTube URL into Claude Code and watch Blotato scrape the transcript + draft a thread / carousel / captions.
- [ ] **The main run (2:50-6:30):** hand Claude Code (wired to Blotato) the finished long-form + 2 reels. Capture Blotato drafting EVERYTHING in one run:
  - YouTube long-form uploaded with real custom thumbnail + full description + publish-at time; the 2 reels cut to Shorts.
  - The 2 reels out as reels to TikTok / Instagram / Bluesky / Threads, each with its OWN caption (show at least two side by side - LinkedIn no hashtags vs Instagram with hashtags).
  - X tweets + replies, LinkedIn text posts, Pinterest pins (keyword titles), Instagram carousels + a TikTok slideshow.
  - The APPROVAL step: you reading a draft, fixing a line, THEN scheduling. Hold on "I am the approve button."
  - The real Blotato queue filling across all 8 platforms, scheduled. Hold on that end frame.
- [ ] **The no-code version (inside the main run):** draft + schedule a post directly in the Blotato dashboard in the browser, pick the platforms, hit schedule. "You don't even need Claude Code for this."
- [ ] **The NEW part - reads everything back (6:30-8:30):** the second climax. Screen-record in Claude Code:
  - Analytics: "what was my top post the last two weeks?" - real numbers come back.
  - Comments: "get me all the comments on it" - the real list.
  - The payoff line: "read these comments, what is my audience asking for?" - it hands back 3 next-content ideas.
  - Conversations/DMs + drafting replies (you still approve each). 
  - REQUIRES a paid plan + real posts with real comments/analytics to pull. Pre-test this the day of.

## The payoff montage (8:30-9:30) - shoot this deliberately
The video's climax and most clippable shot. Get enough coverage to cut it fast and satisfying:
- [ ] A wide "everything at once" view - the finished video on one side, the Blotato platform queues on the other.
- [ ] Individual "tick to Scheduled" moments for each of the 8 platforms so the edit can rapid-fire them.
- [ ] Optionally a screen-record of THIS video's own scheduled entries, so "you're looking at the proof" is literally true.

## Energy and pacing notes
- Overall energy: up and moving, like showing a friend something you're a little proud of. Not hype, not salesy - "look, this actually works."
- The demo (1:40-8:30) is the retention zone. Keep narrating, never sit in silence on a spinner.
- One deliberate gear change: limitations drops to sincere and slow. Everything before and after is brisk.
- Re-say the approval line ("I approve everything, nothing auto-posts") at least twice - intro and limitations. Non-negotiable for trust.

## Assets / pre-flight before filming
- A genuinely finished long-form video + the 2 reels cut from it, ready to run through Blotato live (can be an older real one).
- Its title, description, and custom thumbnail ready.
- A source URL (a YouTube video) for the "create from source" beat.
- **Blotato: all 8 platforms connected + tokens valid** - RECONNECT LINKEDIN (Blotato flagged it expiring 8/11), and verify Bluesky + Pinterest are healthy (recent publish failures). Test each once before rolling so nothing errors on camera.
- **On a paid Blotato plan** - the analytics + comments/DM tools in the NEW part are paid-only.
- Real posts with real comments + analytics to pull for the NEW part.
- The fan-out diagram + the one-tool map graphics for overlay cutaways.
- Face reference image for the thumbnail shoot: ~/.claude/skills/yt-thumbnail/tylerai.png.
