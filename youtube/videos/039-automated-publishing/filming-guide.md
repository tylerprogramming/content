# Filming Guide - I Automated Publishing My Content With Claude Code

Target runtime: 12-14 min. Showcase format, demo-heavy. The energy and the "watch it populate" payoff carry this video. Shoot it so the edit can stay fast.

## The one rule that makes or breaks it
Speed-ramp or cut EVERY wait. Uploads take real minutes. Claude "thinking" takes real seconds. On screen that is death. Any progress bar, spinner, or thinking indicator gets ramped down to 1-2 seconds or cut entirely with a clean jump. The viewer should feel like it's instant even though it isn't. Capture extra handle on both sides of every wait so the edit has room to cut.

## What to shoot (in order)

### A. Talking-head cold open (0:00-0:35)
- Deliver the word-for-word cold open from script.md. Do 3-4 takes, pick the one where "I didn't publish it. Claude did." lands with a little grin, not a flat read.
- Shoot it looking straight down the barrel. This is the thumb-stopper - full energy from word one, no warmup take energy.

### B. Talking-head problem + system (0:35-3:00)
- The posting-grind section and the one-screen stack overview. Conversational, a little frustrated on the grind ("that was me"), then confident on the fix.
- Say "nothing posts without my approval" here with intent - it's the objection-killer.

### C. Talking-head payoff, limitations, opportunity, CTA (9:30-14:00)
- The limitations beat (10:45) is the sincerity moment. Slow down, drop the energy, look right at the lens. This is the only slow part of the whole video - let it breathe.
- Everything else stays up.

## Screen-record checklist (the live demo - 3:00-9:30)
Record each of these clean, full-screen, at high resolution. Narrate live as you go so audio and screen match. Everything on screen must be REAL - no mockups, no staged fakes.

- [ ] **Piece 1 - yt-upload (YouTube):** Screen-record talking to the yt-upload skill, handing it the finished video file, title, description, thumbnail, and publish-at time. Capture the END STATE clearly: the video sitting in YouTube Studio as "Scheduled" with the custom thumbnail set, description filled, and publish time visible. That end frame is the proof - hold on it a beat. SPEED-RAMP the actual upload.
- [ ] **Piece 2 - Blotato (TikTok / Instagram / LinkedIn / X):** Screen-record handing Blotato the short clip and drafting the four platform captions. Capture that the captions are DIFFERENT per platform (show at least two side by side - the LinkedIn one with no hashtags, the Instagram one with the 5 hashtags). Then capture the approval step (you reading a draft) and the queue filling with all four scheduled.
- [ ] **Piece 3 - Pinterest:** Screen-record generating the pins. Capture the real pins - keyword title, Pinterest-written description, board assignment. Show 3-4 pins ready. Keep this recording tight, it's a shorter beat.
- [ ] **Piece 4 - LinkedIn text posts:** Screen-record generating the text posts from the video. Capture a full real post on screen (no hashtags, teaching tone). Show that there are 3 to space across the week.
- [ ] **Piece 5 - Resend email drip:** Screen-record generating the email sequence and the Resend schedule view with the week of emails lined up. Capture you reading/approving one before it's scheduled.

## The payoff montage (9:30-10:45) - shoot this deliberately
This is the video's climax and the most clippable shot. Get enough coverage to cut it fast and satisfying:
- [ ] A wide "everything at once" view if possible - the finished video on one side, platform queues on the other.
- [ ] Individual "tick to Scheduled" moments for each platform (YouTube, TikTok, IG, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, email) so the edit can rapid-fire them.
- [ ] Optionally a screen-record of this actual video's own scheduled entries across the platforms, so the "you're looking at the proof" line is literally true on screen.

## Energy and pacing notes
- Overall energy: up and moving, like showing a friend something you're a little proud of. Not hype, not salesy - "look, this actually works."
- The demo section (3:00-9:30) is the retention zone. Keep narrating, keep it moving, never sit in silence watching a spinner.
- One deliberate gear change: the limitations section drops to sincere and slow. Everything before and after is brisk.
- Re-say the approval line ("I approve everything, nothing auto-posts") at least twice on camera - problem section and limitations. It's non-negotiable for trust.

## Assets to have ready before filming
- A genuinely finished video file to run through the stack live (can be an older one, just needs to be real).
- Its title, description, and custom thumbnail ready to hand to yt-upload.
- The short-form clip already cut, ready for Blotato.
- All platform accounts connected and tokens valid in Blotato / Resend / Pinterest / YouTube so nothing errors mid-demo. Test each once before rolling.
- The one-to-many diagram graphic (one video in, 7 platforms out) for the overlay cutaways.
- Thumbnail identity image at ~/assets/identity/tylerai.png for the thumbnail shoot.
