# X/Twitter Thread — Claude Code Remote Control

---

## Main Tweet

Claude Code Remote Control is not what most tutorials make it look like.

It's a tunnel to your LOCAL machine - not cloud. Your files, your tools, everything stays local. Here's what that actually means and where it breaks.

---

## Reply 1

👉 Setup is one command: type /rc to start, type /rc again and choose Show QR Code, scan with your phone. Works in the Claude app or any browser. Available on all plans.

---

## Reply 2

👉 What you can actually do: approve tool calls remotely, continue conversations from your couch, run local skills while away from your desk - all because it's still hitting your local file system.

---

## Reply 3

👉 The limitation nobody mentions: your terminal must stay open. You CANNOT start a session from your phone - only continue one that's already running on your machine.

---

## Reply 4

👉 If your machine sleeps or your internet drops for ~10 minutes, the session ends. Plan your power settings accordingly if you want to stay connected through the day.
