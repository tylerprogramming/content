# 051 - Thumbnail Concepts

Brand: high contrast, ONE big short phrase, Tyler's face, orange accent #D97757. Dark background. Phrase must be readable at phone size (3-4 words max, huge). No em dashes, no hype.

## Concept 1 - "ANY WEBSITE" (RECOMMENDED)
- Left 60%: Tyler, chest-up, slight lean-in, genuine "watch this" look (not shocked-face). Lit on a near-black background.
- Right 40%: a website (a busy listings page) with a bold orange #D97757 ARROW sweeping into a clean spreadsheet with rows filling. The website is a little chaotic, the sheet is clean and orderly. Visual = mess in, order out.
- Text: **SCRAPE ANY WEBSITE** in two stacked lines, huge white bold, "ANY" in orange #D97757.
- Small orange "2026" tag chip in a bottom corner.
- Why: names the promise (the proven title word "ANY"), shows the payoff (site -> sheet) in one glance, face carries trust.

## Concept 2 - "WEBSITE -> SPREADSHEET"
- Center: a big orange #D97757 arrow between a small website thumbnail (left) and a full spreadsheet (right). The transformation IS the image.
- Tyler in the bottom-left corner, smaller, pointing up at the arrow.
- Text: **INTO A SPREADSHEET** across the top, white bold, "SPREADSHEET" in orange. Optional tiny "with 1 AI agent" underline.
- Why: outcome-first, zero ambiguity about what you get. Pairs with title #2/#4. Slightly lower face presence, so B-option.

## Concept 3 - "1 SENTENCE" contrast
- Split thumbnail. Left half tinted cool/gray: a wall of messy HTML code with a faint red X. Right half tinted with orange #D97757 glow: a clean spreadsheet.
- Tyler center-foreground straddling the split, relaxed, one eyebrow up.
- Text: **NO CODE. JUST ASK.** stacked, or **1 SENTENCE** huge in orange over the right half.
- Why: sells the 2026 shift (old brittle way vs just asking). Strong curiosity, but "NO CODE" flirts with the non-developer framing so keep the phrase as "JUST ASK" to stay on-voice.

## Notes
- Test Concept 1 first. It carries the strongest word ("ANY") from the winning title and the clearest payoff.
- Keep face expression confident-calm, not clickbait-shock (on-brand, humble).
- One phrase only. If any concept needs a second line to make sense, cut it.
