## Reference 1: Sandeep Swadia (241,452 views)

**Layout.** Classic right-third face, left-two-thirds content. Text stacked top-left. Center-left holds a product lockup with a branching diagram below it. Face is cropped at the shoulder, occupying roughly 35% of the frame width on the right.

**Color scheme.** Warm dark background - a blurred purple-to-rust indoor bokeh, low saturation, non-competing. Accent is mint green (roughly #4ECDC4). Face is warmly lit and clearly separated from the background. Overall warm and inviting rather than techy and cold.

**Typography.** Two lines, heavy condensed sans, all caps. Line 1 "YOUR LIFE" in white on the dark background. Line 2 "ON AUTOPILOT" in black on a solid mint block. Two words per line, no more. The block is the only high-saturation element in the upper half and it grabs the eye first.

**Visual elements.** A white rounded card holding the Claude Cowork wordmark and icon, centered. Below it, a bracket-style org chart branching to five white cards, each with an orange Claude agent character doing a different job - email, calendar, painting, documents, clipboard. The diagram literally illustrates "one system, five agents."

**Face.** Yes. Middle-aged man, glasses, warm closed-mouth smile, direct eye contact. Calm and credible rather than shocked. Notably not the open-mouth surprise face - this is a trust play, and it's the #1 video in the category.

**Composition.** Moderately busy but strictly organized. Clear hierarchy: text, then logo, then agent row, then face. Generous dark space between the text and the diagram keeps it breathable.

**Why it's clickable.** "ON AUTOPILOT" is an outcome, not a feature. The agent diagram promises a system rather than a tip. The Claude branding is a trust signal for anyone already using it and a curiosity signal for anyone who isn't. The calm face reads "this person actually does this" rather than "this person is selling something."

## Reference 2: AI LABS (30,152 views) - the faceless outlier

**Layout.** Fully centered. Text as a single top band, node graph filling the lower two-thirds.

**Color scheme.** Near-black (#1E1E1E) background. Yellow highlight block. Blue connection lines. Extremely high contrast, minimal palette.

**Typography.** `/ADHD` in white, `Skill` in black on a yellow block. The slash prefix reads as a literal terminal command. Two words total.

**Visual elements.** A blue node mesh with five floating white app-icon cards - Claude, Claude Code, and a third tool - positioned as nodes in the network.

**Face.** None. Only faceless thumbnail in the top 10 of the research set, and it still earned a 4.2% like rate, beating four conventional thumbnails ranked above it.

**Why it's clickable.** The slash command is *specific*. It looks like something you could type in five seconds. It promises a named artifact rather than a concept.

Four of the top five run the identical structure: face right-third, text left-third, two to three words per line, and a **single highlighter block on the payoff word only**. Sandeep uses mint on "ON AUTOPILOT," Calum yellow on "millionaires," Remy red on "Everything," Tech With Tim mint on "EXPLAINED." Different hue, same skeleton.

## Recommendation

Generate **B and C**. B is the highest-confidence play - it's the exact structure of the #1 video with Tyler's face and his proven title formula. C is the cheap high-upside test, and the AI LABS data point says faceless can outperform in this specific niche.

A is redundant if B works, so skip it unless the face render comes out wrong.

Keep the mint accent on B (matches the #1 and #5 winners) and yellow on C (matches AI LABS and the #2 video). Do not use red - Remy's red is the outlier of the group and his video is a 2.5-hour course, a different promise entirely.
