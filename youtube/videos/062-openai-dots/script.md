## 1. Hook (0:00 to 0:30)

[Screen: Slack, the Dot's brief. No camera yet.]

"So this showed up in my Slack this morning. I didn't open ChatGPT. I didn't type anything. It's from my Dot."

[Camera]

"So I gave OpenAI's new Dot three real jobs. A morning brief for my channel, a check on my GitHub repos, and prepping a post for my Skool community when a video goes live. Every other Dots video I watched ends when it says 'I'll get started on that.' I wanted to see what actually came back. So let's look."

## 2. What a Dot is (0:30 to 1:30)

[Window C, scroll slowly]

"So quick version. A Dot is an agent that lives inside ChatGPT. It has its own computer in OpenAI's cloud, its own browser, and it runs on GPT-6 Astra. It connects to your apps through the same plugins ChatGPT already uses. OpenAI says over four thousand. That's their number, not mine."

"Two things that matter for this video. One, when you're not around, it can research on its own, but that's read-only. It can't send anything. Two, you get one Dot, and it's included on Pro or Business Premium. Not Plus."

## 3. Setup (1:30 to 2:30)

[Window A. Settings > Plugins. Point at GitHub and Slack.]

"So before any jobs, two plugins. GitHub, so it can read my repos, and Slack, so it can message me."

[Settings > Personalization > Custom rules]

"And these, I set before I turned anything on. There are four levels, from 'just do it' to 'hand it to me.' Creating or closing issues, merging or commenting on PRs: Hand off to you. That's the strictest one. Opening a PR: Ask before taking action. We're going to test that first one later."

## 4. Its computer and Activity (2:30 to 3:45)

[Dot's profile > its computer > Take over]

"So this is the part nobody clicks on. This is the Dot's computer. Not my Mac. A real machine in OpenAI's cloud, and I can take it over."

[Click into Chromium, type the address `youtube.com/@tylerreedai`, Enter]

"One thing. I typed a search in here the first time and nothing happened. It's not Google. You type an address. This is my channel, which is what job three checks every morning."

"If it gets stuck on a login or a captcha, this is where you step in."

[Return control. Then Profile > Activity.]

"And this is Activity. In progress, Scheduled, Completed. There are my three jobs. This is desktop only, by the way. You won't find it on your phone."

[Chat, type:]
```
What are you working on right now, and what needs my input?
```

[Read its answer. Point at anything that matches Activity.]

## 5. Job 1: the morning brief (3:45 to 5:15)

[Activity > Scheduled > the brief. Show the instructions.]

"Job one. Every weekday at 7:45, read my content repo on GitHub, my status file, my analytics, my ideas, and send me a short brief. What's filming next, yesterday's numbers, three video ideas."

[Click edit. Change the time by one minute, then back. Save.]

"You can open these and edit them now."

[Window B, the brief, full screen. Blur anything personal and say so.]

"And this is what it sent."

[Say it straight: did it name the right next video? Are the numbers real? Are the three ideas specific to your channel, or generic? One thing it got right, one thing it got wrong.]

"[Your verdict, one line.]"

"So it found [N] things. [One line on whether they were real.]"
