Duet (063) and Muse (065) follow this order so the three videos line up, and 066 (Dots vs Duet vs Muse) can cut between them.

## The 3 jobs (identical across Dots, Duet, Muse)

1. **Morning brief:** read the content repo (status.md, newest analytics, ideas, last 7 days of research), send a short brief: what's filming next, yesterday's numbers, 3 video openings.
2. **GitHub repo check:** daily, tylerprogramming repos, at most 5 items with links (failing checks, stale issues, Dependabot). Read-only.
3. **Skool video prep:** daily, if a video went public in the last 24 hours, draft the Skool lesson and a 120-word community post. Never post.

Plus one **live action that needs approval** (Dots: a PR through Codex, the "ask before" rule fired, approve, read the diff, merge).

## Rules that carried

- Show the real output, read it, say what you'd change. Never mock one up.
- Keep the hiccups (the missing Codex environment stayed in).
- Price said once, plainly, in beat 3, and again in the close.
- About 10 to 11 minutes.
