## Facts we say on camera (all from OpenAI's pages, checked by the researcher 2026-10-24)

- Powered by GPT-6 Astra. Own cloud computer and browser per dot.
- "Over 4,000 apps" is OpenAI's number. Say it as theirs.
- First dot is included on Pro ($100, $200, $500 tiers) and Business Premium. Not Free, Go, Plus, or standard Business. Pro users 18+ and outside EEA, UK, Switzerland. Tyler states which tier he is on; not in any file.
- Dot chats do not count toward ChatGPT limits. Tasks it starts in Work or Codex do. Extended limits for the first month; no number published.
- Custom Rules, real labels: Take action without asking / Take action when you say so (pre-approved) / Ask before taking action / Hand off to you.
- Auto-review runs before actions that affect accounts or share info. Separate from Custom Rules; cannot be turned off by either.
- Proactive research is read-only, enforced in code.
- Activity View: desktop app only. Profile > Activity shows In progress / Scheduled / Completed.
- You cannot view or edit individual dot memories (help center). Reset deletes the dot.
- One dot per account today. More later.
- Oct 13 update: dot can start Codex work from chat context and follow up on threads; Scheduled Tasks are now reviewable and editable; dot creation on mobile.
- Event-triggered tasks in Work can respond to Gmail, Slack, and GitHub activity (PR opened, review, merge). YouTube is not a listed source. Job 3 is a daily check, not an event.

## Title reasoning (checked against packaging-rules.md)

- Head term: "ChatGPT Dots" and "OpenAI Dots" both have demand; 20 of 35 videos over 100k. Contested, not empty. Gate passes.
- "I Tested X vs Y" is the one "I" shape allowed (3.30x). Versus beats price on the same topic (4.8x on TikTok). So the recommended title is versus-shaped.
- No money in titles. Pro price goes in the thumbnail text or line one of the description, not the title.
- Under 70 chars, one caps word late or none.
- Runtime: 13 to 14 min, inside the 10 to 35 band. Not a course, so no time box needed.
- Launch timing: Dots shipped 09-29, this films week of 10-27. That is 4 weeks, past the 48-hour window, so the news angle is gone and the comparison angle is the whole reason to make it. The research shows the vs-self-hosted lane is still empty, which is the only reason a late cover is worth the slot.
