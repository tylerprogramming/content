## Recommended

**How I Take a YouTube Video From Idea to Scheduled in Claude Code** (64 chars)
- The plainest, most honest promise. "Idea to scheduled" is the exact transformation and reads as a full outcome. "How I" keeps it first-person and un-hyped, which is the whole voice. Claude Code lands in the last two words so the keyword is still there.
