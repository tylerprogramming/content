# Hooks (first 15-20 seconds)

> Read/memorize word for word. Each uses a different opening technique. Open ON the result, no setup, cut to the terminal fast.

---

## Hook 1 - Proof-first / result open (RECOMMENDED)
*Technique: lead with the finished thing, then reveal how.*

> "This entire video, the idea, the research, the script, the thumbnail, and it sitting scheduled on my channel, I made all of it inside one tool just by talking to it. Nine skills, in order, idea to scheduled, all inside Claude Code. Let me show you the exact workflow."

---

## Hook 2 - Reframe / the plain problem
*Technique: name the usual mess, then flip to the simple version.*

> "Making a YouTube video usually means five tools and two days of guessing what to even make. I do the whole thing in one place now. Research, script, thumbnail, and scheduling, all inside Claude Code, just by talking to it. Here's the exact workflow, start to finish."

---

## Hook 3 - Direct address / doable
*Technique: put the viewer in the outcome right away.*

> "By the end of this video you'll be able to take a video from a blank page to scheduled on your channel without leaving one tool. That's what I do for every video I make. Nine skills, in order, inside Claude Code. Let me walk you through it."

---

**Delivery notes:**
- The retention line is "just by talking to it, all in one tool." Say it with quiet conviction, do not oversell. The edge is that it's real.
- On "nine skills," cut to the terminal listing the skills or a fast montage of the commands firing. Show the proof while you say the number.
- Keep it under 20 seconds before the first terminal cut. No throat-clearing, no "hey guys."
- Say the free doc out loud once early or right after the hook, not buried at the end.
