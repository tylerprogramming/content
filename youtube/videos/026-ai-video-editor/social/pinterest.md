## PIN 2 - The Concept (Explained)

**Board:** Claude Code Tutorials (`1121537182144784091`)
**Destination link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdvi2ooarDc
**Title:** How to Cut Dead Air With AI (Read the Loudness, Not the Transcript)
**Description:**
Here is the one insight that makes AI silence-cutting actually work. Do not use Whisper transcript timestamps to find silence, because Whisper stretches words across the pauses and the gaps disappear. Measure the real audio loudness instead, then ask Claude Code to cut. A three minute clip drops to under two, and only the silence is gone. Save this reference card.
**Alt text:** Infographic explaining how to cut dead air with AI by reading audio loudness instead of the transcript, in three steps.
