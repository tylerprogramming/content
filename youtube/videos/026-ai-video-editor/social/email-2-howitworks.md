## Email Body (paste into Resend)

```
Hey [first_name],

Following up on the editing video, because a few of you asked how the workflow actually goes start to finish. Here it is, plainly.

I finish recording a raw clip, full of pauses, no captions.

I open it in the HyperFrames studio and ask Claude Code to cut the dead air. It uses the actual loudness of the audio to find the silent gaps and trims them. Nothing I said gets touched, only the quiet. A 3 minute clip drops to under 2.

Then captions. HyperFrames has a registry of caption styles. I pick one, copy the prompt it gives me, and paste it into Claude Code. It transcribes what I said with Whisper so the words line up, and wires the captions onto the clip. If I want a different look, I ask for another style. That is the whole thing.

One genuinely useful tip if you go build your own version. To find silence, do not use the transcript. Whisper stretches words across the pauses, so the gaps disappear inside the words. Measure the actual audio energy instead. That is the difference between cuts that work and cuts that are a mess.

I walk through all of it on real clips here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdvi2ooarDc

The prompts I use and the setup are in my free community if you want to copy them:

https://free.tylerai.dev/youtube/

- Tyler
```
