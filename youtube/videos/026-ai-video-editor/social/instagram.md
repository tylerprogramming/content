## Caption
How I edit my videos with AI now, without being a video editor or building anything.

Swipe to see the full workflow.

The two parts of editing I hated were cutting dead air and adding word-by-word captions. Both are completely mechanical, so I stopped doing them by hand and started asking Claude Code instead.

I drop a raw clip into HyperFrames (HeyGen's open-source video tool), ask Claude Code to cut the silences, and a 3 minute clip drops to under 2. For captions I copy a style prompt and ask, and it transcribes with Whisper and wires them on. Want a different look? Just ask for another one.

It's not one-click magic and it won't replace a real editor. But once your styles are dialed in, this Claude Code AI automation is cheaper and faster than the caption apps I used to pay for.
