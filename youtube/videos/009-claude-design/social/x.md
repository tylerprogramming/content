**Main tweet:**

**Reply 1:**
The part nobody covers well is the handoff. Most Claude Design videos stop at the prototype. They generate a landing page and move on. I wanted the full loop: prompt, iterate, ship, deploy live.

**Reply 2:**
Start with a design system, the most slept-on feature. I didn't point it at a repo. I just uploaded 4 screenshots of designs I liked from Google Images. Claude pulled the palette, type, and spacing from those.

**Reply 3:**
Then you iterate in the canvas without prompting from scratch. Four ways: pull the tweak sliders, click and edit an element, drop a comment, or literally draw an arrow on it. You just point at the thing.

**Reply 4:**
The handoff is one line. Claude Design bundles the design, a readme, and the chat, and hands Claude Code a single command. It writes the page into my real Next.js repo, same components, same tokens.

**Reply 5:**
I deployed from inside Claude Code with the Vercel MCP. No dashboard, no tab switching, live URL back in two minutes. One honest catch: Claude Design has its own weekly meter, and one full workflow ate most of my Pro allowance.
