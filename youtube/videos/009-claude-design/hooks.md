# Hooks — Claude Design Video

Three options for the first 0-30 seconds. Each is word-for-word script.

---

## Hook 1 ⭐ Recommended — Proof Open (curiosity gap + visual payoff)

> I built this landing page in Claude Design, handed it off to Claude Code, and deployed it to a live URL in 23 minutes.

*[SHOW: Fast cut — Claude Design canvas prompt → generated design → Claude Code terminal running → live URL with domain in browser bar]*

> No Figma. No designer. No frontend code.

*[SHOW: Three X marks animate over icons of Figma, a designer illustration, a code file]*

> This video shows the full workflow end to end. Every click. Every prompt. Every error. Plus what it actually costs, because there's a catch nobody's talking about.

*[SHOW: Quick cut to Claude usage screen showing weekly limit warning]*

> Let's go.

**Technique:** Proof open. Show the result immediately, then reverse-engineer. "Plus what it actually costs" is the curiosity spike that carries past the first 30 seconds.

---

## Hook 2 — Contrarian Open (pattern interrupt)

> Everybody's making the same Claude Design video.

*[SHOW: Split-screen montage of competitor thumbnails — "INSANE", "UNSTOPPABLE", "IT'S OVER"]*

> Here's what they're all skipping.

*[SHOW: Tyler on camera, direct address]*

> None of them actually ship anything. They generate a prototype, mention Claude Code once, and call it a day. So I'm going to do the thing they won't. Prototype in Claude Design. Hand it off to Claude Code. Deploy to a real domain. And tell you what it really costs when you try to do this for actual work.

*[SHOW: Quick cut showing the final deployed site on a real URL]*

> Let's go.

**Technique:** Pattern interrupt + honest contrast. Slightly combative — high CTR on "what everyone's missing" framing, but only works if your audience respects Chase/Nate (calling them out by implication can backfire). Safer to leave channel names out.

---

## Hook 3 — Founder Story Open (relatable problem)

> You have one day to launch a landing page. You're not a designer. You don't want to pay three hundred dollars for v0 plus Lovable plus a Framer subscription. And the last thing you want to do is open Figma.

*[SHOW: Chaotic cuts of empty Figma file, Vercel v0 pricing page, Lovable paywall]*

> I had this exact problem last week. So I tried Anthropic's new Claude Design with Claude Code as the implementation layer. And I shipped the whole thing in 23 minutes for about three dollars.

*[SHOW: Final site on live URL, with the cost breakdown slide beneath]*

> Here's the full workflow.

**Technique:** Relatable-founder open. Story hook. Works especially well for LinkedIn / X clip-out of the intro. Slower to the point than #1, so retention through 0:30 depends on the setup landing.

---

## Selection guidance

- **Hook 1 is the pick.** It shows the finished product in the first 5 seconds (proof open has the highest retention on your channel historically), names the three things that are NOT required (Figma / designer / code), and plants the cost curiosity for later.
- Keep it under 25 seconds on camera.
- Energy: confident but not hyped. Let the result do the selling, not your voice.
