# Cold-open hooks - ElevenLabs Dubbing v2 (own-voice localization)

Rules: open on TYLER'S OWN VOICE speaking a language he obviously did not record (Spanish, Japanese, etc.), then cut to Tyler on camera. Say the full product name ("ElevenLabs Dubbing" / "Dubbing v2 in ElevenCreative") inside the first 5 seconds. Only dub content Tyler OWNS. Name the free resource out loud. No em dashes. No money claims. Pick one; the rest are backups or A/B options.

---

## Hook 1 (recommended)
[Cold open: a clip of Tyler from one of HIS OWN videos plays, but his voice is speaking fluent Spanish, his real voice, his cadence, his timing. Hold 3-4 seconds. Then cut to Tyler on camera.]
"That was me. My actual voice, speaking Spanish. I do not speak Spanish. I took one video I already made, ran it through ElevenLabs Dubbing, and it dubbed me into 90 plus languages while keeping my voice, my tone, and my timing. I put the exact step by step in a free doc, link in the description, so let me show you how it works."

## Hook 2
[Cold open: the same clip cycles through three languages of Tyler's own voice - Spanish, then Japanese, then Hindi - a second or two each.]
"Same video. Same voice. Three different languages, and I do not speak any of them. This is Dubbing v2 in ElevenCreative, and by the end of this video you will be able to turn one of your own videos into 90 plus languages in your own voice."

## Hook 4

I am going to dub one of my own videos live so you can see the whole thing, and I will point you to a free follow-along doc as we go."

## Hook 5
[Cold open: Tyler's owned clip plays in Hindi, 3 seconds.]
"Reaching people who do not speak your language used to mean re-recording everything, or hiring a whole localization team. Watch this. That is my own video, my own voice, in Hindi, done with ElevenLabs Dubbing. Stick around, because the real story is not one clip, it is turning your entire back catalog into 90 plus languages."

## Notes for editing the cold open
- The clip in the cold open MUST be content Tyler OWNS (one of his own videos). Never a film or another creator's clip. This is a hard brief rule.
- Use the SAME clip you dub later in the demo, so the payoff lands.
- Keep each language snippet to 2-4 seconds before cutting to Tyler.
- Get the full product name ("ElevenLabs Dubbing" or "Dubbing v2 in ElevenCreative") in before the 5 second mark in every version.
- Name the free follow-along resource out loud in the cold open (it is also pinned in the top comment and linked in the description).
- After the hook, go straight into a 1-line CTA plant (see script) before the build.
