## Honest framing and accuracy guardrails (do not violate)

These protect the payment and the trust. Every file in this package obeys them.

1. **Naming.** Say the full name in the first 5 seconds: "ElevenLabs Dubbing" and "Dubbing v2 in ElevenCreative." Never just "it." See BRIEF.md for the live-verify note on the ElevenCreative vs standalone-tool surface.
2. **Tiers, named accurately:** Automatic Dubbing (v2, Alpha) for the one-click path; Dubbing Studio (V1) for manual control (transcript editing, speaker reassignment, per-clip regeneration); Human-verified dubs via ElevenLabs Productions as the service tier. Lead the demo with Automatic Dubbing v2.
3. **Only dub content Tyler owns.** His own videos/clips. Never dub third-party content (films, other creators) on screen. This is a hard brief ban and it is baked into the filming guide.
4. **Preserves tone, emotion, timing, and voice.** This is the message the sponsor wants centered. Show the original vs the dub side by side so the preservation is obvious, not just claimed.
5. **It costs credits.** Automatic Dubbing v2 is priced per minute. Say plainly it is not free. Failed jobs are not charged.
6. **Alpha honesty.** Automatic Dubbing v2 is in alpha. Say it once. Some languages come out better than others - review the output, do not publish blind.
7. **Dubbing Studio may be maintenance-mode.** Verify before demoing it. If unavailable, describe it verbally, keep the live demo on Automatic Dubbing v2.
8. **API is not live.** "Coming soon." Do not demo or promise it.
9. **Review beat is mandatory.** Tell viewers to actually check the translation and listen through before publishing. This is the trust beat AND accurate product guidance.
10. **The localization-as-a-service angle is Tyler's opportunity framing,** not an official ElevenLabs earnings claim. No dollar figures, no income promises.
11. **No competitors named. No money claims. No em dashes.**
