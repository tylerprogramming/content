## Top 3

1. **I Turned My Videos Into 90+ Languages In My Own Voice (ElevenLabs Dubbing)** - best balance of named keyword, the "my own voice" jaw-drop, and honest showcase-then-system framing.
2. **ElevenLabs Dubbing: One Video, 90+ Languages, Your Real Voice** - keyword-first, three-part promise, strong for search.
3. **Turn One Video Into 90 Languages Without Losing Your Voice (ElevenLabs)** - centers the tone/voice-preservation message the sponsor asked to focus on.

---

## FINAL PICK

**I Turned My Videos Into 90+ Languages In My Own Voice (ElevenLabs Dubbing)**

Why: it names the product for SEO, states the outcome plainly (90+ languages), and the "my own voice" phrase is the single most filmable, most shareable idea in the whole product. It rides the evergreen AI-dubbing/global-audience demand, sounds like Tyler and not an ad, and has no "new," no date, and no dollar amount, so it does not age.

## A/B plan

- **Launch title:** I Turned My Videos Into 90+ Languages In My Own Voice (ElevenLabs Dubbing)
- **48-72h swap if CTR is soft (below channel baseline):** ElevenLabs Dubbing: One Video, 90+ Languages, Your Real Voice
- **Second swap if search impressions are strong but CTR still low:** Turn One Video Into 90 Languages Without Losing Your Voice (ElevenLabs)
- Keep the thumbnail constant across the first swap so you isolate the title variable; only change the thumbnail if both titles underperform.

## Thumbnail concepts

Brand note: ElevenLabs brand orange is fine as the accent. No dollar amounts anywhere. Tyler's face is in-frame, horizontal 16:9. Log a hypothesis before publishing and grade it in video_learnings after.

**Concept A - "One face, many languages" (recommended)**
- Left third: Tyler, smart-casual, slightly amazed expression, looking toward the language chips.
- Right two-thirds: a row of speech bubbles or flag chips around a single waveform, each labeled with a language (Espanol, Japanese, Hindi, Portugues, Deutsch), all flowing out of one video thumbnail of Tyler talking.
- Big short text overlay: "MY VOICE" (white, bold) with "90+ LANGUAGES" in ElevenLabs orange underneath.
- One orange line from the original video thumbnail fanning out to the languages, so the eye reads "one video becomes many."

**Concept B - "Same voice, another language"**
- Split frame with a bold orange divider. Left: the original video still labeled "ENGLISH (me)." Right: the same still labeled "ESPANOL (still me)" with a small waveform.
- Tyler's face bottom-left, pointing across the divider, surprised look.
- Overlay text: "SAME VOICE" with "ElevenLabs Dubbing" small in the corner. Orange arrow crossing the divider.

Both: high contrast, face large enough to read on mobile, 3-4 words max on screen, orange as the single accent color, clean background behind Tyler.
