# Prospect Call - David Okafor (Non-Technical, Tech-Nervous)

Date: 2026-06-17
Attendees: Tyler Reed, David Okafor (consultant, operations + leadership)

## Summary

Intro call with David Okafor, an operations and leadership consultant who wants to build content to support his consulting practice. David is sharp and clearly successful in his field, but he is genuinely nervous about the technical side. He told me three or four times that he is "not a tech person," that he can barely manage his CRM, and that the moment he hears words like terminal or command line his eyes glaze over. So the whole call was really about lowering the fear and showing him the on-ramp.

His first and biggest question was whether he really needs to know how to code to use Claude Code. He assumed it was a developer tool and that he would be hopelessly out of his depth. I spent a good while reassuring him - you do not need to code, you talk to it in plain English, and the skills I set up wrap the technical parts so he is just typing a simple command and answering questions. I shared my screen and showed him a content skill running, and the relief on his face was real. Once that fear came down, his second question was practical - which skill should he build first. He did not want a giant system on day one, he wanted one win. I told him for someone in his position the highest-leverage first skill is usually the one that turns a single idea into a full content piece, because that is the bottleneck that stops most people, and we can layer more on once that one is part of his routine.

He is interested but cautious by nature and wants to sit with it. He asked good questions about what ongoing support looks like since he knows he will get stuck. Solid prospect, just needs hand-holding and a very gentle start.
