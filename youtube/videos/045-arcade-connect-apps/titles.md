# Titles - 045 Arcade Connect Apps

> ## LOCKED (2026-08-13, rev 2 — after the competitor teardown)
> **Turn Claude Code Into A Real Assistant In 7 Minutes (No Code)**

## Why this changed

**rev 1 (Tyler's pick, earlier 2026-08-13):** `Claude Code Can Finally Use ANY
Tool (7 Minutes, No Code)`. Good instincts — it kept the 7-minute time bound and
carried a caps word. The problem is that **"ANY Tool" is abstract**. The viewer
cannot picture what they end up with, so there is nothing to want.

The competitor teardown (`youtube/analytics/2026-08-13-competitor-teardown.md`)
found that Chase AI does **31% of his subscriber count per video**, the best rate
of any channel measured and on a channel only 7x our size. His single best video
in 30 uploads — 5.10x, 256,518 views — is:

> **Turn** Claude Into A Design **GENIUS** In **3 Simple Steps**

The engine is a **transformation verb**: not "here is a capability", but "you end
up with this". Every one of his titles names an end state the viewer becomes.
Notably, his channel contains no "X Explained" video at all.

`Turn Claude Code Into A Real Assistant` is that exact move, and it keeps both
parts of rev 1 that were already right: the 7-minute bound and `(No Code)`.

**Sabrina note:** rev 1's rationale cited "the completeness move that carries
Sabrina Ramonov's outliers." That reasoning is withdrawn. Sabrina runs 5,970
median long-form views on 353,000 subscribers — **1.7% of subs, against our
1.4%**. She is in the same hole we are, so her patterns are not evidence.

## Locked

1. **Turn Claude Code Into A Real Assistant In 7 Minutes (No Code)** — 61.

   kept.

## Strong alternates

2. **Your Claude Code Can't Touch Your Apps. This Fixes That.** — 54.

   This is the day-7 swap: a different strategy, not a reword.
3. **Claude Code Can Finally Use ANY Tool (7 Minutes, No Code)** — 57. rev 1,
   kept as the control.
4. **Turn Claude Code Into Your Personal Assistant In 3 Simple Steps** — 62.
   Closest to a direct clone of the 5.10x. Only use it if the video really does
   break into three steps on screen; do not promise a structure the cut lacks.

## A/B plan

Launch with #1, swap to **#2** if needed — the two are a clean strategy test:
#1 promises a transformation, #2 accuses the viewer of a problem. Whichever wins
tells us something reusable about the whole channel, which a reworded variant
never would.

**Wait 7 full days before swapping.** BRAIN [2026-08-12]: a payoff word buys the
first 72 hours, not the week, and day-1 multiples flatter badly. The Opus 5 video
was swapped off a well-chosen title at 48-72h and now sits at 0.9% on 19,334
impressions — the locked title was never actually tested, and that impression
pool is spent.

Record both the swap date and the impression count at swap in `performance.md`,
or the test produces a number nobody can interpret later.

## Trade-off, stated

Still loses the search surface that naming Gmail or Slack would buy. Accepted:
this is a showcase, not a search play, and 047 covers the search side.
