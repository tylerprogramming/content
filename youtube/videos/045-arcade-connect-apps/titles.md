# Titles - 042 Arcade Connect Apps

**Recommended:** Give Claude Code Access to Your Real Apps in 7 Minutes (Arcade MCP)

1. **Give Claude Code Access to Your Real Apps in 7 Minutes (Arcade MCP)** [RECOMMENDED]
   Promises the outcome and the time, names the tool, and matches Arcade's own "7-minute" framing. "Real apps" is the hook, that is what people actually want.

2. **The Easiest Way to Connect Gmail and Slack to Claude Code**
   Names the two apps people already care about and leads with the low-friction promise. Good for search intent around "connect Gmail Claude Code."

3. **I Gave Claude Code My Gmail, Calendar, and Slack (Here's How)**
   First-person and concrete. The specific app list signals a real demo, not theory, and "Here's How" sets the how-to expectation.

4. **Claude Code Can't Touch Your Apps. This Fixes That in 7 Minutes.**
   Pain-first framing. States the limitation everyone hits, then promises a fast fix. The two-sentence structure reads well as a title.

5. **Connect Claude Code to Any App Without Fighting OAuth (Arcade MCP)**
   Speaks to the developer who knows the auth pain. "Any app" widens the scope and "without fighting OAuth" is the credible engineer wedge.
