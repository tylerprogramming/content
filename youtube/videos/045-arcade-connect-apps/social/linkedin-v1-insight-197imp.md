Most people think the hard part of getting Claude Code to use your real apps is connecting them.

It really isn't. You can wire up connectors for Gmail or Slack yourself, and they work. I have done it plenty of times.

The hard part is the auth. Anything Google related makes you reconnect every seven days unless you are a full production app. So you set up something that actually helps, you walk away, and a week later your agent has quietly stopped working.

So in the new video I show how I actually do it now. I use arcade.dev to connect Claude Code to Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Slack, and ClickUp. It holds the per-user auth for you, so you pick your tools, paste one command, authorize once, and it just keeps running. You can even add other people to it without ever touching a token.

Then I gave it one prompt: read the partnership thread in my Gmail, write it up as a Google Doc, drop that in a Drive folder, and post the summary to Slack. It did all five steps. The whole thing took about seven minutes to set up.

I also left in the mistake I made on camera, because it trips everyone up: if you add a new tool after the fact, you have to reconnect before Claude can use it.

Full walkthrough is on my channel today.
