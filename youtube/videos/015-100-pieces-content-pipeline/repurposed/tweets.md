## Source: How I Create 100+ Pieces of Content Per Week with AI

---

## Standalone Tweets

**Tweet 1 (hot take):**
Most "AI content" is slop because people use AI to decide what to say, not just how to format it. The thinking has to be yours. The reformatting can be the machine's.

---

**Tweet 2 (practical tip):**
If you're creating original content for every platform you're on, you're doing it wrong. One idea should become 8 formats. LinkedIn version, carousel version, X version, short-form version. Same thinking, different shape.

---

**Tweet 3 (hot take):**
"I don't have time to post consistently" is almost never a time problem. It's a systems problem. People with less free time than you are posting 10x more. The difference is the pipeline, not the hours.

---

**Tweet 4 (practical tip):**
The fastest way to write a LinkedIn post: don't start from scratch. Start from what you already said out loud. Record yourself explaining the idea, transcribe it, then let AI reformat it for the platform. Voice-first writing is faster and sounds more human.

---

**Tweet 5 (stat + insight):**
74% of people say they can now tell when content is AI-generated. That number was 40% two years ago. The bar for "sounds like a human" keeps rising. Which means your actual perspective, stories, and voice matter more than ever.

---

**Tweet 6 (practical tip):**
One rule I follow for content: AI never decides what I say. It only decides how to format what I've already said. Sounds simple. Changes everything about how the output reads.

---

**Tweet 7 (curiosity/open loop):**
I made 107 pieces of content this week. The week before that, 112. I work a 9-to-5. The system is the whole story.

## Thread Option (3 tweets)

Step 5: Schedule - 15 min. Everything into Blotato. Posts all week automatically.
