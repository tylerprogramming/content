# Hooks - 054 (word-for-word cold opens)

Structure for all three: cold-open PAYOFF over b-roll, face DELAYED to ~0:08, specific claim,
named deliverable, objection pre-empt, the approval/honesty beat, delayed credibility
(IBM/Chase/Pfizer), roadmap tease, one open loop, hard cut into the build.
No em dashes. No hype words.

---

[0:00 SHOW: screen-record, Instagram post going live + a comment reply landing, no face]

any of it.

[0:04 SHOW: Claude Code terminal running, then the Blotato calendar filling in]

comments and DMs and writes the replies.

[0:08 FACE REVEAL, push-in]
And I never opened the Instagram app to do it.

[0:11] Now before you close this. Nothing here posts on its own. Every post and every reply

[0:18 SHOW: chips - "creator" / "small brand" / "never coded"] It does not matter if you run a

[0:24] I have been a software engineer for eight years, IBM, then Chase, now I do AI at Pfizer,
and I still would not hand my account to a bot I could not check. So I built one I can.

[0:32 SHOW: "In this video" roadmap card, 1-2-3] In this video I am going to build all three

show you the one guardrail that keeps the whole thing from getting my account banned.

[0:40 HARD CUT to build] Let's start with the content agent.

- why recommended: opens on the receipt (a live post plus a real reply), face at 0:08, the
  honesty beat lands before the objection can form, resume is delayed and framed as the reason
  for the guardrail, and the "ban" line is a clean open loop the messaging-window rule closes later.
