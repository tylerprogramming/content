# Thumbnail - 054

Rules: high contrast, ONE big short phrase, Tyler's face, orange #D97757 as the accent.
No money amounts, no hype words. The phrase must read at phone size in under a second.

Palette: near-black or deep charcoal background, white primary text, orange #D97757 on the
one word that carries the meaning. Face lit, real expression (not shocked-face).

---

## Concept A [RECOMMENDED] - "IT RUNS ITSELF"
- Big phrase, two lines: "MY INSTAGRAM" (white) / "RUNS ITSELF" (orange #D97757).
- Tyler on the right, arms slightly out, a "look, no hands" calm-not-hyped expression.
- Left/behind him: a faint Instagram grid dissolving into terminal text, so the eye reads
  "Instagram + code" instantly.
- Small robot/agent chip badge over the grid, subtle, not cartoonish.
- why: the outcome IS the hook. Shortest possible phrase, one orange word does the work,
  pairs cleanly with every title option. Highest contrast, highest clarity.

## Concept B - "3 AGENTS"
- Left: giant "3" in orange #D97757, word "AGENTS" white beneath it.
- Right: Tyler pointing at three stacked cards labeled with tiny icons: a pen (post),
  a calendar (schedule), a chat bubble (reply).
- Background near-black, cards have a soft orange edge glow.
- why: sells the build, not just the result. The "3" gives instant structure and pairs with
  title option 6. Good if we want the curiosity of "three of what?"

## Concept C - "I DIDN'T POST THIS"
- Phrase: "I DIDN'T" (white) / "POST THIS" (orange #D97757), over a real Instagram post mockup.
- Tyler in the corner, hands up, half-grin, "wasn't me" energy.
- The IG post has a visible auto-generated caption + a reply, faint terminal reflected in it.
- why: contradiction hook, most curiosity of the three. Slightly riskier (reads as clickbait
  if the face overacts), so keep the expression dry and honest. Pairs with titles 2 and 3.

---

## Notes
- Keep text to 2-4 words max on all three. One orange word only.
- Test all three at 320px wide. If any word is unreadable, cut a word.
- Face should look calm and credible, not shocked. The honesty of the video is the brand.
- A/B candidates: A vs C (outcome vs contradiction). Lead with A.
