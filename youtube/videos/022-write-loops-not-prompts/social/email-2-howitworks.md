## Email Body (paste into Resend)

Hey [first_name],

Following up on the loops video, because a few of you asked how the workflow actually goes. Here it is, plainly.

There are four commands, and you only need one to start.

/loop runs a task on a schedule. You give it an interval and a job, like "every morning at 7, run my research." It fires quietly in the background and expires on its own so it can't run forever.

/goal runs until a finish line you wrote is true. A second fast model grades the work after every pass, so it keeps going until the job meets your standard, then stops.

/batch fans one instruction across a bunch of agents at once, for volume. And ultracode is the heavy machinery for scale. You will not need that on day one.

Here's my real one. Every morning at 7, before I'm awake, a loop runs my content research, hands what it finds to my script skill, drafts the week's short scripts, and then stops and shows me. It does not post or schedule anything. I read the drafts with coffee and thumbs-up the good ones.

That's the guardrail part that matters. A safe loop knows the intent, has something that can say no, has skills worth calling, and can always stop.

I walk through all of it here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQ57cUcGDGg
