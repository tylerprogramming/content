Two quick things to close out the week.

First, the honest part, because a lot of people talk about loops like you flip a switch and your work does itself. It does not go like that. A loop is dumb on its own. The real work lives in the skills it calls, and a loop that runs forever with no brakes is how you wake up to a mess.

None of that is a reason not to start. You just deserve to know it going in.
