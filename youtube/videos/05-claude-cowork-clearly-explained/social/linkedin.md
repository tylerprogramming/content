## Post C (the honest / contrarian angle)

Every creator showed you Claude Cowork this week like it is flawless. I am not going to do that, because it is not fair to you.

Here is the honest list of what it cannot do yet.

It does not remember across sessions. Normal Claude remembers your past chats. Cowork does not, so if you want it to remember, you keep notes in your folders.

It is desktop only. No mobile version, you have to be at your computer.

A task you start and watch runs locally, so the app stays open for those. Scheduled tasks are the exception. They become routines that run on Anthropic's servers, so they keep firing even with your laptop closed.

None of that is a dealbreaker. It is still a research preview, which means this is the worst it will ever be, and it is already good enough that I use it every week.

What it actually does is give Claude a pair of hands. It opens your folders, reads and writes your real files, browses the web for you, and can even run tasks on a schedule on its own.
