## Email 2 - Monday
**Subject:** Skills, MCP, plugins (the thing everyone mixes up)
**Preview:** Two minutes and you are ahead of most people using this tool.

Hey {first_name},

Quick one today, because this is the thing that confused me the longest.

People mix up skills, MCP, and plugins constantly. Here is the simple version.

A skill is know-how. It teaches Claude how to do something, your way, over and over.

MCP is access. It connects Claude to something it cannot reach on its own, like your Gmail or a database.

A plugin is the box. It bundles skills together so you, or your whole team, can install all of it in one command.

That is it. Once that clicked for me, everything else got easier.

I go deeper on all three in the new masterclass, with real examples on screen so you can actually see the difference.
