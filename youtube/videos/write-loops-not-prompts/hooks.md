# Hooks: Write Loops, Not Prompts

> Three options for the first 30 seconds. Each is word-for-word and uses a different technique. Pick one, memorize the beats, deliver to camera.

---

## Hook 1 — Borrowed-authority + "you're doing it wrong" (RECOMMENDED)

*Technique: curiosity gap + borrowed authority. This is the exact structure that made the 467K winner work — proven on this topic.*

> The guy who built Claude Code just said something that flips everything you've been taught about AI.
>
> He said: "I don't prompt Claude anymore. I write loops that prompt Claude. My job is to write loops."
>
> That clip got 700,000 views in a day - because it quietly made every prompt tip you've ever saved obsolete.
>
> Here's the problem. Almost everyone, including me until recently, is still typing one prompt, getting one answer, and doing it again tomorrow. That's the slow way.
>
> In the next few minutes I'm going to show you the shift - from prompts to loops - and I'm going to show you a real loop that runs my entire content business while I sleep. No code required. Let's get into it.

**Why it works:** Opens on someone else's authority (zero risk for the viewer), drops the viral quote as proof, names the viewer's current behavior as "the slow way," then promises a concrete payoff (a loop that runs a business, no code). The "until recently, including me" keeps it humble and relatable.

---

## Hook 2 — Cold open / show the payoff first

*Technique: pattern interrupt + show-don't-tell. Start on the result, then rewind.*

> This post went out this morning. So did this one. And this carousel. And this email to 400 people.
>
> I wrote zero of them today. I was asleep.
>
> I didn't schedule them either - I didn't touch anything. A loop did. It woke up, figured out what my business needed, ran the skills, and queued it all up before I opened my laptop.
>
> This is the difference between prompting Claude Code and writing loops. And it's the biggest shift in how I use AI in the last year.
>
> Let me show you exactly how it works - and how to build your first one today.

**Why it works:** Leads with tangible output the viewer can see ([SHOW: real posts/carousels]), then the reveal ("I was asleep"). Highest "wait, what?" factor. Best if you have strong B-roll of actual finished content.

---

## Hook 3 — The reframe / bold claim

*Technique: bold claim + simple analogy. Teach the mental model in the first 20 seconds.*

> Prompts are a vending machine. You put one in, you get one thing out, and then it's over.
>
> A loop is an employee. You tell it what "good" looks like once, and it keeps working - checking, fixing, repeating - until the job is actually done.
>
> Most people are still feeding the vending machine one coin at a time. The people getting absurd results from Claude Code stopped doing that months ago. They write loops.
>
> I'm a content creator, not a developer, and loops now run most of my business. In this video I'll show you the shift, the exact commands, and a real loop running my content engine. Let's go.

**Why it works:** The vending-machine-vs-employee analogy makes an abstract concept instantly graspable for non-developers. Establishes Tyler as "not a developer" early, which widens the audience and builds trust with the creator niche.

---

## Recommendation

Use **Hook 1** for the main upload - it's the proven structure, front-loads the viral quote as a retention anchor, and the "while I sleep, no code required" promise is the exact hook your audience clicks for. If your edit has strong finished-content B-roll, **Hook 2** is the higher-ceiling cold open - consider shooting both and picking in the edit.
