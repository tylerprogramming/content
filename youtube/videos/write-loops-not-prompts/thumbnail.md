# Thumbnail Brief: Write Loops, Not Prompts

## Reference 1 — Sean Kochel "Write Loops, Not Prompts" (the direct competitor)

`reference-thumbnails/sean-loops.png`

- **Layout:** Hard split-screen. Left third = "PROMPTS" label (red, underlined) over a cracked terminal icon with a red X. Right third = "LOOPS" label (green, underlined) over a glowing rainbow loop/refresh icon. Face dead-center, large, looking straight at camera.
- **Color scheme:** Dark/neutral background. Red = the "old broken way," green = the "new way." High contrast, traffic-light semantics.
- **Typography:** Two words only — PROMPTS vs LOOPS. Bold, all-caps, colored underline bars. Minimal.
- **Visual elements:** Two competing icons (broken terminal vs glowing loop). The red X kills the old way instantly.
- **Face:** Centered, serious/intense expression, neutral lighting. Confrontational, "listen to me" energy.
- **Composition:** Clean, symmetrical, one idea. Reads instantly at small size: old vs new.
- **Why it's clickable:** Pure binary contrast + a strong "you're doing the left one" implication. Curiosity: which side am I on?

## Reference 2 — Austin Marchese "HOLY SH*T / TRY THIS" (the 467K winner)

`reference-thumbnails/austin-winner.png`

- **Layout:** Faces left, giant white reaction text right ("H*LY SH*T"), orange "TRY THIS" pill bottom-right. Blurred terminal in background.
- **Color scheme:** Dark warm background, white text, orange CTA accent, the Claude burst logo used as the `*`.
- **Why it's clickable:** Raw emotion. Two surprised faces + a reaction phrase competes on the emotion shelf, not the tutorial shelf.

---

## Patterns to carry into ours

1. **Binary contrast wins this topic.** PROMPTS (old/broken/red) vs LOOPS (new/working/green). The whole video is one reframe — the thumbnail should be that reframe.
2. **Two words max.** Don't crowd it.
3. **Use the Claude burst logo** as a recognizable anchor (the `*`, or beside the loop icon).
4. **Face + intensity** carries CTR; a centered, slightly intense or surprised expression beats neutral.
5. **Leave a clean third** for editor text overlay (no baked-in text in the AI generation).

## Differentiation from Sean

Sean's is dev-coded and abstract (terminal vs loop icon). Ours signals **a business running itself.** Swap the generic icons for a creator-business cue: a content dashboard / scheduled posts / "while you sleep" visual on the LOOPS side. Same red-vs-green binary, but the green side shows *output* (content, growth), not just a loop icon.
