# Tomato Soup

**Servings:** 4
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 25 min

## Ingredients

- 2 cans (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh basil

## Instructions

1. Melt butter in a pot over medium heat. Saute onion 3-4 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cook 1 minute.
3. Pour in crushed tomatoes and chicken broth. Add sugar.
4. Simmer 15 minutes.
5. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth.
6. Stir in heavy cream. Season with salt and pepper. Garnish with basil.
