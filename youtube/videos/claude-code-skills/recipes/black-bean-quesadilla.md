# Black Bean Quesadilla

**Servings:** 2
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 6 min

## Ingredients

- 2 large flour tortillas
- 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup shredded cheese (Mexican blend)
- 1/4 cup corn kernels
- 1/4 cup salsa
- 1 tsp cumin
- Sour cream for serving

## Instructions

1. Mash half the black beans with a fork. Mix with whole beans, corn, salsa, and cumin.
2. Spread bean mixture on half of each tortilla. Top with cheese.
3. Fold tortillas in half.
4. Cook in a skillet over medium heat, 3 minutes per side, until crispy and cheese melts.
5. Cut into wedges and serve with sour cream.
