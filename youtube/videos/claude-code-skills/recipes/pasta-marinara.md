# Pasta Marinara

**Servings:** 4
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 20 min

## Ingredients

- 1 lb penne pasta
- 1 can (28 oz) San Marzano tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- Fresh basil leaves
- Salt to taste
- Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. Cook penne according to package directions. Drain.
2. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat.
3. Add garlic and red pepper flakes, cook 1 minute.
4. Crush tomatoes by hand and add to pan. Add oregano and salt.
5. Simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Toss with pasta. Top with fresh basil and parmesan.
