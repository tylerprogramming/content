# Avocado Toast

**Servings:** 2
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 2 min

## Ingredients

- 2 slices sourdough bread
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Red pepper flakes
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Everything bagel seasoning (optional)
- 2 eggs (optional, for topping)

## Instructions

1. Toast bread until golden and crispy.
2. Halve the avocado, remove pit, and scoop into a bowl.
3. Mash with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
4. Spread avocado mixture on toast.
5. Top with red pepper flakes and everything bagel seasoning.
6. Optional: fry or poach eggs and place on top.
