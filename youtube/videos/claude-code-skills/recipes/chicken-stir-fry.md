# Chicken Stir Fry

**Servings:** 4
**Prep Time:** 10 min
**Cook Time:** 12 min

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs chicken breast, sliced thin
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil

## Instructions

1. Toss chicken with cornstarch and 1 tbsp soy sauce.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a wok over high heat.
3. Cook chicken 4-5 minutes until golden. Remove and set aside.
4. Add broccoli and bell pepper, stir fry 3 minutes.
5. Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds.
6. Return chicken, add remaining soy sauce and sesame oil. Toss to combine.
