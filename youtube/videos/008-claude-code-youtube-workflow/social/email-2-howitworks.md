## Email Body (paste into Resend)

```
Hey [first_name],

Following up on the terminal workflow video, because a few of you asked how the whole thing goes start to finish. Here it is, plainly.

I start with an idea for a topic and nothing else.

First I run /yt-search. It hits YouTube, pulls the newest videos on that topic, and sorts them by views. I get a clean report of what's working, and just as useful, what nobody has covered. That gap is usually my angle.

Then /transcribe. I feed it the URL of a top performer, it pulls the audio and runs Whisper, and I get the full transcript. I don't just read it, I talk to Claude about how the video is structured and where the gaps are.

Then /yt, the big one. I give it the topic and my angle, it does its own web research, asks me a few questions, then writes the whole package. Titles, hooks, a full script, a filming guide.

Last, /thumbnail. It calls Kie.ai to generate options from a prompt, and I remix until one lands.

Four commands. Research, competitor analysis, a finished script, and a thumbnail, all from the terminal.

One thing worth saying: these skills aren't magic and they aren't a tool. They're markdown files with instructions, and every one I add makes the next video faster.

I walk through all of it here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MLfyfNj1JrI

The skills and setup are in my community if you want to copy them:

https://www.skool.com/the-ai-agency

- Tyler
```
