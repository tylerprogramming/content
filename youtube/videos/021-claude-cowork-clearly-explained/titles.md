# Titles - Claude Cowork Clearly Explained

**Date:** 2026-06-17
**Strategy:** Replicate the exact title shape that won (Eliot Prince, 966K). "Clearly Explained" + "for beginners" is an evergreen SEARCH title, not a news title. It keeps pulling beginners months after upload. Do not get clever.

---

## Primary (ship with this)

**Claude Cowork Clearly Explained (& How to Use It for Beginners)**

- Near-identical to the proven 966K title. The format works, the search traffic is real and continuous.
- "Clearly Explained" promises the one thing beginners want: clarity.
- Parenthetical adds the beginner qualifier without cluttering the main phrase.

## A/B alternate (swap to if CTR < 4-5% after a few days, while it's still getting impressions)

**Claude Cowork Clearly Explained (Full Beginner Walkthrough)**

- Same anchor, slightly different curiosity. "Full Walkthrough" signals depth/completeness.

## Rules
- Keep "Claude Cowork" first - that's the search term.
- Keep "Clearly Explained" - that's the proven hook word.
- No dollar amounts, no hype words like "INSANE" - this video wins on calm clarity, the title should match the tone.
- Title and thumbnail must agree: calm, clear, beginner-safe. Not loud.
