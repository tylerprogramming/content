# Thumbnail brief — 047 Full Arcade Course

Built from the 2026-08-13 CTR audit and the Sabrina Ramonov thumbnail read in
`~/content/youtube/analytics/2026-08-13-last30-and-sabrina.md`.

## The rule

**Three words maximum, filling 40-60% of the frame, on a bright background,
face doing something.**

Every recent thumbnail on this channel breaks at least two of those. The 0.9%
one breaks all four.

## Spec

| | |
|---|---|
| Text | `CONNECT ANYTHING` or `ANY TOOL` — two words, nothing longer |
| Type size | fills 40-60% of the frame height. Test at 210px wide before accepting |
| Colour | yellow or orange on a saturated background. **Not dark.** Dark thumbnails vanish against YouTube's UI |
| Face | Tyler, expressive, **pointing at the text**. Present-and-neutral is what has been running at 3% |
| Elements | the text, the face, and at most one object. No logo grid, no badge, no second concept |

## What not to do

- Do not put the course length on it. `55 MIN` is a reason not to click.
- Do not put more than one product logo on it. The Arcade mark belongs in the
  video, not competing with the promise.
- Do not use the near-blank white treatment from the Opus 5 thumbnail. That is
  the 0.9%.

## Two to make

1. **`CONNECT ANYTHING`** — huge, yellow, on orange. Face right third, pointing
   left at the words. This is the primary.
2. **`ANY TOOL`** — two words only, even bigger, with a small cluster of
   recognisable app marks (Gmail, Slack, Calendar) as the single object.

Make both. Ship #1. Keep #2 for the day-7 swap if CTR is under 5%.

## Benchmark

The four course videos that produced 554 subscribers ran 5.5 to 9.1% CTR.
**Anything under 5% on this one means the thumbnail, not the topic** - the topic
is the channel's proven best.
