## Honest framing and accuracy guardrails (do not violate)

These protect the payment and the trust. Every file in this package obeys them.

1. **Naming.** Platform = **ElevenCreative**. Feature = **Flows** / **Flows Agent**. Say "Flows in ElevenCreative" or "ElevenCreative Flows." A single pipeline is "a Flow"; the product is "Flows." NEVER just "Flow" as the product name, never "ElevenLabs Flow."
2. **Full product name in the first 5 seconds** of the script. Not "it" or "this tool."
3. **Models:** say "50+ image and video models plus ElevenLabs' full audio stack - text to speech with Eleven v3, voice cloning, lip-sync, sound effects, and music." Do NOT say "30+" (stale). Real models safe to name: Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling, Flux, Seedance, Nano Banana.
4. **No "Dubbing v2 node."** Unverified. Localization is done by swapping the voice/language input and re-running that branch.
5. **Iteration is not free.** Re-running a node = new generation = new credit charge. Frame it as "you only re-run the part that changed," never "free re-rolls."
6. **Flows is in alpha.** Say it once. Protects accuracy as the UI shifts.
7. **API is not live.** "Coming soon." Do not demo or promise it.
8. **The "10 hooks x 3 languages" framing is Tyler's own demo idea,** not an official ElevenLabs stat. Present batching as "here is what I would do," not a quoted spec.
9. **Studio vs Flows.** Studio = timeline editor. Flows = node canvas. You can export a Flow into Studio to finish. Do not conflate them.
10. **No competitors named. No money claims.**
