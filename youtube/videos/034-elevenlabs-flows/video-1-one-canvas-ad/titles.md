## FINAL PICK

Why: it owns the open "ElevenLabs Flows" keyword, states the outcome plainly, rides the proven 200K-330K AI-ad lane, and the "one canvas" phrase is the single most repeatable, filmable idea in the whole product. It is evergreen (no "new," no dollar amount) and it sounds like Tyler, not an ad.

## Thumbnail concepts

Brand note: the ElevenLabs brand orange is fine to use as the accent. No dollar amounts anywhere. Tyler's face is in-frame, horizontal 16:9.

**Concept A - "One canvas, one ad" (recommended)**
- Left third: Tyler, smart-casual, slight surprised/impressed look, looking toward the canvas.
- Right two-thirds: the node canvas with visible connected nodes (image -> video -> voice -> lipsync -> composition), and one bright thumbnail of the FINISHED ad playing on the Composition node.
- Big short text overlay: "ONE CANVAS" (white, bold) with "FULL AD" in ElevenLabs orange underneath, or a single orange arrow from the photo node to the finished ad.
- Orange accent line tracing the node connections so the eye follows photo -> finished ad.

**Concept B - "Photo in, ad out"**
- Split frame with a bold orange divider. Left: a plain product photo (a shoe, a bottle, a can). Right: a cinematic still from the finished video ad.
- Tyler's face bottom-left corner, pointing across the divider.
- Overlay text: "1 PHOTO -> FULL AD" with "ElevenLabs Flows" small in the corner. Orange arrow crossing the divider.

Both: high contrast, face large enough to read on mobile, 3-4 words max, orange as the single accent color, clean background behind Tyler.
