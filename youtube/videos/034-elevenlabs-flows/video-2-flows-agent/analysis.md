## Accuracy and honesty guardrails (do not get these wrong - payment + trust depend on it)

1. **Naming.** Platform = **ElevenCreative**. Feature = **Flows** / **Flows Agent**. Say the full product name ("Flows Agent in ElevenCreative" or "ElevenCreative Flows Agent") in the **first 5 seconds**. Never call the product "Flow" singular. A single pipeline is "a Flow"; the product is "Flows."
2. **Flows Agent is NOT ElevenAgents.** Flows Agent is the AI that builds your Flows pipeline inside ElevenCreative. ElevenAgents is a separate conversational/phone-agent product. This video is only about Flows Agent. Do not mention ElevenAgents at all.
3. **Models.** Say "50+ image and video models plus the full audio stack - Eleven v3 text to speech, voice cloning, lip-sync, sound effects, and music." Do not say "30+". Nameable real models: Veo 3, Sora 2, Kling, Flux, Seedance, Nano Banana.
4. **Billing.** Flows Agent chat is billed on **tokens**; generations use **normal credits**. Iterating and re-running is not free. Any time cost comes up, pair it with Assist mode. Never imply free re-rolls.
5. **Alpha.** Flows is currently in alpha - say it once. Protects accuracy if the UI shifts.
6. **API.** "Coming soon." Do not demo or promise it.
7. **No "Dubbing v2 node."** Not confirmed in the Flows node menu. Do not claim it.
8. **No competitors, no money claims, no puffery.** Honest brand. Show what is possible by demonstrating it.
