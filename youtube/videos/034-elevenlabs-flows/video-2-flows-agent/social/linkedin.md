I typed one sentence. An AI built the whole video pipeline.

Not a demo on someone else's account. Mine, on camera.

So here's the thing about making video ads with AI right now. The tools are powerful, but you end up jumping between five of them, and wiring node canvases together kind of feels like a part-time job.

I tested the Flows Agent in ElevenCreative this week.

You describe the video you want in plain English. It asks a couple of smart questions first, how long, what tone, what voice.

Then it picks the models, creates the nodes, connects them, and runs the whole thing.

Here's what actually stood out to me:

1. It asks before it spends. Every generation costs credits, so it checks with you instead of guessing. An AI that spends your money should work that way.

2. You iterate by talking. I said "use a warmer voice" and it re-ran just the voice, not the whole pipeline. Only the part that changed gets redone.

3. It runs in the background. I kicked off three ad variations, closed the tab, and walked away. Came back to finished videos.

Now the honest part, because there always is one.

It's in alpha, and iterating is not free. Every re-run costs credits. There's a setting called Assist mode that caps what it can spend without asking you, and I would turn that on before anything else.

This was a paid partnership with ElevenLabs, so in the video I show exactly what it does and exactly where it costs you money.

The whole point of all this is simple. There's a difference between a tool you operate and a tool that works for you.
