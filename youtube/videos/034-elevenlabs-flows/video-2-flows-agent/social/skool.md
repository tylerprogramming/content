## Video post

An AI built my whole video ad pipeline from one sentence

So I tested something this week that I want you all to see.

ElevenLabs has a feature called Flows, it's a node canvas for building video pipelines. Image node, video node, voice, music, all connected. Normally you wire that up yourself, and honestly it looks like a part-time job.

The new Flows Agent builds it for you. I typed one sentence describing a 30 second product ad. It asked me a few questions, how long, what tone, what voice. Then it picked the models, created the nodes, connected everything, and ran the generations.

Two things I really liked. You iterate by talking, I said "use a warmer voice" and it re-ran just the voice, nothing else. And it runs in the background, I kicked off 3 variations, closed the tab, came back to finished videos.

One thing to watch. Every re-run costs credits, so if you get in there, set Assist mode to auto-under-threshold first. That's your guardrail so it can't run away with your balance.

This was a paid partnership with ElevenLabs, and I still showed the costs and the alpha rough edges because that's the deal here.
