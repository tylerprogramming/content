Hey [first_name],

Following up on the ElevenCreative video from earlier this week, because a few of you asked how the agent actually works step by step. So let me lay it out plainly.

You open a Flow, which is just a canvas, and you tell the Flows Agent what you want in one sentence. Something like "a 30 second coffee ad." That's it. You're talking to it in plain English.

Here's what it does from there:

1. It asks first. How long, what tone, what voice. An AI that's about to spend credits should ask before it guesses, and this one does.

2. It builds the nodes for you. It picks the image and video models, creates each node, and wires the whole pipeline together. Images, animated clips, a voiceover, a music bed, all connected into one composition. You don't touch a single connector.

3. You iterate by talking. I said "use a warmer voice" and it re-ran just the voice node, not the whole thing. Only the part that changed gets redone, so you're not paying to regenerate work that was already fine.

4. You control the spend. There's a permission setting that lets it auto-run anything under a cost threshold and check with you on anything above it. I turn that on before I do anything else.

The part that still surprises me is how much you get to work with in there. It's a huge library of image and video models plus the full ElevenLabs audio stack, text to speech, voice cloning, lip-sync, sound effects, and music, all in one place.
