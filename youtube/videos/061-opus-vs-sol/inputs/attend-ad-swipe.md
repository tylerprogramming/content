# Attend — Ad Scripts (Cold, Meta)

**Format note:** hooks are locked wording (the first 2 seconds are too load-bearing to ad-lib).
Everything after is **beats** — the point plus the facts to riff on, not a read-aloud script.
Say it your way; hit the beat.

**Before you film, read the numbers warning at the bottom.**

# VIDEO

## V1 — "The 906 problem"
`20-30s · problem aware · at the desk · 9:16 iPhone`

**HOOK (locked):**
> "You have 906 members. 197 of them are actually there."

**BEATS**
1. The member count is the number you tell people. It isn't the number that shows up.
2. Fact: 581 of those 906 haven't opened the community in a month.
3. They're still counted. Still on the list. Still gone.
4. Point: Skool shows you growth. Nothing shows you decay.
5. Attend sorts your members by who is actually still here.

**HEADLINE:** See who actually shows up
**BODY:** Your member count goes up while your community empties out. Attend sorts every member
by who is still active, cooling off, or already gone. $47/mo.

**HEADLINE:** I built this for my own community
**BODY:** My member count was going up and I thought that meant it was working. Then I looked at
how many people had actually opened it that month. Attend is the tool I built after that. $47/mo.

# ⚠️ NUMBERS WARNING — READ BEFORE FILMING

The figures used above (906 members, 581 inactive, 70 never returned, 9 waiting / 21 days,
22% active) are taken from **the product mockup screens** in `~/attend/design/mockups/`.

Before any of these ship, confirm for each one:

- **Is it real data from your own community?** Then V3 and V4 work as written, because they're
  first-person claims ("70 people joined *my* community").
- **Is it demo/placeholder data?** Then you cannot say "my community" about it. Rewrite those
  two as second person ("your community probably looks like this") or swap in your real numbers.

This matters more than it sounds. V3 and V4 are your credibility ads. If the number is invented
and someone checks, the founder-story angle is the one that breaks.

**Action:** pull your actual Attend dashboard, write the real numbers here, and film against those.
Real numbers are also better creative. "70" is convincing precisely because it isn't round.

# Filming notes

- Vertical, iPhone, native look. The masterclass is repeatedly clear that polished
  underperforms organic-looking.
- Vary the setting across the seven so they don't read as one shoot: desk, walking,
  phone in hand, coffee, screen recording.
- Add a text headline on top of the video so it reads native in feed.
- One creative, one angle, one copy set. Do not recycle body copy across all ten.
- CTA on every ad: **Learn more**
- Destination: Attend landing page (must sell the CRM, not just the wedge).
