Skool is where your community lives. Attend is where you keep track of the people in it.

  1. Members

    Every member on one list: when they joined, when they last showed up, what plan they are on, and your notes on them. Search it, sort it, filter it.

  2. DMs

    See which conversations are waiting on your reply and which members you have never spoken to. Stop scrolling your inbox to remember who you owe an answer.

  3. Churn risk

    Attend flags members whose activity is dropping: fewer logins, no posts, a stalled course. You see them while there is still time to reach out.

  4. Course progress

    Know where each member is in your classroom and which lesson most people stall on, so you know what to fix first.

You read one message. Attend does the rest.

No new dashboard to remember to open. The brief shows up where you already look every morning.

  1. First
    Connect your Skool community. Attend pulls in your members, their activity, and their course progress.
  2. Then
    Pick a Slack channel. Choose where the brief lands and what time it arrives.
  3. Every day
    Read the brief, send a few DMs. Start with the members who need your attention.
