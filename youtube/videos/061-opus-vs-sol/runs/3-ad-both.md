## Opus 5.5

V8: "The regular who went quiet"
25-30s · problem aware · kitchen counter

HOOK (locked):
"Your most active member hasn't posted in two weeks. Did you notice?"

BEATS
1. Pain: you notice regulars when they leave, not when they start to.
2. Proof: 14% of members sit in cooling. Quiet, not gone.
3. Attend flags them the week they slow down, so you message first.

HEADLINE: Catch them while they're cooling
BODY: Regulars don't quit overnight. They slow down first. Attend shows you who.
CTA: Learn more

That's 87 words counted with wc, labels included. I left out prices and em dashes, and none of the banned hype words appear.

Closest to V5 ("Skool tells you who joined"), since both are about people slipping away before they're gone. V5 talks about decay across the whole community. Mine is about one person you already know by name, which makes the loss personal.

Before you film: the 14% cooling number comes from I1. The numbers warning in your swipe file says those figures come from the mockup screens. Swap in your real cooling percentage from the dashboard.

## GPT-6 Sol

V8 — “The quiet members”
30s · problem aware · at the desk · 9:16 iPhone

HOOK (locked):
> “Your member count won’t tell you who stopped showing up.”

BEATS
1. A member can go quiet without leaving your Skool community.
2. Proof: Attend sorts members into active, cooling, drifting, and gone.
3. Open the cooling list. Pick someone to check in with while they’re still reachable.
4. CTA: Learn more about Attend.

HEADLINE: Find the members going quiet  
BODY: Your member count stays the same when someone stops showing up. Attend shows you who’s cooling off so you know who to contact.

Closest to V5: both address quiet members, but this one ends with a specific action instead of a general view of decay.
