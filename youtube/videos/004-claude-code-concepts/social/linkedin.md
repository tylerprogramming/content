Most people install Claude Code, type one prompt, get confused by the terminal, and quit.

I get it. The first time I opened it I saw a black screen and a blinking cursor and had no idea what I was looking at.

Here's the thing though. Claude Code has way more features than you actually need.

You don't need all of them. You need about 23 concepts to go from total beginner to building real projects.

So I put every one in a single video and demoed each live in the terminal. No slides, no theory.

Let me give you the few that matter most.

The context window. This is the one most people get wrong. It's Claude's short-term memory, and it fills up as you work. When it fills, Claude starts forgetting what you told it and the answers get worse. Keep it clean and half your problems disappear.

CLAUDE.md. Probably the most important file you'll ever make. You write your rules once, how you like things done, and Claude reads it first every single session. No file means you're making it guess, and guessing leads to bad results.

Plan Mode. Before Claude builds anything, let it think first. It goes read-only, explores your code, and tells you the approach before touching a thing. You approve the plan, then it builds.

/rewind. My favorite safety net. Claude saves a checkpoint before every edit automatically. If it goes down the wrong path, you rewind to any earlier point. It's an undo button for your entire project.

And that's only four of them. There's also skills, MCP servers, sub-agents, hooks, and running the whole thing from your phone.

If you're just getting started, don't try to learn all 23 at once. Focus on the first ten, get comfortable with CLAUDE.md, Plan Mode, and managing your context.

Those three alone will change how the tool feels.
