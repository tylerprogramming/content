# Thumbnail Brief

## Reference thumbnail analysis - Nate Herk (147K views)

**File:** `reference-thumbnails/nate-herk.png`

- **Layout:** Centered focal object (the orange Claude logo icon, glowing) on a dark, dramatic background. Big text banner across the very top.
- **Color scheme:** Dark base (near-black with purple/orange bokeh), the signature **orange Claude glow** as the hero, green-tinted money accents. High contrast.
- **Typography:** One short line, huge bold white sans-serif with drop shadow across the top: **"This should be illegal."** No subtitle. Reads instantly at mobile size.
- **Visual elements:** The Claude logo wears a **gold crown** and is **raining $100 bills** across the whole frame. Warm rim-light glow under the logo. The money + crown do all the storytelling.
- **Face/person:** None. Pure object + text. (Note: his other top thumbnails DO use a big pointing face - both lanes work for him.)
- **Composition style:** Busy but with one clear center of gravity. The crown + cash + glow create curiosity and a money promise without a single number.
- **What makes it clickable:** Curiosity gap ("illegal?") + money imagery + the instantly recognizable Claude logo. You know it's about Claude Code and about money in under a second.

## What we keep vs change

**Keep:** the recognizable orange Claude logo as an anchor, dark high-contrast background, ONE short punchy text line (added in editor, not in the image), one clear focal point, warm glow.

**Change:** drop the fake money-rain (our angle is "real," not "get rich"). Replace the crown/cash story with a **real system / real skills** story - a grid of skill tiles, a terminal with real commands, or Tyler's face with confident "this actually runs my business" energy. Prove-it, not hype.
