## Post 1 — the drop (announcement + give the tools)

**Title:** I benchmarked Opus 5 vs 4.8 vs Fable 5 (and you can run it yourself)

New video is up. I ran the same 6 prompts through all three models and put the cost next to each one, then gave my honest take.

The short version: Opus 5 is half the price of Fable, it over-generates on builds, but it catches its own errors while it works, which is the real difference I felt. Fable is usually faster. 4.8 is the cheapest.

I dropped the exact prompts and the model-bench skill I built (runs one prompt across all three models and shows the cost and time) in the classroom so you can run the same tests on your own machine.
