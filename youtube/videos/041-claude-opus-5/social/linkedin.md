# LinkedIn — 041 Opus 5 (video text post)

**Text post (no hashtags, teach one thing, link at the bottom):**

Everyone is arguing about Claude Opus 5. So I stopped reading takes and ran the numbers myself.

Same six prompts. Three models: Opus 5, Opus 4.8, and Fable 5. Cost and time shown for every single one.

Here is what actually held up:

- Opus 5 is half the price of Fable. That part of the hype is real.
- Opus 5 over-generates. On builds it wrote two to four times the code, which eats the savings on the big jobs.
- But it catches its own errors while it works. The other two did not do that as cleanly. That is the difference I actually felt.
- Fable is usually faster, often by half.
- 4.8 is still the cheapest, and for simple things it is fine.

I also ran Opus 5 on 40+ of my real skills to see if it would break them like people said. Nothing broke.

So the honest answer is not "better" or "worse." It is "depends on the job, and here is the receipt for each one."
