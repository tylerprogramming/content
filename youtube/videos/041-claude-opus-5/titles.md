### Keywords by value

1. `claude opus 5` / `opus 5` - highest volume, must be front-loaded
2. `opus 5 vs fable 5` - the hot comparison query, many videos and articles fighting for it
3. `claude opus 5 review` - steady evergreen review intent
4. `is claude opus 5 worth it` / `should I switch` - decision intent, rising post-launch
5. `claude opus 5 pricing` / half the price - the cost story every source repeats

### Title formulas in play
- **Superlative shock:** "INSANE", "ABSURD", "freak", "GREATEST EVER" - saturated, and off-limits per voice rules
- **I tested X vs Y:** the credible lane, already used by two competitors
- **Proof tag in parens:** "(FULLY TESTED)", "(Fully Tested)"
- **Time-boxed:** "in 8 Minutes"
- **Verdict-first:** "It Beats Fable"
- **Honest hedge:** "brilliant (but annoying)", "Frustrating with Flashes of Brilliance" - the credible middle, and only in articles/one video

### Gaps nobody is filling
- **Nobody shows the same build twice, side by side, with the cost of each.** Competitors either recap benchmarks or test loosely. Tyler's two head-to-heads plus the cost receipts are the differentiator.
- **Nobody answers "do you still need Fable?"** Everything is framed as "which is better," not "can I cancel the expensive one." That is the actual question people have.
- **Nobody demos the effort toggle** despite it having its own search cluster.
