## Top 3 Recommendations

### Pick 1 (RECOMMENDED): "Is Claude Opus 5 Really Better?"
- The plainest version of the exact question everyone is asking on launch day. It reads like a real person's honest question, not a benchmark recap.
- Short, front-loads the keyword, and the "really" quietly signals there is a hype camp being pushed back on. That is the whole video in four words.
- Strong curiosity without a single hype word. The click feels earned because the answer is an honest yes-and-no, not a sales pitch.
- On-brand: it is about USING Claude on real work and reaching a verdict, not about model news.

### Pick 2: "Claude Opus 5: Better, or Just Cheaper?"
- Sharpens the tension the video actually resolves: it is cheaper for sure, but is it genuinely better, or only better on price?
- Names the cost angle (the proven "save you money" lane) while keeping the honest question intact. Best option if Pick 1 tests a touch soft on browse.
- Slightly higher curiosity gap than Pick 1 because it poses two answers and makes you pick.

### Pick 3: "Claude Opus 5 vs Fable 5: Which Do You Need?"
- Rides the comparison search head-on ("opus 5 vs fable 5") and matches the two head-to-head builds in the video.
- Clear, practical, high search intent. Best option if the goal is to win the comparison query rather than the curiosity click.
