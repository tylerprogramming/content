## Email Body (paste into Resend)

```
Hey [first_name],

Two quick things.

First, the honest part, because a lot of people online make AI coding sound like magic and I do not want to do that to you. The app we build in this video is small. A countdown timer. It is not meant to impress you, and it will not build your whole business by itself.

What it does is show you the door is already open. The barrier most people are still waiting on, learning to code first, is gone. You describe what you want in plain English and it builds it. That is the real takeaway, not the timer.

It also is not fully hands-off. You still tell it what you want and nudge it when it is not quite right. But Plan Mode and Escape mean nothing happens without you saying yes, which is exactly what you want when you are new.

None of that is a dealbreaker. You just deserve to know it going in.

Second, if you have not watched yet, this is your nudge. We go from never having opened a terminal to a working app in about ten minutes, together.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XAcqU3zuZmE

And the natural next step: the free starter guide and the resources I use are in my community, so you can set this up and build your own.

https://free.tylerai.dev/youtube/

Watch it if you have not, grab the guide if it is useful, and just pick something small to try.

- Tyler
```
