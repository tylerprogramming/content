# Titles - 053 Agents Do ALL Your Work

Rule: keep the proven "...To Do ALL Your Work" frame (7.4% CTR on the channel). Swap n8n for
AI agents. Under 70 chars. No money amounts. No hype words.

## Recommended

**How To Use AI Agents To Do ALL Your Work (Claude Code)**  (54 chars)
- RECOMMENDED (updated per research 2026-08-17). Preserves the exact 7.4%-CTR frame word-for-word,
  keeps the broad "AI Agents" search term, AND anchors on "Claude Code" in the parenthetical - which
  the landscape research shows is the #1 demand term (millions of views cluster on Claude-agent
  content). Best of both: proven frame + the term the audience actually searches.

**How To Use AI Agents To Do ALL Your Work (Full Setup)**  (52 chars)
- Prior recommended. Use as the A/B challenger if we want to test the neutral parenthetical against
  the Claude anchor. "(Full Setup)" signals a complete, doable build.

## Other options (all preserve the frame)

**How To Use AI Agents To Do ALL Your Work For You**  (50 chars)
- Adds "For You" to lean into the done-for-me promise. Slightly softer, very natural read.

**How To Use AI Agents To Do ALL Your Work (2026)**  (48 chars)
- Year stamp signals freshness against the 2025 original and dated agent content. Good if we
  want the "this is the current way" cue.

**How I Use AI Agents To Do ALL My Work**  (39 chars)
- First-person, more humble and more honest (it is his real setup). Trades the how-to search
  intent for authenticity. Strong if the thumbnail carries the instruction.

**AI Agents That Do ALL Your Work (Email, Calendar, Tasks)**  (57 chars)
- Front-loads "AI Agents," names the real apps so the payoff is concrete. Loses a little of
  the proven "How To Use" opening but gains specificity.

**How To Build AI Agents That Do ALL Your Work**  (44 chars)
- "Build" skews to the maker audience and matches the build format. Slightly narrower reach
  than "Use," which anyone with a job clicks.
