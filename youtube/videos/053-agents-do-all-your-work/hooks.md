# Hooks - 053 Agents Do ALL Your Work

Three word-for-word cold opens. Structure per the Nate teardown: cold-open PAYOFF montage over
b-roll with FACE DELAYED to ~0:08, specific quantified claim, named deliverable, objection
pre-empt, DELAYED credibility (IBM/Chase/Pfizer), roadmap tease, one open loop, hard cut.

Voice rules: no em dashes, no hype words, open on the payoff not an intro, humble, subject is
USING AI for real work.

---

## HOOK A - "before I woke up" (RECOMMENDED)

[SHOW 0:00, FACE HIDDEN: screen-record montage, agents already ran. Briefing doc scrolling,
inbox triaged into labels, a ClickUp task flipping to done, a calendar block appearing.]

"This morning my email was already sorted, my calendar was already cleared, and my tasks were
already updated before I got out of bed.

I did not touch any of it. A few AI agents did, while I was asleep.

[0:08 FACE REVEAL, push-in] And no, this is not one of those demos that only works on stage.
This is my real inbox, my real calendar, my real work.

[0:14, cards build 1-2-3] I am going to show you the four agents that run the boring parts of
my job. One reads my email and drafts the replies. One triages my inbox. One runs my content
pipeline. And one books my own calendar.

[0:24, chips: 'Developer' / 'Never coded'] It does not matter if you write code or if you have
never opened a terminal. An agent is just a text file, and I will show you the whole thing.

[0:32, delayed credibility, small lower-third: IBM -> Chase -> Pfizer] I have been a software
engineer for about eight years, at IBM, then Chase, and now Pfizer, and I still have one agent
in here that I refuse to let send anything on its own. I will tell you why later. [open loop]

[0:41, roadmap card 'In this video: 1 the setup, 2 the four agents, 3 the one I keep on a
leash'] Let's set it up. [HARD CUT]"

## HOOK B - "I stopped doing four jobs"

[SHOW 0:00, FACE HIDDEN: split montage, four small windows, each an agent working: Gmail
drafts appearing, calendar filling, status.md editing itself, a research report writing.]

"There are four things I used to do every single morning by hand. Sort my inbox. Draft the
replies. Update my tasks. Line up my next recording.

I do not do any of them anymore. Four AI agents do, and they finish before I am done with
coffee.

[0:08 FACE REVEAL] This is not a hypothetical. This is running on my real accounts right now,
the same email and calendar I have used for years.

[0:15, cards build] Each one is its own little worker. It gets a job, it uses the apps I
already own, and it hands back the result.

[0:23, chips] You do not need to be a developer for this. The whole agent is a markdown file,
maybe twenty lines, and I will show you every one.

[0:31, delayed credibility] I have written software for about eight years, IBM, Chase, now
Pfizer, and even I was surprised how little of this is actually code. [open loop: the surprise]

[0:40, roadmap card] Here is the plan: the one idea that makes it click, then the four agents,
then the mistake that will get you burned. [HARD CUT]"

---

## HOOK C - "the boring real job"

[SHOW 0:00, FACE HIDDEN: close b-roll of a briefing doc being written line by line by an agent,
timestamps showing it ran at 6am, then inbox labels sweeping in.]

"Everybody shows you AI writing poems and making images. Nobody shows you AI doing the boring
part of an actual job.

So this morning I let a few agents do mine. They read my email, triaged the whole inbox,
updated my tasks, and wrote me a briefing, before I woke up.

[0:08 FACE REVEAL] Real accounts, real work, no staged demo. I will scroll through every one
of these with you.

[0:15, cards build] Four agents, four boring jobs, and one idea that ties them all together.

[0:23, chips] Developer or not, it is the same setup, because an agent is just a text file and
a set of tools.

[0:31, delayed credibility] I have been an engineer for eight years, IBM, then Chase, now
Pfizer, so I have seen a lot of automation that breaks the second you trust it. One of these
I still do not fully trust, and that is on purpose. [open loop]

[0:41, roadmap card] Let me show you the setup first, then we build all four. [HARD CUT]"

---

**Pick:** Hook A. It leads on the strongest, most specific payoff ("before I got out of bed"),
names the four agents as a countable deliverable, plants a clean open loop (the agent he will
not let send), and lands the delayed IBM/Chase/Pfizer credibility exactly on the objection.
