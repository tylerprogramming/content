# Thumbnail - 053 Agents Do ALL Your Work

Rules: high contrast, one big short phrase, his face, orange #D97757 accent. No money amounts.
Phrase must be readable at mobile size (3-4 words max). Face shows a real reaction, not a smirk.

## Concept A - "DID IT ALL" (RECOMMENDED)

- Left: Tyler, chest up, looking slightly off-camera at the screen, mild surprised/impressed
  look (not shocked, humble). Dark background.
- Right: a phone or screen showing an inbox visibly emptying / labels stacking, glowing.
- Big text, two lines, top-right or lower band: "AGENTS DID IT ALL" with "ALL" in orange
  #D97757, everything else white. Heavy condensed sans.
- Small orange arrow or check sweeping across the inbox to draw the eye.
- Why: "ALL" ties straight to the proven title frame and gets the color pop. The emptying inbox
  is an instantly legible "the work is done" cue.

## Concept B - "WHILE I SLEPT"

- Tyler on the left, arms slightly up in a "wasn't me" gesture, relaxed.
- Right: a dark-mode dashboard at 6:00 AM, briefing doc + green checkmarks, faint clock.
- Big text: "WHILE I SLEPT" white, or "I SLEPT" with SLEPT in orange.
- A single orange "6:00 AM" tag in the corner.
- Why: leans on the strongest hook beat (before I woke up). Curiosity plus payoff. Reads as a
  personal, honest claim, not a course.

## Concept C - "4 AGENTS, 0 WORK"

- Tyler center-left, pointing at four stacked agent cards on the right (Email, Inbox, Content,
  Calendar), each with a small icon, one glowing orange.
- Big number "4" in orange #D97757, huge, with "AGENTS" under it; small "0 WORK" tag.
- Why: numbers thumbnail well and match the countable deliverable in the hook. The four labeled
  cards preview the exact payoff. Slightly busier, test against A.
