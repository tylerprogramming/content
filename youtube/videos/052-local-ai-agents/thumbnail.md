# 052 - Thumbnail concepts

Rules: high contrast, ONE big short phrase, Tyler's face, brand orange #D97757. Text must
read at phone size (3-5 words max). No money, no hype words. Pairs with the recommended
title "100% Local AI Agents in 2026 (Free, Private, No API Keys)".

## Concept 1 - "100% LOCAL" (RECOMMENDED)
- Big phrase: **100% LOCAL** stacked, huge, top-left. "LOCAL" in orange #D97757, "100%" white.
- Tyler on the right, half-frame, slight lean-in, one hand gesturing to the text.
- Behind him, a dark desk scene: a laptop with a terminal + a red-slashed cloud icon (data
  NOT going up). Subtle orange rim light.
- Bottom-right corner sticker: "NO API KEY" in a small orange tag.
- Why: leads with the single most-searched promise, one dominant phrase, high contrast on
  dark. Since the phrase already says LOCAL, pair with title 3 or 5 to avoid repeating it,
  OR keep title 1 (the "Free, Private" half still adds new info).

## Concept 2 - "WORKS WITH WIFI OFF"
- Big phrase: **WIFI OFF** (orange) with a struck-through wifi icon replacing a letter, and
  a smaller line above: "STILL WORKS" in white.
- Tyler left-third, surprised/confident look, pointing at a laptop.
- Laptop screen shows a terminal mid-run + an airplane-mode icon glowing.
- Why: sells the proof beat / open loop visually. Curiosity gap (how does an AI work offline?).
  Very scroll-stopping, less keyword-y, best as the A/B challenger.

## Concept 3 - "PRIVATE + FREE"
- Split phrase: **PRIVATE** (white) over **FREE** (orange), stacked left, oversized.
- Tyler right side, arms-crossed calm/trust pose.
- Background: a folder icon flowing into a laptop with a padlock, all on-device; faint red
  "cloud" crossed out in the corner.
- Why: names the two benefits that aren't the tool. Best pair with title 4 (Ollama/Qwen3),
  which carries the tooling keyword while the thumbnail carries the benefit.

## Shared specs
- Face: real, well-lit, orange rim light to tie to brand. Expression: confident, not shocked
  (voice = humble, not hype).
- Palette: near-black background (#0E0E10-ish), white primary text, #D97757 accent on the ONE
  key word only. Never more than two text colors.
- Text: bold heavy sans, max ~4 words visible, huge. Test legible as a small phone thumbnail.
- Keep face and text on opposite thirds; no clutter behind the head.
