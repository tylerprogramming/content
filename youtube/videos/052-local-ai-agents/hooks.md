Structure (from the Nate first-2-min teardown): cold-open PAYOFF over b-roll, FACE hidden
to ~0:08, one specific claim, named deliverable, pre-empt the "free/private/no keys"
objection, DELAYED credibility (IBM/Chase/Pfizer), roadmap tease, one open loop, hard cut.

Voice: no em dashes, no hype words, short sentences, humble, "so" openers, delayed resume.

## Why A is recommended
- Plants the strongest open loop ("I'm going to turn off the internet") as a promise instead
  of spending it at 0:00, so it pulls through the whole demo. C spends it immediately.
- Hits all three winning words by 0:14, kills the developer/beginner objection, roadmap card
  lands by 0:28, credibility stays delayed to ~0:38.
- B is the better A/B test if we want the privacy-pain framing (pairs with title 5).
