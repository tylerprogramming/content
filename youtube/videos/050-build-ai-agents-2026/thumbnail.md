# 050 - Thumbnail Concepts

Brand rules: high contrast, ONE big short phrase (3-4 words max, readable at phone size), Tyler's face, on-brand orange #D97757 as the accent. Dark background for contrast. No money amounts. Must pair with the title without repeating it word-for-word (title already says "5 Steps, No Code," so the thumbnail carries a different angle).

## Concept A (recommended) - "AI AGENT IN 5 STEPS"
- **Left third:** Tyler's face, slightly turned, a little surprised/confident. Lit clean on a near-black background.
- **Right two-thirds:** big stacked text, "AI AGENT" on top in white, "IN 5 STEPS" below with "5" huge in orange #D97757.
- **Behind/around the text:** a faint node diagram (Claude in the middle, thin orange lines out to a Gmail icon and a calendar icon). Signals "connects to real apps" at a glance.
- **Why:** owns the proven "5 steps" hook visually, the node lines say 2026/MCP without words, orange "5" is the focal point. Cleanest, most clickable.

## Concept B - "IT READ MY INBOX"
- **Left:** Tyler pointing at the screen, engaged expression.
- **Right:** a stylized inbox/brief card with a big orange checkmark and "5" flagged emails, one short phrase overlaid: "IT DID MY INBOX" (white, with "INBOX" in orange).
- **Detail:** small "before I woke up" tag in the corner, or leave it off for cleanliness.
- **Why:** leads with the concrete payoff (the cold-open promise) instead of the format. Strong curiosity, slightly narrower than A. Good A/B test partner.

## Concept C - "CHATBOT -> AGENT"
- **Split thumbnail:** left side dim/gray labeled "CHATBOT" with a plain chat bubble; right side bright with the orange accent labeled "AGENT" showing an app being handled. A bold arrow between them.
- **Tyler's face:** bottom-right corner, reacting toward the "AGENT" side.
- **Text:** "CHATBOT vs AGENT" or just "NOT A CHATBOT" in the corner.
- **Why:** sells the reframe (Hook B) and separates this from every toy-chatbot video. More conceptual, so test it against A rather than lead with it.

## Production notes
- Face must read at 120px wide. Test all three shrunk down.
- Orange #D97757 on exactly one element per thumbnail (the "5", the checkmark, or the "AGENT" side). Do not spread it.
- Keep total words on-thumbnail to 3-4. The title carries the rest.
- Generate via /yt-thumbnail, output to ~/content/youtube/thumbnails/. Lead A, A/B test A vs B first.
