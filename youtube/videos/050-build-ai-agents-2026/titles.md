   > Direct callback to the original's "EASILY," updated. "Easiest" is beginner catnip. A touch more crowded on curiosity than the recommended pick.

## Recommended

> **Turn Claude Code Into Your Own AI Research Agent (5 Steps)** — 58 chars

It is the only option that satisfies every priority rule at once:

- **Transformation verb, not description** (rule 2, named as the single biggest
  lever). `Turn X Into Y` is the exact shape of Chase AI's 5.10x.
- **Both head terms in the first 48 characters** — "Claude Code" and "AI ...
  Agent" — so the search engine has something to match on.
- **Ease qualifier with a small number** (rule 3). Five is the count the original
  26k video was known for, so it also inherits the proven promise.
- **Second person by implication** ("your own"), no "I" (rule 1).
- Under 70, no money, no em dash, no wording that implies Tyler is not a
  developer.

It gives up one thing: it does not open with "How To Build AI Agents", the exact
string the 26k original owned. If the priority is defending that query rather
than CTR, ship option 1 instead. That is the real trade, and it is a strategy
choice, not a wording choice.

**Do not swap inside 7 days.** If it needs a swap after that, change strategy to
option 3 (outcome-led), not to a reworded variant of option 2.
