# 050 - Titles (build AI agents 2026 remake)

All under 70 characters. The winner has to read beginner-safe, own the "build AI agents" search term, and signal 2026-current.

## Recommended
**How To Build AI Agents in 2026 (5 Steps, No Code)** - 48 chars
> Wins the exact search query, keeps the proven "5 steps" hook from the original, stamps it 2026 so it reads current, and "No Code" kills the "I'm not a developer" objection right in the title. Best of everything.

## Options

1. **How To Build AI Agents in 2026 (5 Steps)** - 41 chars
   > Cleanest match to the original that beat 26k. 2026 stamp does the "this is the current way" work. Safe, high-CTR, evergreen.

2. **How To Build an AI Agent in 2026 (No Code, 5 Steps)** - 51 chars
   > Singular "an AI Agent" reads more like a build-along and less like a listicle. Leads with the no-code promise. Strong beginner pull.

3. **Build a Real AI Agent in 2026 (Connect It To Your Apps)** - 55 chars
   > Leans on the 2026 differentiator: MCP connecting to real apps. "Real" separates it from toy-chatbot videos. Slightly narrower search match.

4. **How I Build AI Agents Now (Claude Code + 5 Steps)** - 49 chars
   > "Now" signals the old way is dead without saying 2026. "How I" adds the personal, teaching-a-friend trust. Names the tool for the Claude crowd.

5. **The Easiest Way To Build AI Agents in 2026 (5 Steps)** - 52 chars
   > Direct callback to the original's "EASILY," updated. "Easiest" is beginner catnip. A touch more crowded on curiosity than the recommended pick.

## Notes
- "No code" variant: option 2 and the recommended title. "2026" variant: recommended, 1, 2, 3, 5.
- No money amounts, no hype words, no em dashes - all compliant.
- Keep "5 Steps" in the winner: it is the proven promise the original was known for and the reason the format converts.
