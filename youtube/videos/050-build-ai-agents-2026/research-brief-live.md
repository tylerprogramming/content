## What's working
- **"Claude Just Changed [X] Forever!" + a niche + (Tutorial)** - the two biggest OUTLIERS use it
  (Samin Yasar / stock market, 14x subs; Danny Why / YouTube, 8x subs). Niche-specific + big claim.
- **"Connect Claude to [specific tool]"** integration tutorials overperform from smaller channels.
- **Beginner "Full Tutorial / Course"** mega-guides keep compounding.
- **Skills > agents is validated at the SOURCE** - Anthropic's "Don't Build Agents, Build Skills
  Instead" did 1.48M. That is literally 050's angle, and creators are barely covering it.
- **MCP has demand + FOMO** ("learn MCP RIGHT NOW" 1.65M).
