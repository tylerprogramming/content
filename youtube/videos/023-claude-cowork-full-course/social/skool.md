# Skool - Claude Cowork Full Course: Build Your AI Employee From Scratch

**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aOECs8oPZ2c

---

If you use Claude every day, this is the one I have been wanting to make for you.

Most of us treat Claude like a smarter ChatGPT. You type a question, it types back, and you still go do the work yourself. Cowork is a different thing entirely. It opens your folders, reads your files, builds new ones, and hands you finished work on your actual computer.

So I built the full course. An hour, module by module, and there is no coding anywhere in it.

It starts with setup, because Cowork lives in the desktop app, not the website, and you point it at one folder that becomes its memory. Then it gets practical. You organize a messy client folder, turn a pile of receipt photos into an expense spreadsheet, and draft emails that already sound like you. The part that clicks for most people is scheduled tasks. You ask once, and the report shows up in your folder every Monday before coffee.

I use Cowork every single day for the channel and this community, so everything in here is the actual system I run, not theory. It is still a research preview, so it is not perfect, but this is the worst it will ever be and it is already doing real work for me.

I dropped my actual CLAUDE.md files, the full prompt library, and the exact scheduled tasks I run right here in the community.
