## Top 3 Recommendations

### Pick 1: "Learn 80% of Claude Cowork for Your Business in 30 Minutes"
- Directly riffs on Jeff Su's proven formula (317K in one week)
- Adding "for Your Business" differentiates from every other tutorial
- "30 Minutes" sets clear expectations
- Strong SEO: "claude cowork" + "business" + "learn"

### Pick 2: "How I Use Claude Cowork to Run My Entire Content Business"
- Personal authority angle (Dan Martell's formula, 498K)
- "Entire Content Business" is specific and aspirational
- Implies a system, not just features
- Works well with a thumbnail showing Tyler's Cowork dashboard

### Pick 3: "I Replaced 3 Hours of Weekly Work with Claude Cowork"
- Specific, measurable claim creates curiosity
- Business result in the title, not a feature list
- "Replaced" implies a before/after transformation
- Pairs well with a split thumbnail (manual work vs Cowork)
