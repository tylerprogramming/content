# Hooks - Build Your First AI Agent (The Easy Way, No Code)

Cold-open rule for this video: kill the "it's too hard" belief in the first breath, then promise a real build the viewer can copy. Result and reframe first, no throat-clearing, no "hey what's up." Every variant is 10-20 seconds. Land on a "watch me build it live" promise so they stay for the demo. Use the Fortune-500 line as earned authority, once, plainly.

## Hook 1 - The reframe (RECOMMENDED, the provided cold open in his voice)
[Visual: quick flash of an intimidating framework diagram / wall of code, then it gets swept away, cut to Tyler.]
"Everyone makes AI agents sound complicated. Frameworks, orchestration, all this stuff that makes you feel like you need a computer science degree. I built these at Fortune 500 companies, and I'm telling you, the easy version takes about ten minutes. So let me just show you. We're going to build a real one together, right now, and you can copy the whole thing."

## Hook 2 - The belief-flip
[Visual: Tyler straight to camera, calm, terminal open behind him.]
"If you think building an AI agent is out of your reach, I want to fix that in the next ten minutes. I've built these things at big Fortune 500 companies, the hard way, with the frameworks and all of it. And here's what nobody tells you: for what you actually need, none of that is required. So I'm going to build a real agent with you right now, in plain English, and you can steal the whole thing."

## Hook 3 - The honest origin
[Visual: Tyler at desk, a bit more personal.]
"So a while back I tried to teach people how to build AI agents, and I used one of the popular frameworks, and honestly, it was too hard. People got lost, I got lost explaining it. So I dropped it. But agents themselves are not hard anymore. The easy way is genuinely easy. Let me show you. We're going to build a real, working one together in about ten minutes, no code."

## Hook 4 - The "what an agent even is" open
[Visual: plain text on screen: goal + a tool + plain english, then Tyler.]
"An AI agent sounds like this big scary technical thing. It's not. It's really just three things: a goal, a tool it can use, and you telling it what to do in plain English. That's it. I built these at Fortune 500 companies and I promise you the version you need takes about ten minutes to make. So let's build a real one right now, together, and you can copy it."

## Hook 5 - The result-first showcase
[Visual: the finished one-page research brief on screen, web search visibly running, then Tyler.]
"I typed one topic, walked away, and came back to this: a full research brief, real sources, all pulled and written by an AI agent I built in about ten minutes. No code, no framework, no computer science degree. And I'm going to build the exact same thing with you right now, step by step, so you can copy the whole thing."

## Director notes
- The enemy in the first 3 seconds is the word "hard." Show and then sweep away the intimidating thing (framework diagram / code wall), so the reframe lands visually before Tyler even speaks.
- Hook 1 is the lead recommendation. It is the provided cold open, it hits the reframe, the earned authority, the ten-minute promise, and the "copy the whole thing" payoff in one breath.
- Use the Fortune-500 line exactly once and keep it plain. It is authority, not a flex. If it feels braggy on the take, cut it to "I built these at big companies" and let the thumbnail carry it.
- Whatever variant is chosen, end on a live-build promise ("we're going to build a real one together, right now") so the viewer stays for the payoff.
- Do NOT overpromise. No "this will make you money," no "replace your job." The promise is: a real, simple, working agent you can copy, in about ten minutes. That's enough.
