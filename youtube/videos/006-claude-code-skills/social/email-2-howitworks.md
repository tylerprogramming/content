Following up on the skills video, because a few of you asked what a skill actually is once you open it up. Here it is, plainly.

Start with something you do all the time. For me it's pulling a clean transcript, running a daily journal, scripting a video.

You do it with Claude once, the normal way, by explaining what you want. When you like the result, you say make a skill out of this. That's the whole move.

Now open the folder and look. A skill is a single file called SKILL.md. At the top it lists a name, the arguments it takes, and the tools it's allowed to use. Below that is plain English instructions. No code, no config, no plugin system. It's a prompt with structure.

Two things worth knowing when you build your own. Put the skill in your home directory's .claude/skills folder and it works everywhere, in every project. And you update it just by asking, like telling your journal skill to also ask about your mood.

I walk through all of it, including a brand new skill built from scratch, here:
