# Honey Garlic Chicken

**Servings:** 4
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 20 min

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken thighs (bone-in or boneless)
- 1/3 cup honey
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sriracha (optional)
- Sesame seeds for garnish
- Green onion, sliced

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Sear chicken 4 minutes per side until golden.
3. Mix honey, soy sauce, garlic, and sriracha. Pour over chicken.
4. Transfer skillet to a 400F oven. Bake 12-15 minutes until cooked through.
5. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onion.
