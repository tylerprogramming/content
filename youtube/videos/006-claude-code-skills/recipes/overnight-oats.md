# Overnight Oats

**Servings:** 1
**Prep Time:** 5 min
**Cook Time:** 0 min (refrigerate overnight)

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1/2 cup berries (blueberries, strawberries, etc.)

## Instructions

1. Combine oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, and honey in a jar.
2. Stir well. Cover and refrigerate overnight (at least 6 hours).
3. In the morning, top with berries and enjoy cold.
