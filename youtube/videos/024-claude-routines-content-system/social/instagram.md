A Claude Routine is a prompt plus your connectors, packaged and put on a schedule. It runs on Anthropic's servers, so your laptop can be off and it still fires. This is the AI automation people keep missing.

I run two every week. A 6AM email summary that triages my inbox and drafts the urgent replies. And a weekly trend report that pulls data with Apify and lands in Slack every Monday.

Building one takes about 5 minutes. Write the prompt, add your MCP connectors, test it, schedule it.

The part nobody teaches: you can create Routines right from the Claude Code CLI with /schedule, and use /loop for hands-on local work.
