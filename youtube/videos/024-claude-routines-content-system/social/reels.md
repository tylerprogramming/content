## Reel 1 — "This ran at 5am while I was asleep"

**Angle:** the proof-first one. Show the result before explaining anything.

**Target length:** 30-45s. Three of the four researched accounts do better
under 45s, and this idea does not need longer.

**Shape:**

| Beat | What is on screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | The 6am summary sitting in your inbox, timestamp visible | "This ran at 5am while I was asleep." |
| 3-8s | Scroll the summary, show a drafted reply | "It read my inbox, drafted the replies, and left me a summary." |
| 8-15s | Cut to the routine in the Claude interface | "It is a prompt, plus the connectors it needs, on a schedule." |
| 15-25s | Point at the schedule field | "It runs on Anthropic's servers. My laptop was closed." |
| 25-35s | Back to the summary | "Took about five minutes to set up. It has run every morning since." |

**Hook rule:** the timestamp has to be legible in the first second. The whole
reel rests on it being real.

**Caption gate:** `ROUTINES`

## Reel 2 — "Two slash commands people keep mixing up"

**Angle:** the correction. This is the most useful thing in the video and it is
the thing people get wrong.

**Target length:** 20-35s. It is one distinction. Do not pad it.

**Shape:**

| Beat | What is on screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Terminal, both commands typed | "These two look the same. They are not." |
| 3-10s | `/schedule` | "Schedule creates a routine. It runs in the cloud, on a timer." |
| 10-17s | `/loop` | "Loop only runs while Claude is open and your computer is on." |
| 17-25s | Close the laptop | "So the test is simple. Shut your laptop. Does it still run?" |
| 25-30s | Back to the routine list | "If it needs to, you want schedule. Loop was never meant to last." |

**The visual that carries it:** physically closing the laptop. That is the
whole idea in one gesture and it is worth doing on camera.

**Caption:** ungated. This one is pure value, and the week already has two
gated carousels on this video.
