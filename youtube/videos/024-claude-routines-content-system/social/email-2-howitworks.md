## Email Body (paste into Resend)

```
Hey [first_name],

Want to show you the two Routines from this week's video, because they're the ones that made it click for me.

Routine number one is a daily email summary. Every morning at 6AM, before I'm awake, it reads my inbox, sorts what actually matters from what doesn't, and drafts replies to the urgent ones. I wake up and the triage is already done. I just review and send.

Routine number two is a weekly trend report. Every Monday, Apify pulls what's performing in my niche, Claude writes it up into something I can actually read, and it drops the whole thing into my Slack. I used to do that by hand and it ate an hour. Now I just open Slack.

That's the moment it clicks for most people. You're not typing into a chatbot anymore. You've handed off a real recurring job.

So here's the 5-minute exercise. Look at your own week and find the thing you do on repeat:

1. A report you pull every Monday
2. The same inbox you sort every morning
3. The same sites you check for trends
4. The same numbers you copy into a sheet

Pick one. In the video I build a Routine live from scratch so you can see exactly where every setting lives.

Here's the part people get wrong: a Routine doesn't run on your computer. It runs on Anthropic's servers, so it fires on schedule even when your laptop is closed. Your machine only needs to be on if you run something hands-on with /loop in Claude Code instead. Set the Routine once and forget it.

Here's the full walkthrough:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T50plh-k3MY

If you set one up, reply and tell me what you automated. I'll share the best ones next week.

- Tyler

https://www.skool.com/the-ai-agency
```
