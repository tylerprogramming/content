## POST 2 - The tactic (try this today)

Here's the one thing I'd want you to take from this week, and you can try it in about 5 minutes.

Most people pick the wrong task to automate first. They go for the hard one, the thing that takes real thought, and then the output comes back mediocre and they decide none of this works.

The test I use is simpler. Ask whether the task needs you in the middle of it.

If it does, that's a working session. Keep it on your machine where you can watch it and steer it. That's what /loop is for.

If it doesn't, if the whole thing is collect, sort, summarize, deliver, then it can run on a schedule without you there at all. That's a Routine.

The other half is how you write it. Write the prompt like instructions for someone new on your team, not like a question you'd type into a chat box. What to look at, in what order, what to ignore, what the output should look like when it's right.

A question gets you an answer once. Instructions get you the same answer every Monday.

Then add the connectors. Gmail, Slack, Drive, Apify, whatever the job actually touches. That's usually where the value shows up.

All of my Claude skills are free and public if you want to see how I write mine: github.com/tylerprogramming/claude-skills

What's one task in your week that genuinely doesn't need you present?

## POST 3 - The discussion (no link)

I want to ask you all something, because I think the answer is different for everybody.

I've been moving pieces of my week onto scheduled runs. Email triage at 6AM, a competitor report every Monday. Both of them used to eat an hour I never got back.

The thing I learned is that the tasks worth handing off are the ones that are repeatable AND boring. Not the ones that are hard. Hard is usually where your actual value is, and you want to keep sitting in that.

For me it was collecting. Opening tabs, scrolling messages, taking notes. Zero judgment in any of it, and it was the reason I skipped the weekly report whenever I got busy.

So here's my question.

What's the repeatable, boring part of your week? The thing you do the exact same way every time and dread a little before you start?

Be specific if you can. "Email" is hard to help with. "I read 40 messages every morning and forward 5 of them to my VA" is something I can actually work with.

Drop it below. If a few of you name the same thing, I'll build it and share it in here.
