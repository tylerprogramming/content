# Skool - Claude Routines Ran My Business at 5AM While I Slept

**Video:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T50plh-k3MY

---

I finally got my business to do some of the boring stuff without me.

At 5AM, while I was asleep, my inbox got triaged and my weekly competitor report got written. I didn't touch any of it. That's what Claude Routines do. A Routine is kind of just a prompt plus your connectors, packaged and put on a schedule, and it runs on Anthropic's servers, so your laptop can be closed.

Everyone's arguing about whether this killed n8n or Zapier. That's not really the point. The point is pairing Routines with Claude Code as one stack.

I run two every week. A 6AM email summary that reads my inbox and drafts replies to the urgent ones. And a weekly TikTok trend report that pulls data with Apify and drops it in my Slack every Monday.

In the video I also build one live in about 5 minutes, and then I show the part almost nobody is covering, creating Routines straight from the Claude Code CLI with /schedule. For hands-on local work, there's /loop.

I put the exact setup and the connectors I use in here. So here's my challenge to you: pick one thing you do every single week and turn it into a Routine.

What weekly task would you automate first?
