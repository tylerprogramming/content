My business ran while I was asleep.

Not a metaphor. My inbox got triaged and my competitor report got written before I opened my laptop.

That's the thing most people are missing about Claude Routines.

Everyone online is arguing about whether Routines killed n8n or Zapier. That's not really the story.

The real story is what happens when you pair Routines with Claude Code as one stack.

So here's how I think about it.

A Routine is just a prompt plus your connectors, packaged and put on a schedule. It runs on Anthropic's servers, which means your laptop can be closed and it still fires.

I run two of them every week.

The first is a 6AM email summary. It reads my inbox, sorts what matters, and drafts replies to the urgent ones.

The second is a weekly TikTok trend report. Apify pulls what's working, Claude writes it up, and it lands in my Slack every Monday.

Building a new one takes about 5 minutes. Write the prompt, add the MCP connectors you need, test it once, schedule it.

But here's the part almost nobody is covering.

You can also create Routines straight from the Claude Code CLI with /schedule. And when you need to sit there and work hands-on, there's /loop for local runs.

Cloud for the recurring stuff. Your machine for the hands-on stuff. Same stack.

None of this replaces your judgment. It just does the mechanical work you were never meant to be doing by hand.

So pick one task you repeat every single week and hand it off.
