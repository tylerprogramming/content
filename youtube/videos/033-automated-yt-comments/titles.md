## FINAL PICK
**I Automated My YouTube Comments With Claude Code**

Reason: It is the exact sibling of Tyler's #1 performer ("I Automated My Entire YouTube Workflow with Claude Code"). It keeps the brand consistency and search association that already converted for his channel, it is instantly clear, and it carries the proven-lane pattern the audience clicks. The honesty differentiator lives in the thumbnail and the first 30 seconds, so the title does not need to carry it.

## A/B plan
- Variant A (title): I Automated My YouTube Comments With Claude Code
- Variant B (title): How I Reply To All My Comments Without Faking It
- Keep the thumbnail identical across both so the title is the only variable.
- Run A as the launch title (brand-consistent, safest floor). If 48-hour CTR is under channel baseline, swap to B, which leans harder on the honesty curiosity gap.
- Thumbnail A/B secondary test: same face, swap the big text word between "AUTOMATED" and "NO SPAM."

## Thumbnail concepts
Shared style: black background, Tyler's face (real, expressive), huge bold text, orange accent, real proof element, NO dollar amounts.

### Concept 1 - "The Wall"
- Left third: Tyler's face, slightly surprised or amused expression, looking at the text.
- Background: a faint, darkened wall of real YouTube comment bubbles stacked up (the overwhelm), desaturated so text pops.
- Big text (right, stacked): AUTOMATED / MY COMMENTS in white, with "AUTOMATED" in orange.
- Small real-proof tag: a green check badge overlay reading "posted via API" on one comment bubble.
- Feeling: I tamed the chaos.

### Concept 2 - "No Spam"
- Center-left: Tyler's face, confident, one eyebrow up.
- Right: two stacked comment cards. Top card is a gray generic "Great video!!" with a red X through it. Bottom card is a real, specific reply with a green check.
- Big text top: NO SPAM in orange, huge.
- Sub text: JUST REAL REPLIES in white, smaller.
- Feeling: this is the honest version, not the bot version. Directly sells the differentiator.

Recommended launch thumbnail: Concept 2. It pre-answers the "is this just spam" objection in the feed and makes the honesty angle the reason to click.
