# Prospect Call - Priya Shah (Wants to Start a Channel)

Date: 2026-06-18
Attendees: Tyler Reed, Priya Shah (aspiring creator, finance + personal-finance niche)

## Summary

Intro call with Priya Shah, who is right at the starting line - she wants to launch a YouTube channel in the personal-finance and money-mindset space but has not posted anything yet. She has a day job in finance, a lot of knowledge to share, and a real fear of starting. Because she is pre-launch, the conversation was less about fixing a broken system and more about whether she can build the right habits from day one so she does not stall out like most people do at the very beginning.

Her two questions were the ones I hear constantly from people about to start. First, she asked how I come up with video ideas and never seem to run out - she is terrified that she will post five videos, run out of ideas, and quit. I walked her through how I treat ideas as a system rather than inspiration: pulling from what is already proven to work in the niche through research, mining audience questions and comments, and using a repeatable process so the idea well never runs dry. That reframing visibly took a weight off her. Second, she asked how long before she actually sees results, because she does not want to pour months in for nothing. I gave her the honest version - early views are slow and lumpy, most channels see meaningful traction somewhere in the months range not weeks, and the people who make it are the ones who keep showing up through the quiet early phase. I would rather she start with realistic expectations than quit at video ten feeling discouraged.

She is motivated and coachable, and being pre-launch she is actually a great candidate to build a clean system from scratch with no bad habits to undo. Not ready to commit financially yet, wants to start in the free community first.
