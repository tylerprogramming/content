# Tinkr - Full-Stack Engineer (Founder Interview)

Date: 2026-06-16
Attendees: Tyler Reed (candidate), Sloane Mercer (Co-founder / CEO), Bao Nguyen (Co-founder / CTO)

## Summary

This was a casual but pointed conversation with both founders of Tinkr, an early-stage startup around twelve people building a no-code workflow tool for small businesses. Sloane handled the vision and culture part, Bao handled the technical fit. They are pre-Series A, just closed a seed extension, and want a generalist who can own features end to end without a lot of process. The honesty about stage was refreshing - they said there is no QA team, no dedicated DevOps, and the person in this seat will sometimes be talking to customers directly to understand a bug.

Stack is React with TypeScript on the front end, Node and TypeScript on the back end, Postgres, and they are on Vercel and Render. Bao walked me through their architecture, which is a fairly standard monolith with some serverless functions, and was candid that there is meaningful tech debt from moving fast. The technical chat was less about whiteboard puzzles and more about how I would approach shipping a feature with thin specs, how I balance speed versus correctness in an early-stage codebase, and how comfortable I am with ambiguity. We talked through a hypothetical of adding a webhooks feature for their users and how I would scope an MVP of it in a week.

Comp is the big question mark. Base would be a real step down from my current Fortune 500 number, Sloane floated roughly 150k to 165k, but the equity is significant - she mentioned a grant in the 0.4 to 0.8 percent range depending on level, with the usual early-stage upside-or-zero caveat. The pace is fast, expectation is roughly startup hours, and they were clear it is not a coast. I like the energy and the autonomy. The risk is real and the comp tradeoff is the whole decision.
