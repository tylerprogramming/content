## Email Body (paste into Resend)

```
Hey [first_name],

Want to show you the one part of this week's video that made the whole thing click for me. (Quick note: this is a paid partnership with Granola, but everything here is real and I use it daily.)

First, how it connects, because people assume this is hard. It isn't.

Granola has an official MCP server. MCP is just the standard way to plug an outside tool into an AI like Claude Code so it can actually use it. You run one command in Claude Code, log in once in the browser, and you're done. No code, no config files.

After that, Claude Code can read across every meeting Granola has captured. Not one note. All of them.

Now here's the demo that got me.

I asked it to pull all of my meetings with one client and draft a scoped proposal from everything we've discussed.

It didn't summarize a single call. It went back through every conversation, the first discovery chat, the follow-ups, the offhand things they mentioned. Then it connected something the client said in call one to something they asked for three weeks later, and put both into the proposal.

I did not connect those dots. It did. That's a level of recall I honestly can't do as a human across months of meetings.

So here's the 5-minute exercise. Think about one relationship where the context is scattered across a lot of calls:

1. A client you've met with a dozen times
2. A project where scope kept creeping
3. A person whose asks you keep half remembering

Pick one, and imagine asking your AI to pull the whole thread out at once. That's the shift. You stop re-reading notes and start reasoning across them.

The proposal demo is near the end of the video if you want to jump to it:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nFen2thkbmg

If you try it, reply and tell me what it pulled that you'd forgotten. I read every one.

- Tyler

https://free.tylerai.dev/youtube/
```
