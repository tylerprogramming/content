## Top 3
1. **I Automated My Email With Claude Code** - the proven template applied to a universal pain. Lowest risk, strongest keyword, direct sibling of the #1 cohort video.
2. **I Don't Do My Email Anymore. Claude Code Does.** - highest curiosity of the honest options, and it doubles as the cold-open line so title and hook reinforce each other.
3. **Claude Code Now Runs My Inbox** - punchy present-tense proof framing, great for browse.

## FINAL PICK
**I Automated My Email With Claude Code**

Reason: it is the exact winning formula from the best-performing cohort video, applied to a broader and higher-frequency pain. It is unmistakably clear, carries the strongest search keywords, and holds the honest positioning with zero overclaim. The bolder options are better as thumbnail text and A/B challengers than as the primary title.
