# Hooks - I Automated My Email With Claude Code

Five cold-open options. Each is written to open on the same visual: an inbox going from chaos to triaged. Pick one for the word-for-word cold open in script.md. All are honest, no overclaim, no em dashes. Keep the first line under about 8 seconds of speech.

## Hook 1 - "I don't do my email anymore" (recommended)
On screen: a real inbox with a 99+ unread badge, then a hard cut to the same inbox cleanly sorted with a triaged label.
"I don't really do my email anymore. Claude Code does. This is my actual inbox, and I didn't sort any of this by hand. In the next few minutes I'm going to show you exactly how it works, and how you could build the same thing, or even sell it to a business. And no, nothing here is faked. It's a real inbox, so I blurred anything private."

## Hook 2 - The stat-of-time cold open
On screen: a stopwatch overlay next to the messy inbox.
"I used to spend the first hour of every day just sorting email. Now I spend about zero. Claude Code reads my inbox, tells me what actually matters, and drafts the replies before I even sit down. Here's the real thing running, and then I'll show you how to build it yourself."

## Hook 3 - The relatable-pain open
On screen: the chaotic inbox scrolling fast, unread piling up.
"This is what most people's inbox looks like. Newsletters, cold pitches, the two emails that actually matter buried somewhere in the middle. I got tired of digging, so I taught Claude Code to do the digging for me. Watch."

## Hook 4 - The direct-showcase open
On screen: Tyler at the keyboard, terminal on the left, inbox on the right.
"I'm going to talk to Claude Code, and it's going to go through my real inbox, find the emails that need a reply, summarize them, and draft the responses. I'm not touching the mouse. Let's see what it does."

## Hook 5 - The "here's the boring truth" open
On screen: chaos-to-triaged inbox cut.
"Everyone's selling you an AI email app for fifteen bucks a month. I didn't buy one. I built my own with Claude Code and the Gmail API, I own the whole thing, and I'll show you every piece so you can copy it. Here's my inbox before, and here it is after."

## Notes for filming
- Whichever hook you pick, the on-screen chaos-to-triaged cut must land in the first 3 seconds. That visual is the retention anchor.
- Say the privacy line early (Hook 1 does it best). It builds trust and pre-empts the "is this fake" comment.
- Keep the energy up and the pace fast. No throat-clearing intro before the hook.
- Do not promise money results. "Sell it to a business" is framed as an opportunity, not a claim about what Tyler has earned.
