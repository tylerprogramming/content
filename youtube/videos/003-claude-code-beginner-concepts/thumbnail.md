# Thumbnail Analysis — Claude Code Beginner Concepts

## Reference Thumbnail: Simon Scrapes "27 Concepts"

### Layout
- **Left third:** Person (host) — upper body visible, hand on chin in skeptical/thinking pose
- **Center-right:** "Claude" in massive white serif bold text, dominating the frame
- **Anthropic starburst logo** top-left, orange/salmon colored — trust signal
- **4 icon cards** horizontally across the middle-bottom: Sub-agents, Hooks, MCP-Servers, commands — dark brown rounded cards with orange icons and white text labels
- **"27 CONCEPTS"** in enormous white bold all-caps text at the very bottom, spanning most of the frame width

### Color Scheme
- Background: Rich dark warm brown (#3a1f10 ish) — feels earthy, warm, NOT typical dark tech blue
- Text: Pure white — extremely high contrast
- Accent: Salmon/orange for the Anthropic logo and icon card accents
- Overall: Dark/warm palette — distinct from typical blue-toned tech thumbnails

### Typography
- "Claude" — massive, serif/sans bold, white, centered — takes up ~40% of frame
- "27 CONCEPTS" — massive, all-caps, bold sans-serif, pure white, bottom — takes up ~20% height
- Icon card labels — small white text, clean, readable even small
- No subtitle text anywhere — very clean

### Visual Elements
- 4 icon cards showing concept categories (Sub-agents, Hooks, MCP-Servers, commands) — visual preview of content
- Anthropic starburst — credibility/association signal
- No arrows, no badges, no gradients on text

### Face/Person
- Man, mid-40s, blue eyes, beard
- Expression: skeptical/curious — hand on chin, slightly furrowed brow — NOT smiling
- Position: Left third, upper body, medium-close crop
- Takes up maybe 25% of the frame — face is secondary to the "Claude" text

### Composition Style
- Busy but structured — every element has a clear zone
- Three distinct visual bands: person left / text center-right / icons middle / big number bottom
- High contrast makes it readable at 120px thumbnail size

### What Makes It Clickable
- **Number (27)** — specific, implies density of value
- **"CONCEPTS"** — promises clarity and understanding, not just tips
- **Dark warm background** — stands out in a sea of blue/dark tech thumbnails
- **"Claude"** text huge — immediately signals topic without reading anything else
- **Skeptical face** — "I'm going to show you what everyone gets wrong" energy
