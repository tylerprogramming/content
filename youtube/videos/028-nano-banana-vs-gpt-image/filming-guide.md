# Filming Guide: Nano Banana Pro vs GPT Image 2

**Runtime:** 12-15 min. **This is the breakthrough bet - packaging is the main event.**

## Before filming
- [ ] Access to both: Nano Banana Pro (your Kie.ai/`/thumbnail` workflow) and GPT Image 2.
- [ ] Pre-run every test prompt on BOTH models off-camera. Pick the prompts where the creator-relevant difference is clearest. Keep the runs FAIR (same prompt, same intent) - fairness = credibility.
- [ ] Decide your 6 rounds in advance (thumbnails, faces, text-on-image, brand/style, workflow speed, hard infographic). Have the side-by-sides ready to react to.
- [ ] Use your real photo (~/assets/identity/tylerai.png) for the face/likeness round.
- [ ] Clean screen, no secrets/keys, notifications off.

## Honesty rules (this is the brand)
- Genuinely concede the rounds GPT wins (text, fact-checked infographics). The honest losses are what make the Nano Banana verdict believable - and they differentiate you from the hype crowd.
- Don't rig prompts to favor Nano Banana. Judge on real creator value.

## Retention
- Clear round resets ("Round 3: text on images. Watch.").
- Zoom into the ONE detail that decides each round - don't narrate the whole image.
- Speed-ramp generations. Cut dead air.
- Proof-first cold open (the "everyone says GPT won" framing), value fast.

## PACKAGING (the breakthrough lever - do not skimp)
- Thumbnail: use REAL outputs from the video as side-by-side proof (see titles.md concepts A/B/C). 3-4 words max ("STILL #1" / "NOT DETHRONED"). Your reaction face. Generate via `/thumbnail`.
- Make 2-3 thumbnails and run YouTube **Test & Compare** - let watch-time pick the winner.
- Title: launch with the broad verdict, A/B the drama + question variants.

## Post
- `/chapters` on the final cut (one chapter per round - helps the showdown format).
- `/seo` to finalize title/description/tags.
- Upload + schedule via `/youtube`; save video_id to youtube_video_id.txt.
- Track CTR/retention in performance.md; this is the breakthrough test, so watch day-1 CTR closely and swap thumbnails via Test & Compare.
