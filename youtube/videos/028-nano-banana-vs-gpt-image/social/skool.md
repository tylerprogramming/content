Everyone kept telling me GPT Image 2 finally dethroned Nano Banana, so it was time to switch. I make thumbnails and social content every week, so before I changed anything, I tested it for real.

Not on random art. On the stuff creators actually make: YouTube thumbnails, faces, text on images, brand consistency across a carousel, and speed inside my real Claude Code workflow. Three models, same briefs: Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2, and the cheap wildcard, Seedream 4.5 on Kie.ai.

Here's the honest verdict. GPT Image 2 genuinely wins on dense text and fact-checked infographics. Headlines on an image, a quote graphic, an infographic that has to get the facts right. If that's your job, use it.

But for what creators do, Nano Banana Pro is still the best, and it's not close. Better thumbnails out of the box, more consistent faces, a nicer aesthetic across a whole brand set. And wired into Claude Code, I generate these in seconds inside my content system.

The surprise was Seedream 4.5. Not the best, but good enough for fast thumbnails at a fraction of the price. If you're cranking out volume, that's the value pick.

So it's less about one winner and more about using the right model for the job. I dropped the exact prompts and the thumbnail skill I use in here.
