**Reply 1:**
I didn't test them on architecture renders or alphabet grids like everyone else. I tested the stuff creators actually make: thumbnails, faces, text on images, brand consistency, and speed inside my real workflow.

**Reply 2:**
Round 1 was YouTube thumbnails, out of the box, same brief. Nano Banana Pro gave me a usable, punchy thumbnail. GPT Image 2 was fine but flatter. Seedream 4.5, the cheap wildcard, got closer than I expected.

**Reply 3:**
Here's where I'll be honest. GPT Image 2 genuinely wins on text. Quote graphics, a headline on an image, a dense infographic with correct facts. If that's your job, use it. I'm not going to pretend otherwise.

**Reply 4:**
But for faces, brand style across a carousel, and great-looking images fast, Nano Banana Pro is still the best. And when I wire it into Claude Code, I generate thumbnails in seconds inside my content system.

**Reply 5:**
The surprise was Seedream 4.5 on Kie.ai. Not the best, but good enough for fast thumbnails at a fraction of the price. If you're cranking out volume, that's the value play.

**Reply 6 (CTA):**
So: GPT Image 2 for dense text, Seedream for cheap volume, Nano Banana Pro for creators. Full test, category by category: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h5VtHSXY8Hc
