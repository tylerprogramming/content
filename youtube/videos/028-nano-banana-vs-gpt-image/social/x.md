## POST 1 - Thread (the six-round test)

**Main tweet:**
Everyone said GPT Image 2 dethroned Nano Banana.

So I ran all three models through the work I actually repeat every week instead of the usual demo prompts. Six rounds. Here's how it went:

**Reply 1:**
Round 1 was thumbnails, same brief, no extra prompting. Nano Banana Pro handed me one I could ship. GPT Image 2 was clean but flat. Seedream 4.5 got closer than I expected.

**Reply 2:**
Round 3 was text on an image, and GPT Image 2 wins that outright. Headlines, quote graphics, an infographic that has to get its facts right. If that's your work, use it.

**Reply 3:**
Faces and brand consistency went the other way. Nano Banana Pro held the same person and the same look across a whole set. That's the part that usually breaks first for me.

**Reply 4:**
Seedream 4.5 was the surprise. Not the best at anything. Good enough at most of it for a fraction of the price. If you're making volume, that's the one.

**Reply 5:**
The takeaway wasn't a winner. It was that I use a different model depending on what I'm making, and I only know which because I tested them on my own work.
