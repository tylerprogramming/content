## Email Body (paste into Resend)

```
Hey [first_name],

Want to show you the one part of this week's video that changes how I think about picking an image model.

Most of these comparisons stop at "which one draws better." I care about something else. How fast can I go from an idea to a finished thumbnail without leaving my content system.

So here's the part that actually matters for creators.

I wired Nano Banana Pro into Claude Code. Now I don't open a separate app, paste a prompt into a website, wait, download, rename, and drag the file around. I just ask for the thumbnail inside the workflow I'm already in, and it shows up in seconds.

That's the moment the "which model is best" question changes. Because a model that's slightly better on a chart but lives in a slow website loses to a slightly less perfect model that lives inside your system.

So here's the 5-minute exercise. Look at your own thumbnail process and count the steps:

1. Open the image tool
2. Paste the prompt
3. Wait and download
4. Rename and move the file into your project

Every one of those is friction. The win isn't a better render. It's cutting those steps to one.

In the video I show the head to head results, but I also show the workflow, so you can see why the fast path beats the "best on paper" path for anyone shipping content on a schedule.

Here's the video if you want to jump to the workflow part:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h5VtHSXY8Hc

If you try it, reply and tell me how many steps you cut. I'll share the best setups next week.

- Tyler

https://www.skool.com/the-ai-agency
```
