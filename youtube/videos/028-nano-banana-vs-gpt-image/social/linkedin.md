Everyone told me to switch to GPT Image 2.

The headlines all said the same thing. GPT Image 2 finally dethroned Nano Banana. Time to move on.

I make YouTube thumbnails and social content with AI every single week. So before I switched anything, I actually tested it.

Not on random art. Not on architecture renders or alphabet grids like most of these comparison videos.

I tested all three on the stuff creators really make.

Thumbnails. Faces and likeness. Text on an image. Brand consistency across a carousel. And speed inside my real Claude Code workflow.

Here's what I found.

For dense text and fact-checked infographics, GPT Image 2 genuinely wins. Headlines on an image, a quote graphic, an infographic that has to get the facts right. If that's your job, use it. I'm not going to pretend it doesn't.

But for what creators actually do, Nano Banana Pro is still the best, and it's not close.

Better thumbnails out of the box. More consistent faces. A nicer aesthetic across a whole brand set. And when I wire it into Claude Code, I generate these in seconds inside my content system.

The surprise was the cheap one. Seedream 4.5 on Kie.ai got close enough for fast thumbnails at a fraction of the price. If you're cranking out volume, that's a real value pick.

So the honest answer is not that one model wins. It's use the right one for the job.

The dethroned headlines are judging these on the wrong stuff. For creators, the winner didn't change.
