## LinkedIn
I save 10 plus hours a week with Claude Cowork. Here is what it does for me every day.

Claude Cowork is not a chatbot. It runs on your computer, connects to the tools you already use, and executes tasks while you do other things.

Every morning it triages my Gmail inbox. Urgent emails get summarized. Replies get drafted inside Gmail for me to send or discard. The rest gets filed. Thirty minutes I used to lose before my first meeting, gone.

It runs my competitor research for me. I give it a topic, it pulls data from the web, cross-references sources, writes a summary document to Drive. Two hours per research session, gone.

Every Friday it organizes my downloads folder. Invoices go to a finance folder. Receipts go to another. Drafts get archived. One hour a week of housekeeping, gone.

Every morning it builds a briefing document — today's calendar, top emails that need responses, priorities for the day — before I even open my laptop. Thirty minutes a day, gone.

Add it up and it is well over 10 hours a week. That is most of a workday reclaimed. And the cost is whatever my Anthropic subscription already was. No new tool, no new subscription, just connectors you turn on and a schedule.

If you are still doing this work manually, you are paying the opportunity cost every single day.
