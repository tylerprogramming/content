## LinkedIn
A YouTuber with 150K plus subscribers hired me to automate her content pipeline. Here is one of the routines I built her.

Every Thursday at 8am, a Claude routine runs in the cloud. It connects to Apify and Slack. It scrapes 30 Instagram posts for each of her target hashtags. It analyzes what is working, what formats are dominating the feed, and where her content has gaps. Then it drafts her next Instagram carousel with the full structure, slide one through six, caption included.

The full report lands in her Slack channel before she opens her laptop on Thursday morning.

Her old workflow was three hours of manual research every week. Now it is 15 minutes of review, and the research is better than what a human assistant was giving her.

This is the difference between creators who post consistently and creators who burn out. The consistent ones are not working harder. They have a system that runs while they sleep.
