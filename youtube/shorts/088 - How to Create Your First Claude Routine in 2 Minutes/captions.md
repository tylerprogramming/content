## LinkedIn
Claude routines are one of the most powerful features Anthropic has shipped, and almost nobody is using them yet. Here is how to build your first one in under two minutes.

Go to claude.ai. On the left, click Code. At the top, click Routines, then New Routine. Name it Email Summary. Paste a prompt that tells it what to do. Pick a trigger, daily at 6am works well. Add the Gmail connector. Click Create.

That is the whole setup. Hit Run Now to test it. It pulls your inbox, flags what needs your attention, drafts replies, and summarizes the rest.

One of my clients was spending 10,000 a month on virtual assistants doing exactly this kind of work. I replaced them with routines and skills. They kept over 9,000 a month in their business and got their time back.

You do not need to hire a VA for tasks a scheduled prompt can do. Build the routine once. It runs forever.
