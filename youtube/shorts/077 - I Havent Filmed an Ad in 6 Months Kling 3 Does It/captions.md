## LinkedIn
I have not filmed an ad in 6 months. Kling 3 does it all now.

Here is the workflow. Step one, Nano Banana Pro generates the person. Different age, gender, ethnicity, wardrobe, whatever the brief calls for. Step two, I drop that image into Kling 3 as the start frame and prompt the action. Talking to camera, taking a sip, unboxing, scrolling a phone, whatever sells the product. Step three, Kling handles audio natively or I pipe it through ElevenLabs for a cloned voice.

Output is a 10 second clip that looks like a real creator read. Multiple variations per hour.

The economics used to be brutal. A human UGC creator charged $500 per take and took 5 days. You got one take. If it did not perform, you ran it again, paid again, waited again.

Now I generate 10 variations in an hour for about $5 in API credits. I can test hooks, demographics, voice tones, and product angles in a single afternoon — more variation in one day than a brand used to get in a quarter.

The creators who adapt to this workflow have a testing velocity advantage that compounds. The brands that adapt to it reclaim their ad creative budget.
