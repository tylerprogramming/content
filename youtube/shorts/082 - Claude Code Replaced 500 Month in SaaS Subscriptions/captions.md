## LinkedIn
Claude Code replaced $500 a month in SaaS subscriptions for me. Here is what I cancelled and the skill that replaced each.

Canva Pro. $15 a month. Replaced by /instagram-writer plus Nano Banana. Carousels, covers, and social graphics in seconds.

Submagic. $30 a month. Replaced by the /submagic skill which burns the same word-by-word captions via API.

Notion AI. $20 a month. Replaced by Claude Code's memory system plus targeted skills for writing and summarization.

Grammarly plus Jasper plus Copy.ai. $150 a month combined. Replaced by the /content skill which writes platform-native posts in my voice.

A research SaaS plus a trend tool plus a hashtag generator. $90 a month combined. Replaced by /yt-search which pulls top performers and identifies content gaps.

Buffer plus Hypefury plus the other scheduler. $120 a month combined. Replaced by Blotato which schedules every platform from one calendar.

Total cancelled. Over $500 a month. Over $6,000 a year I kept in my business.

The broader point. SaaS stacks were designed for an era when AI could not write, design, schedule, or reason. Most of the tools you pay for today are UI wrappers on capabilities Claude Code now handles natively with a 50 line skill. Audit your subscriptions. Cancel the ones that can be replaced by a markdown file.
