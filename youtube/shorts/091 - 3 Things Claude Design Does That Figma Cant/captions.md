## LinkedIn
Three things Claude Design does that Figma literally cannot.

One. Prompt to full UI in one shot. Figma starts with a blank frame. Claude Design starts with a finished page. You describe what you want, you get back a production-ready layout in seconds. The blank-canvas tax is gone.

Two. Iterate in plain English. No layers panel, no auto-layout constraints, no component instances to manage. You tell it what to change and it changes. Make the buttons bigger. Swap the hero image. Add a dark mode. Every edit is a sentence, not a manual manipulation.

Three. Export working production code. Figma gives you a PNG or a dev handoff link. Claude Design gives you actual HTML and Tailwind. You copy, paste, deploy.

The implications are not subtle. The design-to-dev handoff has collapsed. The blank-canvas bottleneck has collapsed. The iteration speed has collapsed.

If your team still treats UI design as a multi-day, multi-tool process, you are competing with teams that treat it as a multi-minute conversation.
