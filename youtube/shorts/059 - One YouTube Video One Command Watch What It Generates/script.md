## Short #3: "One YouTube Video. One Command. Watch What It Generates."

**Format:** Screen recording (drop the talking head intro)
**Target length:** 55-65 seconds
**Post day:** Saturday April 18

### Hook (first 3 seconds)
"One YouTube video. One command. Watch what it generates."

### Script
One YouTube video. One command. Watch what it generates.

I type /repurpose into Claude Code with my video script.

It reads the whole thing, finds the five strongest moments, and starts generating.

Short-form video scripts for TikTok and Reels - each one with its own hook and its own arc, not just a clip of the original.

LinkedIn posts. X posts. Instagram captions.

And here's the part that surprises people every time.

Every piece is platform-native. The LinkedIn post teaches something. The X post is a punchy standalone insight. The shorts don't reference the original video at all.

[SHOW: scroll through all the generated files]

Keep scrolling.

Five shorts. Five LinkedIn posts. Five tweets. Five captions. A community post.

That's 30 pieces of content from one video. Every week. Five minutes.
