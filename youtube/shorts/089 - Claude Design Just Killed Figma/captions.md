## LinkedIn
Figma just got obsoleted by Claude Design, and most designers have not caught up yet.

I opened Claude Design. Typed one prompt. Build me a SaaS landing page for a fitness tracking app. Dark theme. Three pricing tiers. Hero section with a phone mockup.

30 seconds later I had a full landing page. Hero, pricing, features, footer. Production ready.

Then I iterated in plain English. Make the pricing cards terracotta. Done. Add a testimonials section with four quotes. Done. No layers, no frames, no design system file.

Then I exported the code. Real HTML and Tailwind, ready to deploy.

For most product work, this eliminates the entire design-to-dev handoff. Founders can now skip the Figma phase entirely and go from idea to shippable UI in minutes.

If you are still opening Figma for marketing pages, landing pages, or MVP UI, you are working in the old world.
