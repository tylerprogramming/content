## LinkedIn
I schedule a full week of content — 32 plus pieces — in 5 minutes. Zero manual work.

Here is the system.

Monday morning, I open Claude Code and run one command. The skill pulls my upcoming YouTube videos and shorts from the week. It generates all the text posts — X, LinkedIn, Instagram captions, YouTube Community. Each one is rewritten for that platform's voice, length, and engagement patterns.

Then it pushes everything to Blotato with scheduled times. Monday through Sunday. Each platform at its best-performing slot based on my analytics.

The weekly output is consistent. 2 YouTube long-forms, 5 YouTube Shorts, 5 TikToks, 5 Instagram Reels, 7 LinkedIn posts, 2 Instagram carousels, 2 YouTube Community posts. 32 pieces total, queued and ready.

5 minutes of my time. I used to spend most of Saturday on this — writing captions, rewriting for each platform, manually scheduling in Blotato or a sheet.

If your content cadence is held back by publishing labor, automate the scheduler first. The writing matters, but the scheduler is what actually reclaims your weekend.
