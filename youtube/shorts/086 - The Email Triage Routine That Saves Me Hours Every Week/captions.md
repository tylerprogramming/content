## LinkedIn
I built hundreds of AI automations for Fortune 500 companies and YouTubers with 150K plus subscribers. The one channel everyone checks, every single day, is email.

So I built a Claude routine for it.

It runs every weekday at 8am. It connects to Gmail. It scans unread emails from the last 24 hours and categorizes them into urgent, action needed, and informational. Then it drafts replies directly inside my inbox. I open Gmail, skim the summary, and hit send or delete. That is it.

The entire process takes me under five minutes. Before this I was spending 30 to 45 minutes every morning reading, sorting, and replying.

If you are a founder, creator, or operator, this is the single highest leverage routine you can build. Your inbox is the first thing the day fights you for. Automate the triage and you get that hour back before anyone else is even online.
