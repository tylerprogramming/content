## LinkedIn
3 tools. 100 plus pieces of content a week. This is the entire stack.

Tool one is Claude Code. It is the brain. I have 25 skills running everything from YouTube research to script writing to caption generation to platform rewriting. One command fires the right skill for the job.

Tool two is Nano Banana. It is the visual engine. Every thumbnail, carousel background, UGC character, product shot, and platform graphic. The one tool replaced Midjourney, DALL-E, Photoshop, and Canva for me.

Tool three is Blotato. It is the distribution layer. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads. Everything scheduled from one calendar with platform-native formatting.

No Canva. No Notion. No Buffer. No freelancers. No design subscription, no scheduler subscription, no project management tool for content ops.

Three tools, one person, 100 plus pieces of output a week. The stack I was running two years ago had 10 tools and three contractors. The new math is not just cheaper — it is faster, more consistent, and more scalable.

For founders and creators, the lesson is that the number of tools you stitch together is usually a lagging indicator of operational debt. Every tool you remove without losing capability is money and time reclaimed.
