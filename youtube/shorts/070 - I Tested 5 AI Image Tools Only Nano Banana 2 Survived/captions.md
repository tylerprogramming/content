## LinkedIn
I tested 5 AI image tools head to head with the same prompt. Only one survived.

The prompt was a professional woman in her late 20s, natural lighting, holding a white coffee mug, UGC selfie angle.

Midjourney produced beautiful images but is not controllable enough for likeness work where you need the same person across 10 variations. DALL-E 3 gave me plastic faces across the board. Flux got close but the lighting fell apart at 2K resolution. Seedream 4.5 is fast and cheap but struggled with hands.

Nano Banana 2 won decisively. Likeness consistency across generations, prompt adherence at the detail level, photorealistic lighting, and the ability to supply up to 14 reference images so the model knows what you mean. 2 and a half cents per generation.
