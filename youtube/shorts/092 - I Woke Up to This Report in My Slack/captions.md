I woke up this morning, opened Slack, and a full TikTok trend report was waiting for me. Top 8 performing Claude TikToks by views. Best hook patterns of the week. Priority content directions. Delivered at 9am on a Monday before I was awake.

My computer was off. A Claude routine did it in the cloud.

Here is the setup. A routine is a scheduled prompt that runs on Anthropic's infrastructure. It has connectors — Gmail, Slack, Apify, Calendar, Linear. Every Monday at 9am, my TikTok Trends routine fires. Apify scrapes 30 Claude-related TikToks. Claude analyzes what is working, identifies dominant hook patterns, and drafts a priority content plan. Slack receives the full report.

I used to spend 3 hours every Monday morning doing this manually. Research, tagging, summarizing, writing the plan. Now it is done before I have coffee and the output is more rigorous than what I produced by hand.

This is what autonomous agents actually look like in practice. Not a chatbot. Not a demo. A scheduled prompt with connectors and an output channel, running while you sleep.
