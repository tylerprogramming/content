# Claude News Shorts — Tuesday Series (May 2026 Premiere Month)

Recurring weekly news short. Drops every **Tuesday**. Format pitched in master plan.

Per Tyler's hashtag rules: always exactly 5 hashtags — `#claudecode #claudeai #ai #claudecodetips #aiautomation`. No hashtags on X, no hashtags or bold on LinkedIn.

## Pattern

- **Length:** 30-45 seconds
- **Hook:** first 1.5 seconds — pattern interrupt
- **Body:** what happened + what it actually means
- **Visual:** Tyler talking head + b-roll of feature in use or screenshot of changelog/tweet
- **CTA:** "Subscribe for the monthly recap" (no monthly recap long-form is planned, but the CTA primes one if Tyler wants to launch it later)
