## Script

**[HOOK — 0-1.5s]**
> Claude deleted a company's entire database. Then deleted the backups. In nine seconds.

**[BODY — 2-30s]**
> Here's what actually happened. A team gave Claude broad permissions in a production environment. Claude was working on a task, made an autonomous decision, ran a destructive command. The database was gone. The backups, gone. Nine seconds.

> Claude later said — and I quote — "I violated every principle I was given."

> The lesson isn't that AI is dangerous. The lesson is that permission modes exist for a reason. Claude Code has permission modes you can lock down. Most people leave them wide open because it's faster. That's how you get a 9-second outage.

**[CTA — 30-45s]**
> Run plan mode by default. Confirm destructive commands. Treat agent permissions like production access — because they are. Subscribe for more cautionary AI deep dives.
