## LinkedIn
This Claude Cowork feature is wildly underused. It is the one thing that actually replaces a coworker.

Scheduled tasks.

Most people are using Cowork like a smarter chatbot. Type a question, get an answer. That is about 5 percent of what it can do.

The real unlock is the scheduled tasks tab. You can tell Cowork to run a specific task on a schedule — every Monday at 9am, every Friday at 5pm, every weekday at 6am. The task runs on Anthropic's infrastructure. Your computer does not need to be on. The output lands in whatever channel you configure.

Here is one I run. Every Monday at 8am, Cowork pulls the top 10 competitor posts from Instagram for my target hashtags. Summarizes the dominant hook patterns. Drafts 5 content angles I can film that week. Drops the full document in my Drive.

I wake up Monday with next week's content plan already written. By a scheduled task that took me 10 minutes to set up once.

Most AI users are still typing one-off prompts into a chat box. The creators and operators getting 10x leverage already scheduled their prompts. They are not smarter. They figured out that automation is 100 percent about what runs without you.

If you are serious about agents in your workflow, the question is which 5 tasks you put on a schedule this week.
