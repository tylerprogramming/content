## LinkedIn
I replaced my entire content team with 25 Claude Code skills.

Before. I was paying a writer $2,000 a month. A graphic designer $1,500. A thumbnail designer $800. A video editor assistant $600. A social media scheduler $400. Total content labor was $5,300 a month, or roughly $64K a year.

After. I built 25 skills in Claude Code. /yt plans my YouTube videos from research to script. /yt-search pulls top performers and identifies content gaps. /thumbnail generates branded thumbnails in Nano Banana. /content writes every platform post tuned to voice and length. /instagram-writer builds 6-slide carousels with backgrounds. /shorts creates full weekly short-form packages. /submagic burns word-by-word captions. /chapters timestamps final videos from transcripts. /seo optimizes titles with competitive research. /analytics tracks everything.

Every role I used to pay for is now a markdown file. 50 to 150 lines of instructions per skill.

Same output. Better voice, because every skill speaks in mine by default. And the cost dropped about 95 percent — the only variable cost now is API credits, which come to maybe $50 a month across all 25.

The question is not whether this is the direction. The question is which role in your content team you automate this week.
