# Hook Options: I Built an AI Video Editor With Claude Code

**Date:** 2026-06-19
**Rule:** First 20-30 seconds delivered WORD FOR WORD from a teleprompter. The lead hook opens ON the result, then reveals "I built the thing that did this." Keep it honest - never imply built from scratch.

---

## Hook 1: Open On The Result (LEAD - recommended)

> [COLD OPEN - no talking yet. On screen: a raw talking-head clip plays for 2-3 seconds, full of awkward pauses and dead air. Then a hard cut to the SAME clip, tight, no dead air, with bold karaoke captions popping word by word. Let it play for 4-5 seconds so the viewer feels the difference.]
>
> [Tyler, to camera] That clip you just watched? The before was three minutes. The after was one minute and fifty seconds. I did not touch a timeline. I did not drag a single clip. I ran one command, and the dead air was gone. Then I ran one more, and the captions were on. And here is the part nobody else can say - I built the thing that did that. I built my own AI video editor with Claude Code, and in the next ten minutes I am going to show you exactly how it works, edit a real clip with it live, and tell you honestly what is mine and what is just good open-source tools doing the heavy lifting. Let's get into it.

**Why it works:** It rides the exact cold open that made the 276K lane leader win - the result first, the "AI did this" promise in the first three seconds. Then it immediately out-depths the entire lane with the line none of them can use: "I built the thing that did that." The honesty teaser ("what is mine and what is open-source") pre-empts the overclaim and sets the trust tone Tyler's brand runs on. Showing the before-and-after clip live is the verifiable proof, not a claim.

---

## Hook 2: The Cost-Anxiety Open (rides the "replaced my editor" cluster)

> Every creator I know hates the same two parts of editing. Cutting out all the dead air, every um, every pause, every awkward silence between sentences. And then sitting there adding captions word by word. It is the most tedious, soul-crushing, time-eating part of making a video, and it is the part you cannot skip. So I did something about it. I used Claude Code to build my own little AI video editor on top of some open-source tools, and it does exactly those two things - kills the dead air and drops on captions - in about two seconds each. I am going to edit a real clip with it right now, live, so you can see it actually work. No faked outputs. Let's go.

**Why it works:** Opens on the universal pain (dead air plus captions) that the "replaced my editor" cluster taps, then pivots to the build. "No faked outputs" is a direct trust signal that separates Tyler from the hype videos. Honest from the first breath ("on top of some open-source tools").

---

## Hook 3: The Reveal Open (builder-first, curiosity gap)

> I am going to say a sentence that sounds like a lie, and then I am going to prove it is true on camera. I built my own AI video editor. Not bought one, not subscribed to one - built one, with Claude Code, in an afternoon, on top of open-source tools. And it does the one thing I hate most about editing better than I do it by hand. Watch. [SHOW: run the silence command, 3:00 to 1:50 in 2 seconds] Three minutes of footage, one minute fifty after, seventy-one seconds of dead air gone, in two seconds. That is real. Let me show you how it works and exactly how you could build the same thing.

**Why it works:** "Sounds like a lie, then I prove it" is a strong pattern interrupt that creates an open loop the viewer needs closed. The immediate live proof (run the command in the first 25 seconds) pays the loop off fast, which protects retention. "Exactly how you could build the same thing" plants the reproducible-path promise that converts viewers into builders.

---

## Hook 4: The Honest Contrarian Open

> Everybody is making the same video right now. "AI replaced my video editor." And honestly? Most of those videos are overselling it. AI cannot pace your story, it cannot pick what matters, it cannot choose your b-roll. So I am not going to tell you AI replaced my editor. I am going to tell you the truth - AI is incredible at the boring mechanical parts of editing, and useless at the creative parts. So I built a tool with Claude Code that does just the boring parts. It cuts dead air and adds captions, in seconds, and it does it well. I am going to edit a real clip with it right now and show you exactly where it shines and where it falls apart. That is the honest version of this video.

**Why it works:** Directly calls out the lane's overclaiming, which is a credibility move that makes Tyler the trustworthy voice in a hyped category. The "boring parts yes, creative parts no" framing is the honest thesis of the whole video, stated up front. Riskier hook (it questions the lane) but the strongest trust play, and it sets up the honest-limitations section as a payoff rather than a letdown.

---

## Recommended Pick

**Hook 1 (Open On The Result)** is the recommended lead. It rides the proven cold open that already won the lane, delivers the uncopyable "I built it" reveal in the first 20 seconds, and bakes in the honesty teaser that protects the brand. It is the cleanest expression of the whole video in 25 seconds.

**Backups by goal:**
- If you want to compete head-on with the "replaced my editor" cluster: **Hook 2** (cost-anxiety open).
- If browse CTR needs a sharper curiosity gap: **Hook 3** (sounds-like-a-lie reveal).
- If you want to lead with trust and own the honest lane: **Hook 4** (honest contrarian).
