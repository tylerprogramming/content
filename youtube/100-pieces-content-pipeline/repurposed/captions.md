## Caption 2 (for Short 2 - The Centaur Model)

Most AI content is slop. Here's the specific reason why - and how to fix it.

The centaur model: human thinking + machine formatting.

When AI decides what to say, you get generic content. When AI only handles the formatting of what you've already said, you get platform-native posts that still sound like you.

The AI never writes my ideas. It takes what I already said on camera and reformats it for each platform - LinkedIn's style, Instagram's visual structure, X's character limit.

That's the line. Keep your thinking. Outsource the reformatting.
