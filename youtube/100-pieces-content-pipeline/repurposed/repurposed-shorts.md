## Short 2: The Centaur Model

**Title:** The real reason AI content doesn't have to be slop

**Source Section:** Addressing AI Slop (20:00 - 22:00)

**Format:** Talking head

**Hook (first 3 seconds):**
"Most AI content is garbage. Here's why mine isn't."

---

**Script:**

Most AI content is garbage. And there's a specific reason why.

The people making bad AI content are letting AI do the thinking. What should I post about? What's my take? What angle should I use?

That's where slop comes from. AI deciding what matters.

Here's what I do differently. I use something called the centaur model. Half human, half machine.

The human half - that's me - decides what topic matters, what my actual take is, what stories I tell on camera.

The machine half handles reformatting. Taking what I already said and putting it into the right shape for LinkedIn, or Instagram, or X.

The AI never decides what I say. It decides how to format what I've already said.

And people can tell the difference. There's data on this. Sixty percent of people used to prefer AI content. Now it's dropped to twenty-six. Because AI is doing too much of the thinking.

Give AI the reformatting job. Keep the thinking for yourself. That's the whole secret.
