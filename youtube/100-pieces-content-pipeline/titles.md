# Title Options

## 1. How I Create 100+ Pieces of Content Per Week with AI ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **Why it works:** Specific number (100+) is higher than any competitor claim (most say 50+). "Per week" adds urgency. "With AI" is broad enough to attract non-developers.
- **Character count:** 53

## 2. I Make 100+ Posts a Week Working Full-Time (Here's How)
- **Why it works:** The "working full-time" qualifier is the real hook. Every creator watching this has a day job or limited time. Parenthetical creates curiosity gap.
- **Character count:** 56

## 3. My AI Content System: 100+ Posts Per Week in 3 Hours
- **Why it works:** The contrast between 100+ posts and 3 hours is the scroll-stopper. "System" signals this is repeatable, not a one-off hack.
- **Character count:** 53

## 4. The AI Pipeline That Creates All My Content For Me
- **Why it works:** "For me" implies full automation. "Pipeline" signals this is engineered, not thrown together. Shorter title performs well on mobile.
- **Character count:** 51

## 5. How One Video Becomes 100 Pieces of Content (Full System)
- **Why it works:** "One video becomes 100" is a concrete transformation the viewer can picture. "Full system" promises completeness. Mirrors the proven "1 video = X pieces" format but 2x the standard claim.
- **Character count:** 60
