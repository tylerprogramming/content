# Cohort Launch Playbook (Harut-derived)
The full launch runbook for the paid implementation cohort (Months 2-5). Sourced from a /harut session, June 2026. Conversion-sensitive — re-run final copy/pricing through /harut before going live.

Companion to `free-community-plan.md` (strategy) and `month1-modules.md` (the free Month 1 that feeds this).

---

## THE CORE PRINCIPLE (why this works)

Give the information away free; charge for **implementation, personalization, and access**. (Harut 06:49:49.) The free Month 1 is the "give away" that proves you can help. People who finish it and want it done *with* them become cohort buyers. You are NOT selling locked videos — you're selling help.

The free community is the lead magnet and the conversion engine, not a separate thing. (Harut 11:36:33 → 11:41:25.)

---

## WHAT THE COHORT ACTUALLY IS

A time-gated **4-month implementation cohort** (Months 2-5). Members pay for:
- Weekly live training
- Hot-seat content reviews (their actual channel/work)
- A weekly Claude automation drop (scripts they customize)
- Private cohort channel
- ~2 1:1 reviews per member across the program
- **Limited seats (30-50)** — protects outcomes + creates scarcity

Months unlock on a time-drip (M3 unlocks 30 days in, M4 at 60, etc.) = anti binge-and-cancel. (This is the real reason the reference community gates "Unlocks after Month X.")

---

## MEMBERSHIP QUESTIONS (filter on join — even for FREE)

Only accept ideal avatars; reject the rest. Captures first-party data + keeps culture high. (Harut 11:41:19 → 11:46:00.) Ask on join:
1. Where did you find me? (platform — tells you which content makes buyers)
2. What's your niche / what do you do?
3. Audience size and/or current monthly revenue
4. Your main channels
5. Your #1 goal in the next 90 days
6. Biggest blocker right now
7. Email (+ phone if allowed)

## THE 4-WEEK CONVERSION SEQUENCE (run inside the free community)

The launch isn't a lock icon — it's an **event**. (Harut 11:58:57 → 12:03:13.)

- **Week 1 — Deliver the win.** Member does the Month 1 Starter Path. Send 1 automated onboarding DM showing how to use the starter kit. Goal: fast first win + data capture. (Harut 03:36:57.)
- **Week 2 — Live workshop.** Run "Automate Your Next 5 Videos with Claude Code." Record it, post to classroom. Pure value, no hard pitch yet.
- **Week 3 — Proof + offer.** Showcase 3-5 early member wins (case studies). Run a live Q&A + a short VSL for the cohort inside the classroom. People convert after seeing results + getting help. (Harut 11:36:33.)
- **Week 4 — Open seats.** Open the cohort with limited seats + fast-action bonuses (a 1:1 audit spot, a custom Claude script). Follow up via DM + email + calendar invites. (Harut 11:58:57.)

Conversion mechanism stack: classroom + calendar/events + DM + VSL + application form.

## PRICING (Harut ranges — confirm via /harut before launch)

Principle: "sell to the rich" for better LTV and faster scale — position as **implementation, not content**. (Harut 13:46:34.)

- **Flagship cohort (high-touch, limited seats):** ~$997-$2,497 one-time, OR ~$397/mo x 3-6. Recommended start: **$997-$1,497 for the 4-month cohort.**
- **Evergreen lab (low-touch, ongoing):** ~$97-$297/mo — templates, weekly office hours, library, no heavy hand-holding.
- **Hybrid (recommended):** the cheaper monthly tier catches the volume of beginners; the cohort gets better LTV + fast testimonials.

Revenue-shape examples (pick one):
- Volume-heavy recurring: 350 x $297/mo ≈ $103k/mo
- Creator-friendly hybrid: 150 x $197/mo ≈ $29.5k/mo + 40 cohort seats x $1,497 per launch spike

(No dollar amounts in any Skool course/tier/event TITLES — pricing lives in the offer, not the label.)

---

## PROTECT TRUST (don't kill the engagement you're rebuilding)

- **Don't oversell.** "If you sell too much, your community will die." Give, give, give; sell the right amount via high-value events + occasional offers. (Harut 11:58:57 → 12:03:13.)
- **Keep it simple.** "Very few things and good, not many things." Remove noise; measure engagement on a few key assets (starter path, live office hour, hot seats) and cut what isn't used. (Harut 12:03:05 → 12:07:34.)
- **Be transparent.** Label the cohort as an advanced implementation program for creators. No bait-and-switch. Market benefits/outcomes, not "a community." (Harut 11:45:51 → 11:50:25.)

---

## METRICS & GUARDRAILS

- Free → paid conversion: aim **10-20%** on a launch; **14% is Harut's stated average.** (Harut 12:16:27.)
- Track where members came from (onboarding Q1) → double down on platforms that produce BUYERS, not just views. (Harut 03:36:57.)
- Avoid feature bloat — kill assets nobody uses. (Harut 12:03:05.)

## EXAMPLE MESSAGING HOOKS (Harut style — direct, outcome-first)

- Free starter: "Build an automated Claude Code workflow that creates 2 shorts + 1 long-form video per week in under 90 minutes."
- Cohort: "4 months to automate your entire content engine — we'll customize your Claude Code automations for your channel and review every video."

## DO-THIS-WEEK CHECKLIST (when ready to launch)

1. Build the Month 1 Starter Path in Skool + turn on membership questions (capture email, platform, audience size, revenue, goal).
2. Schedule the Week-2 live "Automate Your Next 5 Videos" event + build a short VSL/sales page for the cohort.
3. Pick cohort price + seat count + set the paid classroom gating and an application form. Start: $997-$1,497, 30-50 seats, with urgency.

Sequencing reminder: prove Month 1 lands (completion + feed engagement) BEFORE building all of Months 2-5. Don't build the paid cohort on spec.
