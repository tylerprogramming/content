# Skool Course Filming Schedule

- Each YouTube video gets chopped into Loom-length clips for Skool modules
- Looms are casual, 1-3 min each, talking to camera or screen share
- YouTube videos are 10-15 min, polished, tutorial format
- Film in batches - one session per course

## TIPS
- Film Looms in one batch per course - don't spread across days
- YouTube videos: use your normal setup, these ARE your weekly long-form content
- Looms: casual, screen share, no need for fancy editing
- Upload Looms to Skool as you finish each batch
- Each YouTube video can also go through /repurpose + /content for social posts
