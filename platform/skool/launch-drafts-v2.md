## WHAT THE 14-DAY SHIP CHALLENGE IS

It's the backbone path for every new free member. A simple promise: **"In 14 days, you will have one working AI automation deployed in your business."** Not watched a course. Not learned about AI. Actually running a thing that saves you hours or makes you money.

**Why it matters (Harut's framing):**
- Engineers a specific early win inside the 7-14 day activation window (when new members decide to stay or churn)
- Gives every member a clear path: you know exactly what "winning" looks like
- Generates visible proof on auto-pilot (screenshots + Loom videos from completers become your testimonials)
- Creates the natural upgrade wedge: people who ship their first automation want live help for their next one, which is what Starter and Builder deliver
- Replaces the vague "explore the community" onboarding with a structured 14-day sprint

**Completion = 3 things:**
1. An automation deployed in your actual business
2. A 60-second Loom showing it working
3. A post in the Builds category with your Loom + one metric (hours saved OR revenue impacted)

**What completers get:**
- Named in the weekly Builder Roundup post
- Access to the private Shipped Builders channel
- First month of Builder free (for anyone who ships within 14 days of joining)

### The 14-Day Ship Challenge (day-by-day)

**Days 1-3: Pick + Plan**
- Day 1: Intake (reuse your 3 membership questions; pick ONE outcome: more revenue OR saved time)
- Day 2: Open "AI for Your Business: 5 Automations You Can Deploy Today." Pick the automation that maps to your Day 1 outcome.
- Day 3: Watch the install walkthrough + Claude Code Cowork intro

**Days 4-7: Build**
- Day 4: Install the skill or n8n workflow from the Free Skills Library
- Day 5: Configure it to your data, accounts, and inputs
- Day 6: Test run against real inputs
- Day 7: Mid-challenge checkpoint - post in the Builds category: "What I'm building + one thing I'm stuck on"

**Days 8-11: Deploy + Iterate**
- Day 8: Run the automation for real (first live execution)
- Day 9: Tweak based on output quality
- Day 10: Track the first measurable result
- Day 11: Second run for consistency

**Days 12-14: Ship + Share**
- Day 12: Polish + document what you built
- Day 13: Record a 60-second Loom showing it working end to end
- Day 14: Post completion in the Builds category with Loom + metric. Tag me. I reply to the first 10.

**Tools you'll use (all already in the community):**
- Claude Code + Cowork course: how to install and run skills
- Free Skills Library course: the actual skill templates
- 5 Automations course: picks the right automation for your niche
- Builds category: your public accountability + proof loop

**Tyler operational lift to run this:**
- "Builds" category created in Skool (2 min in UI)
- Automated Day 1, 7, and 14 emails via `/email` drip
- Weekly "Builder Roundup" post (you can draft via `/skool`)
- Update Start Here course to point to the challenge as first action
