## WHY A PILOT AND NOT THE FLAGSHIP

Three facts, as of 2026-08-03:

1. **the-ai-agency has 870 active members and 2 paid seats.** The Starter ($9) / Builder ($27) tiers locked in `STATE.md` (April) have converted ~0.2%. Stacking a third tier on top without proof repeats that.
2. **Month 1 is not built.** `month1-modules.md` is a build sheet: ~10 modules still need recording, 1 of 8 PDFs done. The master plan gates the cohort behind Month 1 landing. That gate is not met.
3. **Live demand appeared anyway.** The DM audit on 2026-08-01 put 8 warm members on a Calendly link. They are asking for help now, by name, with specific blockers.

A pilot resolves this: it converts the demand that exists today, without waiting on Month 1, and produces the testimonials `cohort-launch-playbook.md` says to gather before opening 30-50 seats at $997+.

## THE 6 WEEKS

Promise: **you leave with at least one automation running in your business without you.**

| Wk | Session | They leave with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Setup and your first skill** — install, directory structure, SKILL.md anatomy, watch each person get one firing | One working skill, installed correctly |
| 2 | **Map your work, pick the one that pays** — audit their actual week, find the repeating task, choose by hours-saved not by coolness | A written blueprint + the chosen target |
| 3 | **Build it live (hot seat)** — 3-4 members share screen, build in front of everyone, rest follow along on theirs | Automation v1 running locally |
| 4 | **Connect your tools** — MCP servers, connectors, and where n8n genuinely beats code (and where it doesn't) | Their automation touching real data |
| 5 | **Make it run without you** — scheduling, deterministic vs agentic design, failure modes, what to never automate | It runs on a schedule, unattended |
| 6 | **Ship and show** — demo day, everyone presents, then the path after the cohort | A recorded demo + next 90 days |

**Week 1 is the highest-value hour in the program** and it's the cheapest to deliver. Mark cannot install a Skill. That is not a Mark problem: he finished every n8n training you have and still can't do it. Assume half the cohort is stuck in the same place and fix it live in week one.

**Every week also ships:**
- A recording, posted to the cohort classroom
- One automation drop (a script they customize, not a demo they watch)
- Async questions answered in a private cohort channel
