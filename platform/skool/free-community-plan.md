## STRATEGY DECISIONS (locked this session)

1. **Three separate buckets - stop tangling them:**
   - **Free Skool community** (the-ai-agency, ~813 members) = audience + trust + email + lead magnet. The funnel.
   - **Paid offer = an implementation COHORT** (not locked content). Months 2-5 arc. Charge for help/personalization/accountability, NOT for videos. (Harut 06:49:49.)
   - **SaaS = separate product, separate audience** (only serves people who OWN Skool communities). Does NOT match the broad YouTube/beginner audience, so the free community does NOT funnel to it. Sell the SaaS to Skool owners directly, elsewhere.

2. **Be GENEROUS with free.** Harut: give the content away, charge for implementation. The more loaded free is, the more trust, the more cohort raise-hands. Generosity in free feeds paid, doesn't cannibalize it.

3. **The paid cohort is the answer to "what's the paid offer for the broad audience"** - the gap the SaaS never filled.

4. **Guardrails:** no dollar amounts in course/tier titles; pricing + offer positioning go through `/harut` before launch; confirm before each Skool write.

## THE PAID COHORT (separate layer - sits above free, gated)

NOT locked videos. A time-gated 4-month implementation cohort. Members buy the HELP.

- **Months 2-5 arc** (tailored, Tyler's version):
  - M2: The Content Engine - automate your content workflow (his real /yt pipeline)
  - M3: First Dollar - land your first paying client / monetize
  - M4: Authority - become the go-to in your niche
  - M5: Scale - recurring revenue model
- **Format:** weekly live training + hot-seat content reviews + weekly Claude automation drop (customizable scripts) + private cohort channel + ~2 1:1 reviews/member.
- **Seats:** limited (30-50) for outcomes + scarcity.
- **Time-gate the unlocks** (M3 unlocks 30 days in, etc.) = anti-binge-and-cancel.
- **Pricing (Harut, for /harut review before launch):** flagship cohort ~$997-$1,497 for 4 months; OR lower-touch evergreen lab ~$97-$297/mo; hybrid recommended.
- **DELIVERY-LOAD CAUTION:** a live cohort is real weekly work. Tyler's window is tight (SWE 8-4, family 5:30-9). Size seats + live hours to what he can actually hold.

## CONVERSION PLAYBOOK (Harut - sell without killing trust)

- **Filter on join:** Skool membership questions (where they came from, audience size, monthly revenue, biggest blocker). Reject non-avatars. Captures first-party data. (Harut 11:41:19.)
- **Conversion is an EVENT, not a lock icon:** ~Week 3 run a live "Automate Your Next 5 Videos with Claude Code" workshop, show early member wins, open limited seats. (Harut 11:58:57.)
- **Don't oversell:** give give give, sell occasionally via high-value events. Selling too much kills the community. (Harut 12:03:13.)
- **Benchmark:** ~14% free->paid average; aim 10-20% on a launch. (Harut 12:16:27.)
