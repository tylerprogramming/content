## ONBOARDING CALLS

### $9 Tier: 15-Minute Setup + First-Win Call
**Pre-call form:** Business name, what they do, #1 thing to automate, email
**Script:**
- (1 min) Intro - "This call = one thing you can implement in 48 hours"
- (5 min) Constraint finder - "What's the one activity that makes you money?" + 2 follow-ups
- (6 min) Give 2 concrete quick wins (a template + an automation to try)
- (2 min) Next step - "Do this one thing in 7 days, post results in the community"
**Post-call:** automated email with recording + 3-step checklist + link to upgrade to $47

### $47 Tier: 45-Minute Strategy Audit + 90-Day Plan
**Pre-call form:** Business name, revenue, platform links, top 3 content topics, #1 constraint, dream 90-day outcome
**Script:**
- (3 min) Intro - "You leave with a 90-day plan and your first 3 tasks"
- (10 min) Constraint discovery - dig into their business, find the bottleneck
- (15 min) Walk through their current setup, identify 3 fixes
- (10 min) Build the 90-day plan together
- (7 min) Agree on commitments + schedule first check-in
**Post-call:** Loom recording with their plan + checklist + invite to onboarding thread in Skool
