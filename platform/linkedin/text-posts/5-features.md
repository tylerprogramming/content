5 Claude Code features almost nobody uses. Each one saves me hours every week.

1. Plan Mode

Before you build anything, hit Shift+Tab. Claude shows you the full plan first - what it's going to create, where the files go, the structure. You review it, adjust it, then tell it to execute. Stop letting it just build blind.

2. Compact

Your context window is filling up and Claude starts forgetting things. Type /compact. It clears the conversation but keeps a summary of everything you discussed. You go from 90% context usage back to almost nothing, without losing your place.

3. Session History

You close the terminal and think everything is gone. It's not. Type claude --resume and you get a list of every session you've had. Pick one and continue exactly where you left off. Your work is never lost.

4. Rewind

Claude broke something and you kept hitting enter without checking. Type /rewind. It shows you snapshots from every checkpoint in your session. Pick the one before it went wrong, restore it, and keep going. Undo on steroids.

5. Extended Thinking

Claude can reason through complex problems before responding. It thinks step by step internally, then gives you a better answer. The difference in output quality is noticeable on anything non-trivial.

Most people open Claude Code, type a prompt, and hope for the best. These 5 features are the difference between fighting with it and actually getting work done.
