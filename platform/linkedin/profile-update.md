# LinkedIn Profile Update

Do this before the next posting push. It is a one-time fix that compounds: the headline renders under your name
on **every** impression, and a breakout post sends thousands of people to it. Right now it points them at a job
you left.

**Current headline:**
> 🤖 Teaching 30k+ People How to Create AI Agents to Automate Their Businesses/Lives 💻 Skool.com/the-ai-agency| Software Engineer at JPMorgan Chase & Co. | YouTube Content Creator@TylerReedAI

**Four problems:**
1. Says **Software Engineer at JPMorgan Chase** while the profile's current company shows **Pfizer**. Stale, and
   it reads as careless to anyone who scrolls two inches.
2. The Skool URL burns headline characters. There is a dedicated profile link slot for that (see below), and a
   raw URL is not clickable in the headline anyway.
3. **"Claude Code" appears nowhere.** The headline is indexed by LinkedIn search. That is the term your people
   actually type, and you rank for none of it.
4. Ex-IBM and ex-JPMorgan Chase is real credibility that the platform actively surfaces, and it is currently
   framed as a current job instead of a track record.

---

## Headline options (220 char limit)

**A. Keyword-forward — recommended**
> AI Engineer at Pfizer | I build Claude Code agents that automate real work | Teaching 30k+ people how to do the same | YouTube @TylerReedAI

Leads with the credential, carries "Claude Code" and "agents" for search, keeps the 30k proof.

**B. Proof-forward**
> I run my entire content business on Claude Code agents and show exactly how | AI Engineer at Pfizer | Ex-IBM, Ex-JPMorgan Chase | Teaching 30k+ | YouTube @TylerReedAI

Leads with the thing only you can say. The folder/system framing is already your best-performing frame on
Instagram. Longer, but under the limit.

**C. Plain**
> AI Engineer at Pfizer. I build agents with Claude Code and MCP, and teach 30k+ people how. Ex-IBM, ex-JPMorgan Chase. YouTube @TylerReedAI

Closest to your actual voice. No emoji, no pipes-as-decoration.

All three drop the em dashes and the raw URL, name the current employer correctly, and turn Chase and IBM into
history rather than a stale present tense.

---

## The link slot

Move `skool.com/the-ai-agency` out of the headline and into the profile's website/services link, labeled
something like **"Free AI agent community."**

This is the clean resolution to the whole link problem: readers can always reach the offer, so no post ever needs
a click CTA. Posts close on a comment or DM ask, the profile carries the destination. See rule #7 in
`BRAIN/linkedin/brain.md`.

---

## About section, first two lines

Only the first ~2 lines show before "see more," so they carry the weight:

> I am an AI engineer at Pfizer, and I spent 8 years as a software engineer at IBM and JPMorgan Chase before that.
> These days I build agents with Claude Code that take the repetitive parts of my work off my plate, and I show
> exactly how, step by step.

Then the specifics: the content operation that runs out of one folder of text files, the skills that chain
research through upload, and the 30k+ people learning it. No money amounts, no guru framing.
