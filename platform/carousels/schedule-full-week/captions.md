## Instagram
I schedule an entire week of social media in one Claude Code conversation. Every platform, every post.

Save this if you're tired of manually scheduling content.

Here's how it works: I open Claude Code, tell it what content I have ready for the week, and it pushes everything to Blotato. LinkedIn posts, Instagram carousels, tweets, TikToks, YouTube Shorts, and Reels all get scheduled in one session.

It knows my posting cadence. It knows which platforms get which content. It handles the formatting differences between each one.

One conversation replaces an hour of copying, pasting, and clicking through scheduling tools.
