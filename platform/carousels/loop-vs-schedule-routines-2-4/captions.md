## Instagram

So these two look like the same thing in the terminal, right? They are not.

/schedule creates a routine that runs in the cloud on a timer. It keeps going
whether or not you are at your desk.

/loop only runs while Claude is open and your computer is on. Close it and it
is gone. It was never meant to last, it is for hands-on work in a session.

The test is simple. Shut your laptop. Does it still need to run? Then you
wanted schedule.

This is the one people get wrong, and it is why their automation quietly
stopped.

Save this, and send it to whoever set one up and wondered why it stopped.
