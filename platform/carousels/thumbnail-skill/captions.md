## LinkedIn

Thumbnails used to be my biggest bottleneck.

I'd open Canva, find a background, manually cut out my face, add text, try 5 different layouts, hate all of them, and spend 45 minutes on something that should take 5.

Now I type /thumbnail and describe the concept.

It generates multiple variants through the Kie.ai API. Uses my likeness for brand consistency. Creates text overlays optimized for YouTube CTR. Saves everything locally so I can compare options.

3-4 variants in under a minute. No Photoshop. No designer. No design skills needed.

Thumbnails went from my biggest time sink to a 60-second decision.
