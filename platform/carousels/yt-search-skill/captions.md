## LinkedIn

YouTube research used to eat my entire morning.

Open YouTube. Search manually. Click through 20 videos. Copy titles into a spreadsheet. Check view counts. Try to spot patterns in what's working.

Now I type one command: /yt-search

It searches YouTube by keyword, sorts results by view count, pulls titles and metadata, downloads competitor thumbnails, and saves a full markdown report. 30 seconds.

Every video I plan starts with this. I know exactly what's performing before I write a single word.

The difference between guessing and knowing is one command.
