## What the numbers actually say

**Comments outrun likes on every single post.** On a normal post likes are 5-10x
comments. Here the ratio is inverted, up to 4.4x the other way. That is not
organic conversation. Every one of these posts gates a deliverable behind a
comment:

- brodyautomates: "Comment SKILLS and I'll DM you the PDF with every install command"
- willnelson: "Comment TOOL"
- anay.tech: "Share in my DM"

The comment is the price of the lead magnet. Comments are the heaviest ranking
signal IG has, so the gate buys reach and the DM buys the follow. This is the
single mechanic all four share and the one we are not running.

**Sends are the reach multiplier, and they are a different lever.** theromanknox
got 4,094 sends against 2,429 likes — more people DM'd it to a friend than
liked it. His post is the only one that is *useful without the creator*: five
copy-paste prompts, complete on the slide, nothing gated. willnelson's
comment-gated post got 1,515 comments but only 134 sends, because there is
nothing to forward — the value is behind the gate.

So the two mechanics pull in different directions:

- **Gate it** → comments spike, reach comes from ranking, you capture DMs
- **Give it away complete** → sends spike, reach comes from person-to-person sharing

theromanknox ran both and got the best absolute numbers of the four.

## Format patterns

- **Every post is a carousel with music attached.** theromanknox (TVN),
  brodyautomates (Giulio Cercato, New Horizons). Music on a carousel makes it
  eligible for surfaces reels get, which is the cheap version of "make more
  reels".
- **Slide count 5-8.** Nobody is doing 10+.
- **Persistent footer rail** on every slide: handle, page counter (1/4, 3/5), and
  a swipe affordance. brodyautomates runs a top rail instead (©2026 BRODY
  AUTOMATES / SKILL 03 / THE END).
- **The last slide is always the ask**, and it is a single sentence.
- **Screenshots of the actual product** (willnelson) work — real UI, not mockups.

## Design: brodyautomates is the closest to our existing palette

Worth noting because it is almost a coincidence. His carousel runs cream
(~#EDE8DF) with terra-cotta (~#C4622D) accents, a bold grotesque headline, an
editorial serif italic subhead, a numbered scalloped badge, a dark navy "What
it does" card, and mono type for the technical path (`claude-skill /
viral-reel-generator`).

Our instagram-writer skill and the Carousel Studio defaults are already cream
`#F5F0EB` + terra cotta `#E07355`. The palette is effectively the same. What he
has that we do not:

1. The serif italic subhead against the grotesque headline (two-voice typography)
2. The numbered badge as a persistent device across slides
3. A top rail with issue/series numbering, which makes it feel like a publication
4. A dark card for the dense "what it does" content, so it reads as a unit
5. Hand-drawn spot illustration for warmth
