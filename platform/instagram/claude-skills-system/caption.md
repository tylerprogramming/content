I run my whole content business out of one folder of text files.

Save this. You'll use it.

So a Claude Code skill is not an app or a script. It is a folder with one markdown file in it called SKILL.md. The top of the file says when to use it, and everything under that is just the instructions you would give someone you were training.

Mine break into three groups. Seven research and write the content. Six take one video and turn it into a week of posts. Four handle the email and the community side. A few of them run on a schedule while I am asleep, and everything defaults to draft so nothing goes out without me reading it.

They are not perfect. They get me about 80 percent there and I do the last bit. That is kind of the whole deal with this stuff.

If you want a first one to build, pick the task you did twice this week and write the instructions down.
