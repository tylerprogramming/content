Post 3 - the misconception hook

So if you've ever gotten confused about skills vs MCP, you're not alone. It's the question I get most.

Short version: MCP is the connection to a tool or service. A skill is instructions you write to teach Claude how you want a task done. And plugins just bundle and share them.

I break all of it down and build skills from scratch in the new video: https://youtu.be/9ZsZgnWrs_E
