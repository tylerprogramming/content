## Email 2 - The "it is just a text file" hook

Subject: A Claude Code skill is just a text file
Preview: No SDK. No framework. Let me show you.

Hi {first_name},

So let me clear up the biggest thing people get wrong about Claude Code skills.

Most people assume a skill is some complicated piece of engineering. It is not. A skill is a markdown file. A few lines of setup on top, plain English below telling Claude what to do. No SDK. No framework. No plugin to install. That is really the whole thing.

I run my whole content business on three of them, and each one is maybe 50 to 100 lines of English.

So in my latest video I did something I had been meaning to do for a while. Instead of only showing the polished ones, I built a brand new skill from scratch, on camera, no edits. It is a personal people tracker called peeps. It remembers the people in your life, names, birthdays, how you know them, notes, and gives you a clean page to search them.

Start to finish, a few minutes. You describe what you want. You save the file. You test it. Done.

Here is the thing though. The moment you see how simple that file really is, you stop asking "how do I automate this" and start asking "what do I call the skill and what should it say." And that little shift is kind of the whole game.

So watch me build one from nothing, then go build yours.
