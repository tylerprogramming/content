So Claude Routines launched and my whole feed filled up with the same take. "Routines killed n8n."

Here is the thing. A Routine is just Claude Code running in the cloud, on Anthropic's servers. Not on your laptop. So you can close the lid, go to sleep, and it still runs. You give it a schedule, you connect a few things like Gmail or Slack or Apify, and it does the work while you are gone.

- A skill tells Claude what steps to take.
- A Routine tells Claude what steps to take AND when to do it, without you there.
- Claude Code is when you want to sit next to it and drive.

Email 2 - The VA math

Subject: The task eating your mornings is a Routine, not a hire
Preview: 45 minutes a day. 195 hours a year. Handed off.

Hi {first_name},

So let me give you the little bit of math I run before I automate anything.

A virtual assistant is somewhere around 15 to 30 dollars an hour. Call it 20. Five hours a day, five days a week is 500 dollars a week. That is about 26 grand a year.

A Claude Routine run costs pennies. You are really just paying for tokens.

Now I am not telling you to go fire anyone. I am saying that every recurring task you do, or pay someone to do, is kind of a candidate for a Routine.

For me it looks like this:

- Morning inbox triage, 45 minutes a day, 195 hours a year
- Competitor research, two hours a week, a hundred hours a year
- The content pipeline busywork, gone

The whole point is not to do more work. It is to stop doing the work that does not really need you. Direct it, do not do it.

One thing you have to get right though. Routines run hands-off, in the cloud, so you cannot jump in and course correct mid-run. So you write the prompt like an SOP. Step one, step two, step three, done. You tell it exactly what to do, you do not hope it figures it out. Do not expect it to be 100 percent right if you leave it vague. But once you write it that way, it kind of just works, every morning, while you sleep.

Email 3 - Build a Routine from the CLI

Subject: The Claude Routines move nobody is showing you
Preview: You can build them straight from your terminal.

Hi {first_name},

So most Claude Routines tutorials stop at the website dashboard. They show you the scheduler, you click a few buttons, done.

Here is the part almost nobody covers, and it is the one I use the most.

You can create Routines right from the Claude Code CLI, on your own machine. You do not have to click through the web interface at all. You just describe the automation in your terminal, wire up the connectors, and schedule it, all from the same place you already build everything else.

And here is why that really matters. Your local setup is where your skills, your context, and your real work already live. So if you build the scheduled automation from that same terminal, there is no context switch. You write it where you already think, and then it goes and runs in the cloud on Anthropic's servers while you move on to the next thing.

That is kind of what turns Routines from a neat feature into an actual system:

- The cloud side runs the recurring work, laptop closed
- The local side is where you write it and refine it
- Same tool, two surfaces, one workflow

And that is the nice thing, right? You can keep tweaking the prompt until it does what you want, then let it run. If you have only ever seen the dashboard version, this is the piece that will change how you use it.
