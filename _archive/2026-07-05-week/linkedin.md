## Tue 7/7 - standalone (Cowork, the assistant framing)

Here is the simplest way I know to figure out what to automate.

Think about what an assistant would do for you. Then hand that to Claude Cowork.

Triage the inbox. Draft the follow ups. Organize the messy client folder. Turn a pile of receipts into an expense report. Pull your weekly numbers and tell you the three things to do next.

None of that is the exciting AI demo everyone posts. It is the boring, repetitive stuff that quietly eats your week.

That is exactly why it is worth handing off. Anything you do more than once is a candidate.

Start with one small thing. Not your whole business. One task you already do every week. Do it once with Cowork and see how it feels.

## Thu 7/9 - standalone (skills)

If you find yourself asking Claude to do the same thing over and over, you are doing it the hard way.

That repeated thing should be a skill.

A skill is just a file with the steps written down once. Then Claude runs it the same way every time. You stop re-explaining and start running it with one command.

I have skills for my weekly analytics report, my standup, turning a short into a slide deck, and a dozen other things I used to redo by hand.

The point is not that any one of them is impressive. It is that you only teach it once, and it stays taught.

Look at what you repeat this week. That is your first skill.

## Fri 7/10 - standalone (scheduled tasks)

The version of AI I actually like is the one that works while I am asleep.

Not a chat window I have to sit in front of. A task that runs on its own and hands me the result.

I have one that reads my inbox and calendar every morning and gives me a scannable brief. One that scrapes what is trending every Sunday. One that pulls my numbers for the week.

I did not build any of them by coding. I described what I wanted, checked the first run, and left it on.

That is the shift. You stop doing the recurring work and start reviewing it. Same output, none of the clicking.

## Sat 7/11 - standalone (Cowork + Claude Code together)

People keep asking me whether to use Claude Cowork or Claude Code. The answer is both, for different jobs.

Cowork is the assistant. It handles the recurring, redundant work. Organize this, draft that, pull these numbers, run it on a schedule. Friendly, fast, no code.

Claude Code is the engineer. When I need to actually build something, a funnel, a site, a tool, that is what I reach for.

I run them in parallel. Cowork keeps the business moving. Code builds the new things.

You do not have to pick. You just have to know which one fits the task in front of you.
