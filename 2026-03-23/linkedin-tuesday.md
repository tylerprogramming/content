# LinkedIn — Tuesday March 24
**Topic:** Context Rot — why Claude sessions get worse over time

---

Your Claude sessions are slowly getting dumber. And you're probably causing it.

Here's what's happening. Every message, every file Claude reads, every response it gives — all of that goes into the context window. It's like short-term memory. And it has a hard limit.

Once you hit that limit, Claude starts dropping earlier context to make room. It forgets what you told it an hour ago. Instructions stop sticking. Output quality drops.

You think something is broken. It's not — your session just got too long.

👉 Start a fresh session every 1-2 hours, or whenever you switch tasks

👉 Don't try to keep one conversation going all day — the compounding degrades the output

👉 If Claude seems confused about something you already discussed, that's your signal to start fresh

👉 Use CLAUDE.md for instructions you need every session — it survives context resets

Most people have never heard the term "context rot." Once you know it's happening, you can't unsee it. And fixing it is free — just start a new session.
