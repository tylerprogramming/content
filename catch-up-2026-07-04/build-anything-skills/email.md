Subject: Skills vs MCP, explained in one line
Preview: The question I get more than any other.

Hi {first_name},

So if you have ever been confused about how a skill is different from MCP, this one is for you. It is the question I get more than any other, and honestly it is an easy thing to mix up.

Here is the cleanest way I can say it.

MCP is a connection. It wires Claude to a tool or a service, like your database or an API.

A skill is instructions. It is you teaching Claude how you want a task done, in plain language, saved in a markdown file.

Plugins are just packaging. They bundle skills and tools so other people can install them.

So MCP is the connection, a skill is the know-how, and plugins are the wrapper. Once you see it that way, you kind of stop reaching for MCP when all you needed was to write your process down.

And here is the part I want you to sit with. Most of the real leverage is in skills, and it is the piece people use the least.

I made a full guide that lays all three out clearly, then builds skills live and runs one in Claude Cowork so you can watch it work end to end.
