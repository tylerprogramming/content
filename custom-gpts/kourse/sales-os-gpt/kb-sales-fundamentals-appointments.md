## Video 2: Sales Fundamentals

### The Sales Mindset

#### Conviction
You need full belief in what you're selling to close consistently. Two forms:
1. **Conviction in your product** — you know it gets results
2. **Conviction in yourself** — if you haven't proven your process for others yet, have full conviction you'll go above and beyond to figure it out for them

If you lack conviction in your product but have conviction in yourself, you can still sell. You'll struggle long-term without it. Build it early.

#### Helping People by Selling
If a prospect is interested, they're in a position of pain. **Selling them — if your stuff is good — is helping them.** Treat sales calls as help calls:
- You're getting on a call to diagnose their situation
- If you can help them, they should buy
- If you can't, they shouldn't
- You're not there just to close — you're there to figure out if it's a fit

#### Moral Obligation
> If you have conviction that what you do can help someone in a position of pain, you don't just *should* sell them — you have a **moral obligation** to.

**The Lottery Ticket Analogy:** You have a lottery ticket guaranteed to win tomorrow. You can't cash it yourself — you have to sell it to a loved one for $500. They say "I'm not sure, let me think about it." Would you give up? No — you'd find every reason to help them understand why they should do it. That's how you should feel about your product.

The outcomes your members achieve (financial freedom, quitting addictions, saving relationships) are worth more than a lottery ticket to most people. If you believe you can help them, giving up when they say "maybe" is the wrong move.

### What Selling Is (The Full Process)

Selling is not just what happens on a call. **Selling is exposing a prospect to a set of conditions that increases the likelihood they buy.**

Those conditions include:
- **Marketing** — is your content relatable? Does it speak to the right avatar? Does it hit real pain points?
- **Pre-call nurture** — VSL, follow-up messages, reminders, content sent before the call
- **The call** — the script, tonality, questions, objection handling
- **Post-call** — follow-up emails, testimonials you send after, BAM (book a meeting from a meeting)

Sales OS covers everything from pre-call through post-call. Traffic and content strategy are covered in their respective modules.

---

### The Underlying Principle: Pain

**Prospects buy because they're in pain.** Most big buying decisions are made to avoid pain, not seek pleasure — even pleasure-seeking often has pain at its root.

Examples:
- "I don't have enough leads" → hire a media buyer
- "My sales team is underperforming" → hire a sales coach
- "I want to turn my knowledge into a profitable online business so I can spend more time with my family" → buy Kourse

> People don't buy products or services. They buy their way out of painful situations.

Many prospects don't consciously think about their pain. They just say they're "checking it out" or "want more information." But deep down, they booked the call because they hate where they are and want something different.

**Pain avoidance is stronger than pleasure seeking.** This is why people hit $10K/month and stop pushing hard — the pain is gone, the pleasure-seeking isn't strong enough to match it.

---

### Current State → Desired State → The Gap

Every prospect has:
- **Current state** — where they are now (the pain)
- **Desired state** — where they want to be (state without their current problems)

**Your job:** Define both states. Build the gap between them. Present yourself as the bridge.

Building the gap means helping them see: if you don't change your actions and decisions, you will stay in your current state and never reach your desired state. It's not manipulation — it's helping them see the truth that will motivate them to act.

> Only do this if you genuinely believe you can help them. If you have conviction, building the gap is moral obligation in action.

### Common Misconceptions

| Misconception | Reality |
|--------------|---------|
| **Sales requires natural talent** | It's a learned skill. More reps = more skill. Miles was terrible at his first door knock. |
| **Introverts can't sell** | Introverts often make better sales reps — they listen better, ask deeper questions, build real trust |
| **Follow-up is key** | Follow-up helps if you can't close. Learn to close in one call first. One-call closes are the goal. |
| **Add value on the call to build trust** | Adding value on the call actually kills conversions. The prospect goes and implements and doesn't need you anymore. Your paid offer is the solution — not the call. |

## Video 3: Inbound Appointment System

### Qualification

**The goal:** Get serious people onto your calendar — and keep time-wasters off it.

#### The Friction Scale

- **Low friction** = more calls, less qualified
- **High friction** = fewer calls, more qualified

Start low when you're new — you need volume and data. Incrementally add friction as you identify which lead profiles convert and which don't.

You can add friction through:
- Calendar booking form questions
- VSLs (pre-booking and post-booking)
- Multi-step processes
- Making them watch your free course before qualifying

#### Calendar Booking Form — SSI Framework

Use the **SSI framework**: Situation → Seriousness → Investment.

**Situation questions (low friction):**
- "What's your main struggle with [problem] right now?"
- "Why had you checking out our free community?"
- "What's your current monthly income?" (+ "What's your target monthly income?")
- "What are you looking to achieve with [topic]?"
- "How much of the free course have you completed?"

**Seriousness question:**
- "If we find working together to be a good fit, how soon would you want to get started?"
- Options: Right away / In less than 2 weeks / In less than a month / Over a month
- This pre-handles the time objection on the call

**Investment question:**
- "How willing and able are you to invest in your growth and success?"
- Options: I have funds and I'm willing to invest / I may be able to raise funds / I have no funds and I'm not willing
- Cancel calls from option 3

**Higher friction option:**
- "Our program is a four-figure investment. Why is this something you're interested in investing in?"
- This drops call volume but dramatically increases close rate

**Laptop checkbox:** Add a confirmation checkbox — "I confirm I will attend on a laptop or desktop, not a mobile device." Reason: "To avoid connectivity issues." People on mobiles almost never buy.

**GoHighLevel setup:** Drag-and-drop questions in Sites → Forms. Use multiple choice wherever possible — it lowers friction because clicks are faster than typing.

### Post-Booking VSL (Success Page Video)

After someone books a call, redirect them to a **success page** with a video. This video:
- Pre-handles common objections before the call
- Sets expectations for what the call will look like
- Builds trust with social proof and testimonials
- Tells them to have decision-makers present ("reschedule if your business partner can't join")
- Primes them to pay on the call ("if it makes sense, we'll process your investment today")

**VSL Outline Framework:**
1. Hook + set expectations for the video
2. Attend on a laptop, quiet place, confirm the calendar invite
3. Have all decision-makers present or reschedule (pre-handles the spouse/partner objection)
4. Social proof (your story, your results, your clients — use relatable examples, not outliers)
5. Identify prospect goals and pain points
6. Address common objections (e.g., "Can you send the price first?" — explain why you don't)
7. Tell them why you do things your unique way (unique mechanism alignment)
8. Storytelling + case study (relatable person similar to them)
9. Frame your system as a shortcut (pay time or pay money)
10. Remove early price objections ("we work with people at different levels")
11. CTA to show up curious and ready to engage
12. Tease a bonus for showing up

### Triage Calls

A **triage call** is a pre-call phone call to pre-qualify a lead, gather information, and optionally pull them forward to an earlier time slot.

**Use a triage when:**
- You're unsure if someone is qualified
- You want to maximize close rate by doing a mini discovery before the main call
- You have open time slots and want to pull future bookings forward

**Triage script summary:**
1. "Hey, is this [Name]? It's Miles from Kourse — just giving you a quick call because I saw you booked a call for [day]."
2. "I just wanted to ask a few quick questions to make sure I'm as prepared as possible — do you have a couple of minutes?"
3. Current situation questions (niche, where they're at, idea stage vs. built out)
4. Goal + financial goal (if relevant)
5. Timing and urgency ("Why is now a good time to focus on this?")
6. Awareness check ("Have you gone through any of our free content yet?")
7. If 2–3 red flags, cancel the call and send them back to free content
8. Commitment check + confirm attendance
9. **Pull forward:** "You sound super serious — I actually have some free time right now. Would you be able to do the call now or in a couple of hours?"

### Reminders

**Show rate is as important as close rate.** If you double your show rate with the same close rate, you double your sales.

#### Automated Reminders
- Set up in GoHighLevel (already included in the automation)
- 24 hours before, day-of, 1 hour before
- Don't try to make these look real — people expect automated reminders after booking

#### Manual Reminders (the multiplier)
One Kourse sales rep was getting 20–30% higher show rates by reaching out via WhatsApp with personalized voice notes and texts. That alone = 20–30% more sales.

**Manual reminder templates:**
1. **First message:** "Hey, [Name] here from [Course]. We have an upcoming call on [day] at [time]. Just confirming you're able to attend. By the way, if you haven't yet, please watch this video before the call — it'll save us a bunch of time: [success page link]. Let me know if you have any questions."
2. **Follow-up (if no response):** "Hey, just checking in. I have a couple people who asked if they can take the time slot we're booked in for. Can you confirm you're still able to attend?"
3. **Final bump:** "Last call before I give this slot away — are you still able to make it?"

Before sending message 3, try a different medium: Skool DM, a Loom video, a phone call.

#### Loom Reminders (best practice)
Record a quick Loom before sending it. Show the time/date on screen to prove it's real: *"I'm recording this at 2pm on January 11th to show you this is a real message — not automated. I saw on your form that you're in the real estate niche. Looking forward to our call Tuesday."*

This dramatically outperforms automated messages and builds trust instantly.

#### Skool DMs
Use Skool to send DM reminders — members are already in the app and respond well.

### Nurture

#### 1. Triage Call
The triage call itself is the most powerful nurture — you're having a real conversation, gathering info, and building rapport before the main call.

#### 2. Personalized Testimonials
After the triage or by reviewing their calendar form answers, send a testimonial from someone in **the same niche or with the same current situation** as them.

*"Hey, I was just looking over your details and thought you'd appreciate this — here's how we helped [Name] in [similar niche] get to [result]."*

This exposes them to a condition that makes buying more likely before they even get on the call.

#### 3. Pre-Call Content
If there's something fundamental about your approach that prospects need to align with before the call (e.g., high-ticket annual vs. monthly), send them a video or YouTube link that explains it.

If they don't align → they'll cancel or won't move forward → saves everyone time.

If they do align → they arrive on the call already bought into your method → close rate skyrockets.

### Bonus Tips

| Tip | Why It Matters |
|-----|---------------|
| **Set availability by customer time zone** | If your leads are mostly US-based and you only open UK times, you lose half your calls |
| **15-minute calendar increments** | Hormozi recommended this — people find slots better, more bookings, quarter-past times book well |
| **3–4 day booking max** | Someone books Monday hyped up; by the following Monday they've forgotten you. 3 days is the sweet spot. |
| **Take calls Saturday and Sunday** | People spend money on weekends. If you don't open slots, you miss those leads. At least try it. |

#### Pull-Forward Strategy (Advanced)

When you have an open time slot and a lead booked for 3 days out, call them:

*"Hey [Name], it's [you] from [community] — just saw we have a call booked for Wednesday. I actually have some time right now, or in a couple hours. Would you be able to jump on the call today?"*

If yes → you pull that call into now, open up their future slot for someone else, and show massive business competence. If no → no harm done, confirm the original meeting.

> "I know 90% of you won't do this. The ones who do will love me for it. Please prove me wrong."

### Auto DM System

This system has generated **thousands of appointments** at Kourse.

**What it is:** Skool's native auto DM feature sends a pre-written message to every new member within minutes of joining. It's automated but can be written to feel personal.

**The goal:** Optimize for a **response**, not a booking link click. Get your foot in the door first — conversations convert.

#### Step 1: Conversational Curiosity (Opener)

Pick one of these openers — they work because they feel human and ask about their current situation:

- *"Hey [Name], just accepted you into the community — great to have you here. What had you checking us out? Are you currently in the [niche] space?"*
- *"Hey [Name], welcome in. I'm just sending some messages out to new members — what's the main thing you're looking to get out of this community? I'll see if I can point you in the right direction."*
- *"Hey [Name], just noticed you jumped into the community. Awesome to have you. Why had you checking this out? I'll see if I can send you some relevant material."*

#### Step 2: Situation Gap

Once they reply, figure out their current state and desired state:

- *"Totally get it. Where are you currently at with [topic]? I'll see if I can send you some relevant resources."*
- When they share their situation: *"Got it. What would you say the main goal is — is it a make-money thing or more of a passion project?"*
- When they share their goal: *"Love it. How long have you been going after that?"*

#### Step 3: Obstacle Identifier

*"What stopped you from getting there so far?"*

If they don't give a clear obstacle: *"Would you say getting [goal] is a priority for you right now?"*

If they say yes → move to permission ask.

#### Step 4: Permission Ask & Statement of Confidence

*"Got you. Is this something you're open to getting outside help on?"*

If they had a goal + it's a priority → they can't say no to this without contradicting themselves.

Then:
- **If you have proof:** *"Well, I've been able to help [X] people overcome [obstacle] and get to [outcome]. Since you're serious about this, I'm pretty confident I can help."*
- **If no proof yet:** *"Well, I actually help [your offer statement] — and since you're serious about this, I'm pretty confident I can help."*

#### Step 5: Call Invitation (Assume the Close)

Send this directly after the statement of confidence — don't wait for a response:

*"What I normally do here is just book in a quick call over Zoom to see if what we have can actually help you. I have some time tomorrow — would morning or afternoon be better for you?"*

**Why "morning or afternoon" works:** You're assuming yes and giving them two options, not yes or no. Harder to reject.

#### Step 6: Diffusal (If They Push Back)

Most pushback is fear of a salesy call:

*"I totally get it. Don't worry — this isn't some salesy sales call. We'll just have a quick chat to see if I can help you with [obstacle]. If I can't, we'll wrap it up pretty quick. Does that sound fair?"*

**Note:** Calling them is always better than DM. If they respond to the auto DM, try to get them on the phone. Use the cold calling script from there.

### Cold Calling Script

**Calling is always better than DM.** Two hours of dialing → 5 appointments booked → 40% close rate → 2 sales → at $2,000 each = $4,000. Do that 20 days a month = $40K/month with consistent calling.

**Opener:** Tone up at the end of their name — *"Sarah?"* — not a statement, a question. Sounds natural.

**Script:**
1. *"Hey, it's [Name] getting back to you from [community]. How's it going?"*
2. *"I was just giving you a quick call because I noticed you recently joined our free community. Do you have 30 seconds?"*
3. Current situation: *"Do you currently run a [type of thing] or are you more in the idea stage?"*
4. Goal: *"What's the main goal with this right now?"*
5. Pain check: *"What's the main thing stopping you from hitting that goal?"*
6. Priority check: *"Would you say that getting to [goal] is a priority for you right now?"*
7. Permission: *"Got it. Would you be open to getting outside help with this if it meant getting there faster?"*
8. Call invitation: *"What I'd normally do here is set up a quick call to see if what we've got can actually help. Are you free tomorrow — morning or afternoon better for you?"*
9. Qualification while booking: Run through your calendar form questions (current situation, income, timing)
10. Commitment check: *"If we sit down together and you love what we've got — is there any reason you wouldn't be able to get started right away? Timing-wise, not investment-wise."*

### Inbound Opt-In Setting

These are **the hottest leads** — people who visited your funnel page and started but didn't complete the booking. They intended to book. Call them immediately.

**Rule:** Never send them a booking link. Book them yourself on the call.

*"I have time [tomorrow/this week] — morning or afternoon work for you?"*

Then fill out the calendar booking yourself using their information while you're on the call. After booking, run through your qualification questions conversationally.

**Funnel opt-in notification:** Set up a GHL automation to notify you (text/email) the moment someone opts in but doesn't complete the booking. Speed to lead is everything.
