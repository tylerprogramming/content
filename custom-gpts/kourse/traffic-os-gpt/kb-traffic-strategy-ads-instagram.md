### Traffic Nirvana

The goal is to have all 6 channels running simultaneously:

## Video 2: Awareness Levels

Understanding where your prospect is in their awareness journey determines what ad creative or content to show them.

| Level | State | What They Need |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| Completely Unaware | Don't know they have a problem | Broad educational content |
| Problem Aware | Know they have a problem, don't know solutions exist | Problem-focused content |
| Solution Aware | Know solutions exist, haven't chosen one | Comparison/differentiation content |
| Product Aware | Know about you, haven't bought | Social proof, urgency, specific offer |
| Most Aware | Ready to buy | Direct CTA, minimal friction |

**Cold ads** should target completely unaware → problem aware.
**Retargeting ads** should target solution aware → most aware.

Matching the message to the awareness level is the #1 lever for improving ad performance.

## Video 3: Meta Ads — The Andromeda Update

### What Changed (July 2025)

Meta released the **Andromeda update** — a new AI-based ad engine that fundamentally changed how targeting works.

**Before:** You chose interests, demographics, and custom audiences. Meta showed your ad to people matching those parameters.

**After:** You go broad. No interest targeting. The creative itself tells Meta's algorithm who to show it to.

**The new rule: Creative IS the targeting.**

If your ad talks about "making money with AI tools," Meta's algorithm figures out who responds to that message and finds more of them. Your job is to create the right message — Meta's job is to find the right people.

### Campaign Structure

- **Use Advantage+ campaigns** (Meta's AI-optimized format)
- **Broad audience** — no interest targeting, minimal restrictions
- **Multiple creatives in one ad set** — let Meta optimize which performs best
- **Test creative, not audiences** — your variables are hooks, visuals, and copy

### What to Test

- **Hook (first 3 seconds of video or headline of image)** — this drives 80% of performance
- **Format** — video vs. image vs. carousel
- **Awareness angle** — problem-focused vs. solution-focused vs. result-focused

### Budget Guidance

- Start with $20–$50/day per campaign
- Don't touch campaigns for 3–5 days after launching — let the algorithm learn
- Scale what's working by duplicating the winning ad set at a higher budget

## Video 4: Meta Cold Ad Creative

### The 3 Creative Types

**1. Problem-aware creative**
Lead with a pain point your audience recognizes. No need to mention your product.
> *"Most people trying to build an online course make this mistake..."*

**2. Transformation/result creative**
Show a result, then tease how. Used for warmer cold audiences.
> *"How I went from 0 to 1,000 members in 90 days using Skool..."*

**3. Social proof creative**
Testimonials, case studies, community screenshots. Best for retargeting but also works for warmer cold traffic.

### Video Ad Structure (for cold)

1. **Hook (0–3 sec):** Interrupt the scroll. Bold statement, surprising result, or provocative question.
2. **Problem (3–10 sec):** Agitate the pain or curiosity.
3. **Solution tease (10–20 sec):** Hint at what you're going to show them.
4. **CTA (final 5 sec):** "Click the link to join for free" or "Watch the free training."

Keep cold video ads under 60 seconds. Under 30 seconds is ideal for most audiences.

## Video 5: Skool + Meta Pixel Integration

### Why Server-Side Tracking Matters

iOS privacy updates degraded browser-based pixel tracking significantly. Server-side tracking bypasses this — it fires the conversion event directly from Skool's servers to Meta, not from the user's browser.

**Result:** More accurate data → better Meta algorithm optimization → lower cost per lead.

### How to Set It Up

1. Go to your Skool community → Settings → Integrations
2. Connect your Meta Pixel ID
3. Enable server-side events (Skool handles this natively)
4. Verify in Meta Events Manager that events are firing

**What to track:**
- Lead (someone joins your free community)
- Purchase (someone buys your paid community)

Once your pixel has 50+ conversions per week, Meta's algorithm can fully optimize. Below that, use cost-per-result campaigns and be patient.

## Video 6: Meta Retargeting

### Why Retargeting Wins

Cold traffic converts at 1–3%. Warm retargeting audiences convert at 5–15%+.

Retargeting is where you recoup your cold ad spend. Someone saw your YouTube video, visited your funnel, or engaged with your Instagram — now you follow them with a more direct offer.

### Retargeting Audiences to Build

| Audience | Source | Warmth |
|----------|--------|--------|
| Website visitors (last 30 days) | Meta Pixel | Warm |
| Video viewers 50%+ (last 60 days) | Meta video engagement | Warm |
| Instagram engagers (last 60 days) | Instagram profile | Warm |
| YouTube viewers → Meta | YouTube retargeting (Video 7) | Warm |
| Free community members | Customer list upload | Hot |

### Retargeting Campaign Structure

- Separate campaign from your cold traffic campaigns
- Smaller budget ($10–$20/day) — audience is smaller
- Use more direct creative: testimonials, case studies, specific offer, scarcity/urgency
- CTA goes directly to booking page or paid community (skip the VSL for hottest audiences)

### Retargeting Creative Principles

- They already know who you are — skip the broad education
- Lead with social proof or a direct result
- Show them what they're missing by not joining
- Urgency works here because they've already considered it

## Video 7: YouTube Retargeting

### The Strategy

YouTube viewers are warm leads — they've watched your content, they know you, they just haven't opted in yet. You can retarget them with Meta ads.

### How It Works

1. **Connect YouTube channel to Google Ads** — creates YouTube audience lists
2. **Export/sync those audiences to Meta** via third-party tools or manual list upload
3. **Run retargeting ads on Meta** to everyone who watched your YouTube videos

**Alternatively:** Use Google/YouTube ads directly to retarget YouTube viewers — this keeps retargeting inside the same platform.

### YouTube Retargeting Ad Creative

- Reference the YouTube content they watched: *"If you saw my video on X, here's what to do next..."*
- Bridge from education (YouTube) to action (book a call / join for free)
- Shorter and more direct than cold ads — they know you

## Instagram Daily Strategy (Max's Framework)

### Why Instagram

Instagram is the daily content layer that keeps you top-of-mind with warm audiences and generates consistent organic opt-ins to your free community.

### Daily Posting Framework

Post daily. Mix of:
- **Reels** — highest reach, drives new followers
- **Carousels** — high saves/shares, good for value content
- **Stories** — daily relationship building, direct CTAs

### Content That Converts to Opt-ins

- Problem/pain posts → CTA to free community for the solution
- Mini case studies → "Here's exactly how [student] did X — we teach this inside [community name], link in bio"
- Behind-the-scenes → builds trust and personality
- Polls and questions → drives engagement which boosts reach

### CTA Strategy

Every piece of content should have one clear CTA. For traffic purposes, that CTA is almost always: **"Join [free community] — link in bio."**

Don't pitch the paid product on Instagram. Get them into the free community first. The funnel handles the rest.
