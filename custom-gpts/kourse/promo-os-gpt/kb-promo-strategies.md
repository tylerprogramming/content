### 1. Weekly Workshop (Mandatory)

**Frequency:** One live workshop per week — every Sunday

**Structure:**
- 2–3 minute welcome (let people join, play some music)
- 30-minute training (teach what to do, not how to do it)
- 5-minute CTA to book a call (with scarcity, urgency, and bonuses)
- 15–20 minute live Q&A (on a community post, not in the live stream chat)

**How to rotate topics:**
- Build 4 different workshops
- Rotate them weekly: Week 1 → Workshop A, Week 2 → Workshop B, etc.
- After 4 weeks, circle back to Workshop A
- Over time, iterate and improve each one

**Why Sundays work:** People are relaxed at home, they have time, and a 45–60 minute workshop fits naturally into a casual Sunday.

**What to teach:** Cover what they need to do next (e.g., traffic, promotions, sales), but not how to do it. The "how" is in the paid course. Teaching the "what" builds trust and makes the CTA to book a call logical and natural.

**One call per week rule:** Don't have a separate Q&A call AND a workshop. Combine them. One event = easier to promote, easier for members to remember, only one recording to watch.

See the dedicated webinars video for full workshop setup instructions.

### 2. Skool Email Blast (Every 72 Hours)

**Frequency:** Every Monday and Thursday

**How it works:** Every time you create a post in Skool and check the "email blast" box, Skool sends an email to every single member of your community. Skool has exceptional deliverability and open rates — don't sleep on this.

**Bonus:** When someone joins your free community, they automatically follow you on Skool. Max has 153,000+ Skool followers. Every post notifies them all.

**What to post:**
- Value + CTA to book a call
- Share a YouTube video or lesson with a soft CTA at the end
- Use Tella or Loom for embedded video posts

> "Arguably the most powerful Skool promotion feature there is. Use it on cooldown — every 72 hours."

---

### 3. Skool Auto DM

**What it is:** Every new member who joins your free community automatically receives a welcome DM. Set it up once, runs forever.

**How to enable:** Go to Settings → Plugins → Auto DM (Pro feature — must be enabled)

**Recommended message format:**
```
Hi {{name}}, welcome to [Your Free Community]! 🎉

Here we'll teach you [core promise]. Get started with our free course here: [link to first video]
```

**Link format in Skool DMs:**
```
[Link text](https://yourlink.com)
```

**What to include:**
- Short friendly greeting with their name
- One-sentence value statement
- Link directly to the first video in your course

**Optional:** Add a short Tella video welcome — more personal, builds trust faster.

### 4. DM Outreach (Setting)

**What it is:** Proactively DM new members who join your free community, start a conversation, and convert them into booked calls. This is called "setting" (setting sales appointments).

**Why it works:** These are warm leads. They joined your free community — they already showed interest. This isn't a cold call; it's a friendly welcome that naturally leads to a conversation.

**Simple approach:**
1. Member joins
2. You DM: *"Hey [Name], super cool to see you inside the community! What are you working on right now?"*
3. They reply — you learn their problem
4. You offer: *"Would you like to jump on a quick call and chat about it?"*

**Reality at scale:** Once you have thousands of members joining, you'll need a setter to do this for you (covered in Sales OS). But in the beginning, do it yourself — it moves the needle.

> "Most people sleep on DM outreach. Don't. You have leads right in front of you."

---

### 5. Cold/Warm Calling (Optional Power Activity)

**What it is:** Calling new members who've just joined your free community to welcome them and offer a one-on-one call.

**Why it's a warm call, not a cold call:** They joined your community. They know who you are. When you text/call and say *"Hey, this is Max from the free course — just wanted to welcome you,"* they're pleasantly surprised, not annoyed.

**How to set it up:**
- Go to Settings → Plugins → Membership Questions
- Add a question asking for their phone number + country code: *"Please provide your phone number so we can confirm you're not a bot. Please include your country code."*
- **Important:** Don't send the phone number to GoHighLevel via Zapier — formatting errors cause the SAP to fail and you lose their email too. Collect the phone number in Skool, use the email only for the automation.

**The process:**
1. New member joins
2. You see their phone number in Skool
3. You call or text: *"Hey [Name], this is [You] from [Community] — just wanted to welcome you!"*
4. Friendly conversation → offer a one-on-one call
5. They agree → book it

This is a high-leverage activity. The ones who do it get rewarded massively.

### 6. Weekly Q&A Calls

**What it is:** A live call where you answer questions from your free community members, build trust, and add a CTA to book a call.

**How to run them:**
- Start the call or webinar
- Ask people to post questions in a community post (not in the live stream chat — see the engagement hack in the webinars section)
- Answer questions one by one from the post
- Include a CTA: *"If you want more help with this, book a call — we'll help you build a 90-day plan"*

**Max's approach:** He merges Q&A calls with the weekly workshop. One event every Sunday = training + CTA + Q&A. This is simpler to promote and easier for members to follow.

**Use school webinar format (not school call)** in the free community once the group gets bigger. Webinar = only you can talk. This makes the free feel more like a broadcast (less chaotic) and saves the two-way interaction for the paid.

### 7. Retargeting Ads

**Non-negotiable.** Covered in detail in Traffic OS, but it's on the Promo Checklist too.

- Install Google and Meta pixels on your Skool community
- Retarget everyone who lands on your page
- Run retargeting to your free community AND to your VSL
- Start with as little as $10/day — it adds up fast
- Warm retargeting is the most powerful and cost-effective form of paid ads
- Combined with organic traffic, it compounds everything

> "I want to see as few people as possible inside this course who don't run retargeting ads."

### 8. Email Marketing (GoHighLevel)

**Why email is underrated:** Email has the highest ROI of any marketing channel — estimated $30–40 return for every $1 spent. You own the list. No algorithm. Minimal cost.

**Frequency:** 1–3 emails per week via GoHighLevel (on top of the Skool email blasts)

**What to send:**
- Share your latest YouTube video
- Promote the upcoming workshop
- Give a quick value tip
- CTA to book a call

**How to use the template in GHL:**
- Go to Marketing → Email → Campaigns → New → Use Templates
- Select the weekly newsletter template (Arial font, 16px, 1.5 line height)
- Add headline, image, value content, and a CTA button to your VSL/booking page

**Every time someone joins your free Skool community**, their email gets sent to GoHighLevel via Zapier (SAP integration). Your email list grows automatically as your free community grows. This is the flywheel.

> "I ignored email for too long. I had tens of thousands of people on my list and wasn't emailing them. The moment I started, I made more money."

### 9. Skool Community Links

**What it is:** A Skool Pro plugin that lets you add a navigation link inside your community — pointing directly to your book-a-call funnel.

**How to enable:** Settings → Plugins → Links

**Critical setting: Make it PRIVATE (not public)**
- If it's public, the link shows on your Skool landing page to non-members
- This gives people a reason to NOT join (they see a sales CTA before they're warmed up)
- Private = only visible once they've joined the community
- Non-members only see one CTA: "Join Group"

**Also:** Add a link to your book-a-call funnel underneath each video in your free course. Simple, non-intrusive, always present.

---

### 10. Onboarding Video

**What it is:** A custom welcome video that plays when someone joins your free community, replacing Skool's default generic one.

**How to set it up:** Settings → Plugins → Onboarding Video → Upload your own

**Keep it under 2 minutes.** Longer = people skip it.

**What to include:**
- Welcome to the community
- Quick tour: here's the Classroom, the Calendar (weekly workshops every Sunday!), the Community tab, the Map
- CTA: *"If you'd like a one-on-one call to get started right away, here's a link to book — I'll see you inside!"*

**Don't overthink it.** Record a quick, casual 90-second screen share walkthrough and have your CTA at the end. You can update it later. The most important thing is having something live.

### 11. Daily Activity in the Community

**Be present every single day.** Reply to posts, welcome new members, ask questions, answer questions, stay visible.

School has confirmed directly: **active owner is a major ranking factor** for Skool Discovery.

This doesn't have to be elaborate. A quick reply here, a welcome there, a fun poll — it all counts.

---

### 12. Value Posts (2–4 Per Week)

- 2 of these are your Monday and Thursday email blast posts
- Add 2 more posts per week without a blast
- Teach something, share something valuable, guide them
- Soft CTA at the end: *"If you want help with X, book a call"*
- Mix in personal updates, behind-the-scenes content, fun polls — not every post needs to be a perfect pitch

---

### 13. Random Live Streams (1–2 per week)

**Different from workshops:** These are unannounced, casual, drop-in live streams.

**How Max does it:** Goes live randomly, creates a post in the community (not the live stream chat) for questions, answers them on the post during the stream, then posts the recording with a poll afterwards.

**Why this works:** Engagement. Every comment on the post counts toward your Skool Discovery ranking. And it keeps the community warm and active between scheduled workshops.

## Summary: The Weekly Promo Schedule

| Day | Activity |
|-----|---------|
| Monday | Skool email blast post (value + CTA) |
| Tuesday or Wednesday | Random live stream (unannounced) + Q&A post |
| Thursday | Skool email blast post (value + CTA) |
| Friday/Saturday | Promote Sunday workshop (post in community) |
| Sunday | Weekly workshop (30 min training + CTA + Q&A) |
| Daily | Welcome members, reply to posts, stay visible |

On top of this: email newsletters 1–3x/week via GHL, retargeting ads running daily, DM outreach to new members.

> "Plan your work. Then work your plan. And remember — most people won't do this consistently. Don't be one of those people."
