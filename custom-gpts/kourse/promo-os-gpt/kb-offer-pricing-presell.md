## Video 3: Offer & Pricing

### Keep the Offer Simple

One clear problem. One clear outcome. One clear solution. Simple offers convert better than complex ones.

> From Hormozi: your first million should be one offer, one pricing, one funnel, one lead magnet.

Don't add upsells, downsells, and complexity early on. Focus on one thing.

---

### Sell the Transformation, Not Information

People don't buy information. They buy the result the course helps them achieve.

- They're not buying a book — they're buying the knowledge to complete a transformation
- The course is the vehicle for that transformation
- Your job is to show them: *"Here's where you are. Here's where you want to be. My course is how you get there."*

---

### The Free-to-Paid Structure

Give away the first part of the transformation for free. Charge for the rest.

**Why this is so powerful:**
- The free course does the selling before the call
- By the time someone books a call, they already know you, like you, and trust you
- They're not cold — they've experienced your value firsthand
- The sales call is just: answer a few questions, confirm the details, process the payment

> "Most people on your sales calls will already be sold. They just need a few questions answered."

---

### What the Paid Offer Includes

**Core value proposition — 4 pillars:**

1. **Full course access** — Step-by-step training from where they are to their desired outcome, end to end
2. **Community access** — Private community for support, feedback, accountability, and momentum
3. **Weekly Q&A calls** — Live calls where they can ask questions, get clarity, and talk to you directly (not just post in a chat)
4. **More help and support** — More access to you, more feedback, more guidance

> "People are always willing to pay money to get to their destination faster and easier with more help and support."

This is the single most important principle in pricing. Say it three times and remember it.

**Optional additions:**
- **Bonuses** — Tools, templates, extra training that helps them win faster (Hormozi-style)
- **Custom GPT** — Build a GPT trained on your course transcripts. Students get instant 24/7 Q&A support. Create it in ChatGPT by uploading your course transcripts. Adds massive perceived value.

---

### The $100M Offer Formula (Hormozi)

Know this in your sleep. Use it for launches, pre-sells, and ongoing offers.

| Element | What It Is |
|---------|-----------|
| **Scarcity** | Limited spots — "Only 10 people get this offer" |
| **Urgency** | Time-limited — "This offer ends in 15 days" |
| **Discount** | Price reduction for fast action — "20% off, only for the pre-launch" |
| **Bonuses** | Extra value added — "You also get 2 one-on-one coaching calls with me" |
| **Risk Reversal** | Guarantee — conditional or unconditional (see below) |

Use all five to create a no-brainer offer, especially for launches and pre-sells.

---

### Guarantees: Should You Use Them?

**Conditional guarantee:** "If you complete the course, attend at least X Q&A calls, and you're still not happy within 30 days, I'll refund you."

**Unconditional guarantee:** "If you're not happy within 2 weeks for any reason, I'll refund you."

**Max's current position:** He stopped using guarantees. Here's why:
- When you've built strong free content and people know exactly what they're buying, the sale should stand on its own merit
- Guarantees attract lower-quality buyers who are hedging their bets
- Instead: filter people on the front end. Make sure they're committed. If they're not ready, disqualify them on the call and send them back to the free course

**How to handle the guarantee objection:**
> "Our guarantee is: if you don't do anything, nothing will happen. Our training is exceptional — and you already know that from the free. You know exactly what you're getting. But there's also a risk in NOT buying: if you don't take action now, you'll keep staying where you are. Which risk is bigger?"

---

### Risk Reversals (When You Do Use Them)

Conditional is better than unconditional. Tie the refund to completing key actions:
- Complete the course
- Attend at least X Q&A calls
- Join the community for at least X weeks

This filters out people who just want to watch passively and then ask for money back.

### Pricing Models on Skool

#### Low Ticket Monthly ($30–$200/month)
**Difficulty: 10 out of 10**
- Constant churn — you add one customer and lose another
- Lower-quality buyers who are less invested
- Customers complete the transformation and then have no reason to keep paying
- Requires non-stop promotion to maintain revenue
- Max scaled to $150K/month on monthly pricing — and hit a wall. Working harder just to maintain the same revenue.

> From Alex Hormozi directly to Max: "You're selling your stuff too cheap. You're getting people amazing results for very little money."

**The book analogy:** If you rent a book for $30/month, read it in a month, why keep paying? Same logic applies to monthly course subscriptions.

#### Mid Ticket Automated Webinar ($500–$1,500)
**Difficulty: 6 out of 10**
- Can work, but requires strong funnel, high traffic volume, and constant optimization
- You're not selling on the phone, so you can't handle objections, tailor your pitch, or close in real-time
- More moving parts, more things to break

#### High Ticket Phone Sales ($1,000–$10,000+)
**Difficulty: 2 out of 10**
- Sell over the phone after warming people up with the free course
- Fewer customers, higher margins, better results, better quality buyers
- You can handle objections, present your pitch deck, and close in real time
- The warm leads from your free course make the sales call much easier

> A person Max helped was stuck at $40K/month with low ticket monthly. They switched to high ticket, phone-based sales — and hit $200K/month with fewer customers, less headache, and better results.

---

### Recommended Pricing

| Option | Price | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Minimum | $1,000 | Bare minimum — anything lower creates more problems than progress |
| Good starting point | $1,500–$2,000 | Still on the low end |
| Sweet spot | $2,000–$3,500 | Strong course offers typically land here |
| Higher end | $5,000–$10,000+ | Possible once you have testimonials, results, and confidence |

> Sam Ovens' guidance: $3,500 is the bare minimum for a course. Max is slightly more flexible for people building confidence, but challenges you to think about the real value you deliver.

**Why higher price = better customers:**
- Higher investment = more commitment
- Committed buyers implement more, get better results, ask better questions, are easier to work with
- The person who pays $25K is often the easiest client Max has

---

### The Recommended Offer Structure

**Core offer:**
- Full course access
- Weekly Q&A calls
- Private paid community
- Ongoing support

**Pricing:**
- **Split pay:** $1,000/month × 4 months = $4,000 total (lowers friction, signed contract)
- **Pay in full:** $3,000 (saves them $1,000 — rewards fast decision, improves your cash flow)

**Sales logic:**
- Split pay feels more accessible (easier to say yes)
- Pay in full feels like a win (they save money)
- Works at any price point — just adjust the numbers proportionally

> "Which one do you want to use — Visa or MasterCard?"

**Later: Mastermind upsell**
Once students complete your paid course, offer a mastermind backend with in-person events, more support, and deeper access. Covered in Ascension OS.

### The Offer Worksheet

Use the worksheet at **course.com/offer-worksheet** to design your offer:

1. What is your niche?
2. What is their current situation?
3. What is their desired outcome?
4. What is it costing them to stay where they are?
5. What would it be worth to reach their desired outcome?
6. What is your course structure? (outline)
7. What's in your free course vs. your paid course?
8. What is your price?
9. What bonuses do you include?
10. What guarantee (if any)?
11. Value stack — show that the price makes sense

Do this exercise even if you've done it before. It builds confidence in your offer and makes your CTAs much more natural and compelling.

## Video 4: Launch & Pre-Sell

### The Core Idea

**You do not need to build your course before you start selling it.**

This goes against most people's instincts. But it works — every time. And your customers will be happy to buy a pre-sell offer, not despite the course being unfinished, but because you give them a no-brainer deal to compensate.

> One of Max's customers pre-sold his course before it was built and made **$150,000 in contracted value and $55,000 in cash collected in three weeks.**

Software companies do this all the time — MVP first, sell early access, build as you go. Do the same with your course.

---

### When to Launch

**Launch immediately.** As soon as your free community is live, start promoting calls and selling your paid course. Don't wait for:
- A certain number of free members
- The course to be complete
- "Enough" social proof

The goal is to make your investment back as soon as possible. Start now.

---

### What You Need to Start Selling

You do NOT need:
- A finished course
- All your videos recorded
- A complete paid community

You DO need:
- Your paid Skool community created (with a welcome post and clear expectations)
- Your course outline (what's in each module)
- Your offer ready (price, bonuses, timeline)

That's it. Everything else gets built as you sell.

---

### The Pre-Sell Framework (Hormozi Style)

Use this to structure your pre-sell offer and launch announcement.

**The five elements:**

| Element | Example |
|---------|---------|
| **Scarcity** | Only 10 spots available for this pre-launch offer |
| **Discount** | 20% off because the course isn't built yet (e.g., $2,400 instead of $3,000) |
| **Launch timeline** | First module drops in 2 weeks, then one new module every week |
| **Bonus** | 2 one-on-one hour coaching calls with you (removed after the pre-sell) |
| **Social proof ask** | In return, they agree to leave a testimonial if they like it |
| **Risk reversal** (optional) | 100% refund if they complete the course, attend X Q&A calls, and aren't happy |

---

### Example Pre-Sell Pitch (Word for Word)

> *"Okay, so [Name], here's what I want to do. I'm launching my paid course, but instead of waiting until everything is built, I'm opening early access to a small group. I'm only taking 10 people.*
>
> *Because the course isn't fully built yet, those first 10 people get a 20% discount. So instead of $3,000, you'd pay $2,400. The first module launches in 2 weeks, and then I'll release one new module every week after that. So in 5 weeks, the full course is done.*
>
> *On top of the discount, I'm also including two one-on-one coaching calls with me — worth $2,000. These calls go away after the pre-launch. I'm only doing this to make sure the first 10 people get great results.*
>
> *In return, I'll ask you for a testimonial once you've gone through the course and coaching — if you like it.*
>
> *Once these 10 spots are gone, the price goes to $3,000 and this deal disappears forever. I've already sold [X] spots. I have [Y] calls on my calendar this week. Do you want one of the early access spots?*"

---

### How to Announce It in Your Free Community

Use ChatGPT to write your post. Example prompt:

> *"I'm launching a pre-sell offer for my paid course. I'm looking for the first 10 people to join at a 20% pre-launch discount. I will also work one-on-one with the first 10 people (this goes away later). I have only 10 spots. Already have 2 people interested. If interested, book a call here: [link]. This will be posted in my free Skool community of [X] members."*

ChatGPT will give you a clean, punchy community post. Adjust as needed and post it immediately.

---

### Why Customers Love the Pre-Sell

- They get a discount
- They get exclusive one-on-one coaching they can't get later
- They're part of the founding group
- They influence the course as it's built (their feedback shapes it)
- They get first access

This is not a scam or a shortcut. It's a win-win. Your buyers get an incredible deal. You validate demand, generate cash, and build testimonials before you ever finish the product.

---

### The Outcome

When you close your first pre-sell:
- You get cash in the bank before the course is built
- You have committed buyers who will actually go through the material
- You have social proof obligations (testimonials) baked in
- You have a fire lit under you to build the course (because people have paid for it)

> "Now you want to build it because people have paid. It creates the best kind of pressure."

---

### Action Step: Do This Now

1. Build your pre-sell offer using the five-element framework above
2. Write a community announcement post (use ChatGPT with the template above)
3. Post it in your free community today
4. Start getting calls booked
5. Use the pre-sell pitch on the calls

**Don't overthink it. Just do it.**

Set the timeline: this offer ends in 14 days, or when 10 spots are filled — whichever comes first.

> "You can start pre-selling your course today. Right now. You don't need the product built. Start."
