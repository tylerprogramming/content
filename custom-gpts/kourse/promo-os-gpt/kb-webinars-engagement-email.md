## Video 5: Weekly Webinars / Workshops

### Why Weekly Workshops Beat the "Perfect Webinar Formula"

Most people hear "webinar" and think of a polished, scripted sales presentation where you pitch someone into buying at the end. That's NOT what we're doing.

**The distinction:**
- Traditional webinar = same script every week, pitch to buy, people join once and never come back
- Workshop = fresh training each week, valuable learning, CTA is only to book a call (not to buy)

Because we're not selling on the workshop, you don't need the perfect webinar formula. What you need is:
- A topic your audience cares about
- A 30-minute training that shows them what to do (not how to do it)
- A natural CTA to book a call at the end

The word "workshop" is better than "webinar" anyway. Workshops convey value and learning. "Webinar" signals "sales pitch" to most people.

---

### Why to Do Them Every Week

- **Fills your calendar** with booked calls week over week
- **Builds trust and credibility** through consistent value delivery
- **Warms up leads** before they ever get on a sales call
- **Boosts Skool Discovery ranking** through engagement (posts, likes, comments, recordings)
- **Tracks your progress** — every Sunday you run a workshop and fill in your performance tracker

> "You start loving doing those workshops every Sunday because you go live and you just see — boom — I have calls in the calendar for the week."

---

### Workshop Structure

| Segment | Duration | What to Do |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Welcome | 2–3 min | Greet people, play some music, let latecomers join |
| Training | 30 min | Teach what to do, not how to do it |
| CTA | 5 min | Pitch booking a call using scarcity, urgency, bonuses |
| Q&A | 15–20 min | Answer questions via a community post (not the live stream chat) |

**Total:** ~50–60 minutes

---

### What to Teach: "What, Not How"

This is the core principle that makes workshops work without cannibalizing your paid course.

**The framework:**
- You show people **what they need to do next** on their journey
- You don't show them **how to do it** (that's in the paid course)
- If they want help with the "how" → they book a call

**Example topics (mapped to your course modules):**
- Course building workshop → "Here's everything you need to do to build your course" (don't show the Tella setup)
- Traffic workshop → "Here's what you need to do with YouTube, ads, Instagram, and Skool discovery" (don't walk through ad manager)
- Promo workshop → "Here's how you convert free members: email, workshops, DMs" (don't build the automation)
- Sales workshop → "Here's what you need to learn: setting, closing, scripts, objections" (don't give the script)

Each workshop reveals the landscape. The paid course teaches the path.

---

### The 4 Rotating Workshops

Build 4 different workshop decks (using the same Keynote style as your course slides). Rotate them weekly:

- Week 1: Workshop A
- Week 2: Workshop B
- Week 3: Workshop C
- Week 4: Workshop D
- Week 5: Back to Workshop A

New members join every day. Even if someone attends all 4 in a month, it's fine — they're getting more value, building more trust, and warming up faster for a sales call.

---

### How to Set Up Workshops on Skool

1. Go to your free community → Calendar
2. Create a recurring event: Weekly on Sunday
3. Set it as a **Skool Webinar** (not a call — webinar = you control the mic, members use chat only)
4. Give each individual occurrence a unique title and description (edit individual events, not the series)
5. Upload a custom cover image for each workshop
6. Enable "Remind members 1 day before"

**Recurring event trick:** Create one recurring Sunday webinar, then edit each individual occurrence to give it a unique topic, description, and image.

**Webinar vs. Call:**
- Use **Webinar** format for your free community — you speak, they chat → keeps it structured, signals higher value for paid
- Use **Call** format only if you want everyone to be able to unmute (reserved for paid community Q&A calls)

---

### Promoting Your Workshop

Promote 3 days before on Thursday/Friday/Saturday:

*"Don't miss the workshop this Sunday, guys — I'm going to walk you through everything you need to do with traffic. Don't miss it."*

On the day of the workshop, make a post:

*"Going live in 2 hours 📚 [Workshop topic] — see you there!"*

People get a notification. They're home on a Sunday. They join.

---

### How to Pitch the Call from the Workshop

**The world's easiest pitch:**
> *"If you like this free workshop, you're going to love the one-on-one call we offer. It's completely free. We'll map out your 90-day plan, look at your niche and offer, and give you full clarity on what to do next. Whether you join us or not — you'll leave with a plan."*

**Adding scarcity + urgency + bonuses:**
> *"For the next 10 people who book a call from this workshop today, I'll include an exclusive bonus worth $X. This is only available to people on this live call, and only for the first 10 who book. Once those spots are gone, the bonus is gone."*

Keep the "bonus" as a surprise — they don't need to know exactly what it is. The scarcity and exclusivity is enough.

**Set a timer:** "This offer is available for the next 15 minutes while we do Q&A."

---

### The Skool Engagement Hack: Q&A on a Post (Not the Live Chat)

**The problem with Skool's live stream chat:**
- 250-character limit (hard to ask good questions)
- You can't highlight which question you're answering
- Less engagement = lower Discovery ranking

**The solution:**

During your workshop, after the training and CTA, say:

*"Okay guys, now it's Q&A time. Drop your questions in the post I just created in the community. I'm going to share my screen and answer them one by one."*

Then:
1. Create a community post: *"Live Q&A — drop your questions below!"* (add a poll too)
2. Share your screen showing the post
3. Answer questions directly from the comments

**Why this is better:**
- No character limit — better questions
- You highlight each question on screen (clear for everyone watching)
- Every comment drives community engagement
- Higher engagement = higher Discovery ranking
- The post stays live after the stream → more comments, more likes, more ranking

**Rule:** Use the live stream for teaching. Use a community post for Q&A. Always.

---

### After the Workshop: Post the Recording

1. End the live stream → Skool generates a recording in a few minutes
2. Get notified → Create a post with the recording
3. Add a poll: *"What was your biggest takeaway from today's workshop?"* or *"Which traffic strategy resonated most?"*
4. Include a CTA link to your booking page
5. Pin the post in the community

**Result:** Even more comments, likes, and engagement — from people who missed it live. More Discovery ranking. More calls booked.

---

### Building Your Workshop Slides

No need to start from scratch. Use the same slide format you built for your free course.

The workshop is just a live version of a free course video — covering a topic at the "what" level, then pitching the call.

Your slides already exist. Your structure already works. Just go live.

## Video 6: Skool Engagement Secrets

### Why Engagement Matters

Skool Discovery ranking is based on community engagement. The higher you rank, the more free traffic you get from Skool's Discovery page.

**Max's results (first 30 days of the free community):**
- 25,000+ members
- Ranked **#24 most active community** on the entire Skool platform
- Ranked **#6 in the Money category**
- Featured on the first page of Google

This didn't happen by accident. Here's exactly how.

---

### Strategy 1: Pin Check-In Posts Under Each Module

Under every module/video in your free course, create and pin a check-in post.

**What it does:** After watching the video, members complete the post as an action item. They vote in the poll, leave a comment with their biggest takeaway, and mark the module complete.

**Result:** Every member who goes through the course generates at least one comment and one poll vote per video. That's dozens of engagement actions happening daily without you doing anything extra.

**How to set it up:**
1. Create a post in your community
2. Add a poll (e.g., "Biggest takeaway from this video?") with 2–3 options
3. Ask them to comment their biggest takeaway
4. Pin it to the specific course module page

Go into Max's free Skool community and copy the exact format he uses.

---

### Strategy 2: Accountability Posts

Similar to check-in posts, but focused on accountability actions.

Create templates for members to publish as they move through the free course:
- *"Just finished [Video X] — my biggest takeaway was ___. My action step this week is ___."*
- Pin these under the relevant video

Every published post = more likes and comments from the community = more engagement. Members also feel more accountable, which means better results.

---

### Strategy 3: Be an Active Owner

Skool has confirmed directly: **active owner is one of the biggest ranking factors.**

What this looks like:
- Reply to posts and comments daily
- Welcome new members by name
- Create posts with polls multiple times per week
- Pin great member posts to recognize them
- Post behind-the-scenes updates, personal content, fun questions

Activity from the owner = activity from the members. Your posts get more engagement than anyone else's because everyone in the community is notified when you post.

---

### Strategy 4: Daily Posts + Polls

Post 4–6 times per week in your free community. Mix of:
- Email blast posts (Monday + Thursday) → value + CTA
- Workshop promotion posts (Thursday, Friday, Saturday)
- Workshop recording post (Sunday after the workshop)
- Fun personal posts, polls, behind-the-scenes
- Value posts with a soft CTA

Posts don't all have to be serious or promotional. A poll asking "What's your biggest challenge this week?" or a behind-the-scenes photo gets real engagement and builds personality.

---

### Strategy 5: Live Streams + Q&A Posts

Already covered in the webinars section — but worth repeating here:

- Go live 1–2 times per week (including your Sunday workshop)
- Do unannounced random live streams mid-week
- Always do the Q&A inside a community post, not in the live stream chat
- Post the recording afterward with a poll and a CTA

Every comment on every post counts toward your ranking.

---

### Strategy 6: Contests and Giveaways

Run a monthly giveaway where community activity earns entries:

**Max's system:**
- Level 3 = 1 entry
- Level 5 = 3 entries
- Level 7 = 5 entries
- Raffle winner announced at end of each month
- **Activity requirement:** Winner must have been active in the past 30 days — if not, pick again

**What to give away:**
- Apple products (AirPods, Apple Watch, iPad)
- A coaching call with you
- A valuable resource or tool
- Any prize relevant to your niche

**Why this works:** People love contests. Higher level = more chances to win = incentive to stay active in the community month after month.

---

### The Engagement Flywheel

More posts + comments + polls + live streams + engagement
→ Higher Skool Discovery ranking
→ More free members joining daily
→ More people engaging
→ Even higher ranking

Once it starts spinning, it compounds. And you can see exactly how Max does all of this by just joining the free Skool community — everything is right there to model.

## Video 7: Video Email Sequence

### Why Plain Text Emails Aren't Enough

Most welcome sequences are just text emails with a pitch at the bottom. People open them, skim them, and forget them.

The video email sequence changes everything.

---

### How It Works

Instead of sending text-only emails, each email contains:
1. A hook and intro
2. A screenshot/thumbnail of a training video on a landing page
3. A link to that landing page
4. A CTA to book a call

On the landing page:
- An exclusive training video (hosted on Tella)
- A CTA button below the video to book a call
- A "locked" navigation showing future videos they can't access yet

---

### Why This Converts Better

| Benefit | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------------|
| **Higher perceived value** | Each email contains a private training — not just a pitch |
| **Locked content** | The only way to watch the next video is to open the next email → trains them to open every email |
| **Anticipation** | They look forward to the next email because they know a new training is waiting |
| **Video builds trust faster** | They hear your voice, see your face, feel your energy — far more powerful than text |
| **Automated call generation** | Once built, this runs forever — every new lead gets the same high-quality experience |
| **Soft selling** | You give value first, then naturally invite them to book a call |

> "I wish I started doing this a lot earlier. Our booked calls went up significantly when we made our welcome sequence actually valuable."

---

### The Structure: 7 Emails, 7 Landing Pages

Max runs 7 emails over 14 days (one every other day). Each email links to a unique landing page with a different training video.

**How the "locked" navigation works:**
- Landing page has a two-column layout
- Left column: text navigation showing "Video 1 (unlocked), Video 2 (locked), Video 3 (locked)..."
- Each item is just a text link with a divider line underneath (not a fancy menu)
- The lock is just the link not being active — they can't click it until they get the email with the real URL

**This gamifies the experience.** They want to open the next email to unlock the next video.

---

### Email Copy Structure

Every email follows the same pattern:

1. **Subject line** — Curiosity or value hook (e.g., "How I get traffic, leads and sales")
2. **Opener** — Short, friendly, connects to the video
3. **Screenshot** of the video landing page (with a link)
4. **"Watch the video here"** — link to the landing page
5. **CTA to book a call** at the bottom
6. **PS** — Preview the next email: *"In the next email, I'll show you exactly how I got 25,000 members in 30 days. Don't miss it."*

**Email 1 special instructions:**
- Ask them to mark the email as important (or star it in Gmail)
- Explain why: it tells the email provider to keep future emails in the inbox
- Include a screenshot showing them how to do it
- Add a simple to-do list: Watch the video → Mark as important → Reply to this email → Dive into the community

Getting a reply on email 1 dramatically improves deliverability for the entire sequence.

---

### Building the Landing Pages

**Use your existing VSL funnel template.** The layout is the same — headline, video, CTA button. You're just adding a two-column navigation section.

**Step-by-step setup in GoHighLevel:**
1. Start with your existing VSL/start page template (e.g., yoursite.com/start)
2. Keep the logo and headline
3. Add a two-column section
4. Left column: plain text listing each video (Video 1 → active link, Videos 2–7 → gray text, no link)
5. Add a divider line under each item
6. Right column (or full width): embed your Tella video
7. Add a button below the video → links to your booking page

**How to get the Tella embed code:**
- Go to Tella → your video → Share → Embed
- Turn on animated thumbnail
- Select Responsive
- Copy code → paste into your GHL page builder as a custom HTML element

---

### Recording the Videos

You don't need new content. Use your existing free course material.

**Max's approach:**
- Each landing page video = a mini version of a topic already covered in the free course
- Different angle, different points, ~10–15 minutes
- It still feels exclusive because you're going deeper or framing it differently

**Topics for a 7-video sequence (example):**
1. Welcome + overview of the system
2. How to pick a profitable niche
3. How to get traffic (what you need to do, not how)
4. How to do promotions (overview)
5. How to sell — the sales call process
6. Why Skool is the best platform right now
7. The 90-day plan to launch your course + CTA to book a call

Record all 7 in one day. It's worth it.

---

### Setting Up the Automation

You don't need a new automation. Use the **existing welcome email sequence** you set up in Launch OS.

The automation already triggers when someone joins your free Skool community → their email gets sent to GoHighLevel via Zapier (SAP integration) → GHL fires the email sequence.

All you're doing is upgrading the content of the emails from plain text pitches to rich video emails with landing pages.

---

### Time Investment

- **Day 1:** Write 7 emails and build 7 landing pages (copy/edit the VSL template 7 times)
- **Day 2:** Record 7 short videos (15–30 minutes each, all on Tella)
- **Day 3:** Embed videos, connect everything, test

That's it. Set it up once. Every new lead gets the same premium experience forever.

---

### Summary: The Three Things That Make It Work

1. **Locked content** → incentivizes opening every email
2. **Video on landing pages** → builds trust faster than text alone
3. **Double CTA** → you pitch the call inside the video AND on the page below

> "Once you set this up, it runs on autopilot and turns new leads into booked calls daily."
