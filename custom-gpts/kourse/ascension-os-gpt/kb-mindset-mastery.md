## Video 2: Self Mastery

### Know Thyself — Why It Matters

Most decisions happen on autopilot. A hidden part of you is making calls in the background. Until you notice it and work with it, it runs your life.

We're all running belief systems installed since childhood — by teachers, parents, authority figures. These beliefs shape what we think is possible, what we think we deserve, and how we show up every single day. The scary thing is most of these beliefs operate below the surface, in the subconscious, without us even being aware of them.

**The practical reality:** You cannot reach a bigger goal while living inside the mental bubble you're in right now. You have to start thinking bigger to pull ideas from that bigger reality. And that starts with becoming aware of where you currently are.

### Imposter Syndrome
Very common, even at high levels. Max has spoken with people generating $200K/month who still didn't feel ready to teach others. They thought they needed to hit $1M/month first.

The truth: if you've done something and gotten results, you have value to teach. The story you tell yourself about not being ready is just a belief — not a fact.

### Who Am I?

Over time you collect labels. What you like, what you hate, what you're good at, what you're not. Eventually you start calling that collection "me."

The problem: **You built this version of yourself. And if it was built, it can be rebuilt.**

Think of it like driving a car. You look through the front windshield — toward where you're going — 95% of the time. You only glance in the rear mirror occasionally. Most people live their lives staring in the rear mirror, wondering why they keep crashing.

> "Who am I becoming?" is more important than "Who am I right now?"

**The change is guaranteed either way.** Whether you work on it or not, you will be different in a year. The question is: are you consciously designing that change, or just letting it happen?

### Self Awareness

Understanding how you work — your emotions, habits, biases, patterns, strengths, weaknesses, and where they all came from.

Without self awareness, life feels like something that just happens to you. With it, you start to see the patterns and take control.

**Positive mindset — the practical case:**
- You become a better problem solver (see solutions, not just problems)
- You stop blaming others and take full responsibility for results
- You become more pleasant to be around (for yourself and others)
- You perform better in business — simpler, cleaner decisions

The mistake: thinking who you are right now is fixed. It's not. You are not an unchangeable identity. You are a work in progress.

### Understanding Your Current Self — The Baseline

You can't improve what you haven't measured. The same way you wouldn't optimize an ad without data, you can't grow as a person without a clear baseline.

**How to create your baseline:**
- Honest self reflection
- Structured questions and simple experiments
- Personality assessments (Myers Briggs, DISC)
- Feedback from people who know you well

> "What you track improves. What gets feedback improves faster. What gets rewarded repeats. What repeats long enough becomes your life."

### The Dog on a Nail Story

Two guys on a porch. Their dog is whining. One guy asks why. The other says: "He's lying on a nail." "Why doesn't he move?" "Doesn't hurt bad enough."

Most people are living on a nail. It's uncomfortable but not uncomfortable enough to force change. Real change happens when the pain builds to a point where you simply cannot stay where you are anymore.

**Use pain as a motivator.** Get genuinely pissed off about where you are. Use that contracted energy to take action. Once the pain is high enough, you finally start moving.

### Hidden Secrets & Practical Tools

**Transcendental Meditation (TM):**
- Twice per day, 20 minutes each (morning and before dinner)
- Use a nonsensical two-syllable mantra repeated silently in your head
- Not about clearing your mind — it's about focusing your thoughts
- 20 minutes of TM = roughly the restorative equivalent of 4–5 hours of deep sleep
- Proven to lower stress and blood pressure — only meditation approved by the American Heart Association
- Learn it at a TM center (they have free intro sessions worldwide)
- Life-changing — Max started in March 2020 and attributes major improvements in calm, creativity, and focus to it
- Conor McGregor, Ray Dalio (attributes major success to TM), many top performers use it

**Think Tank Sessions:**
- 30–60 minutes, multiple times per week
- Sit quietly, speak to yourself, ask questions: "What am I missing? How can I make this better? What needs to change?"
- Your subconscious works on these problems while you sleep
- Best ideas come the next morning — in the shower, on a walk, at breakfast
- Max's think tanks have generated the highest-ROI ideas of his entire business journey

**Other daily practices:**
- Write down 25 things you're grateful for each morning (from a priest — very powerful)
- Visualization — see your future self, feel it, make it vivid. Daily practice for weeks builds the skill
- Journaling — write as if you've already reached your goals
- Morning routine: exercise (cardio + gym), quality breakfast, start early
- Evening routine: limit screens, review goals, visualize, calm music, same bedtime every night
- Positive self-talk — catch negative thoughts, replace them immediately
- Oura Ring — track sleep quality and consistency
- Apple Watch — track workouts

**Sleep:**
- Max needs 8–9 hours of sleep + 8.5–9.5 hours in bed
- Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day — no exceptions
- Sam Ovens quote: "If you don't have the discipline to go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, you won't have discipline to do literally anything else."
- Top performers (Jordan, Kobe, many athletes) sleep 8–9 hours and go to bed early

## Video 3: Discipline

### The Real Equation
10% tactics, 90% mindset. Discipline is what bridges the two. You can know exactly what to do — have the traffic checklist, promo checklist, annual planner — but without discipline, none of it gets executed.

> "Motivation fades. Discipline remains. Masters rely on discipline, not feelings."

### How to Develop Self-Discipline

**Control impulses**
- Say no to unnecessary scrolling, eating, shopping, distraction
- When you feel the urge to quit or escape, that's exactly when you push through
- Over time, those impulses decrease because your brain stops offering them

**Embrace discomfort**
- Put yourself in hard situations on purpose
- Discomfort is a signal that you're growing and evolving
- Face difficulty, feel the pain, and accept it — then push through anyway

**Exercise daily**
- Doesn't have to be intense — even a daily walk counts
- Max does 45–60 min on an exercise bike every morning (average 130–145 BPM)
- Gym every other day for strength training
- Clears your mind, builds discipline, creates energy for the rest of the day

**Eat clean**
- Simple, healthy diet
- Don't overcomplicate it — just reduce junk and eat real food
- Better nutrition = sharper focus and stronger discipline

**Meditate daily**
- 20 minutes, before you work, no skip days
- Consistency > intensity
- See Video 2 for full breakdown of the three types of meditation

**Sleep discipline**
- Same bedtime. Same wake time. Every single day.
- Sleep is a form of self-control — protecting it is one of the highest leverage habits you have

**Organization**
- Keep your space, systems, and environment clean and ordered
- Chaos kills discipline
- **How you do one thing is how you do everything** — if your car is a mess, your desk is a mess, your business is a mess
- If your environment is organized, your thinking will be organized

**Time control**
- Plan your months, weeks, and days intentionally
- Protect your time aggressively
- Use the annual planner + performance tracker

**Goal focus**
- Review your vision, mission, and goals constantly
- Use the Paradigm Shift Workbook daily
- Write down what you want every single day — every morning

**Gratitude**
- Balance ambition with gratitude to avoid fear, stress, and burnout
- The performance tracker helps with this — you can look back and see the progress you've made even in tough months

**Consistency**
- Stick to the plan regardless of emotions, setbacks, or uncertainty
- Plan your work. Work your plan.

### The 100 Units of Time Exercise

One of the most powerful exercises for identifying what to sacrifice.

**How to do it:**
1. For **7 days**, write down what you do every 30 minutes
2. Group activities and total the time spent on each
3. Be completely honest — if you scrolled TikTok for 2 hours, write it down
4. Review the results — you'll see clearly where your time actually goes

This exercise shows you the truth of your priorities. It's easy to say you're working hard. This exercise proves whether that's true.

> "Imagine spending those 10 hours you wasted scrolling on planning your VSL instead."

### What to Sacrifice

Winning requires sacrifice. If you don't sacrifice for your goal, your goal becomes the sacrifice.

**What stays:** health, sleep, your closest relationships
**Everything else is on the table**

Kobe Bryant said he sacrificed everything that wasn't basketball. He lost friends who stopped calling because he never answered. But his family understood. The people who truly care about you will support what you're doing.

**Resistance = fear.** When you say "I can't do that," it usually means you don't want to or you're afraid to let it go. You can learn anything if you apply yourself consistently over time.

### Why Discipline Is the Real Advantage

**Motivation vs. discipline:**
- Motivation is temporary — it's a feeling that comes and goes
- Discipline is a system — it works regardless of how you feel
- High performers don't rely on motivation. They're disciplined whether they feel like it or not.

**Win by default:**
- Most people quit before the finish line
- If you just stay consistent longer than everyone else, you will win
- It's not a race — it's a long game, and persistence beats talent in the long run

**Winners focus on winning. Losers focus on winners.**
- Don't watch what competitors are doing — you'll always be one step behind
- Focus on your own game, your own metrics, your own improvement
- By the time they copy what you're doing, you're already 10 steps ahead

**Self discipline defined:** The ability to control your actions and stay focused despite difficulty or distraction.

**Delayed gratification:** Train yourself to value the future reward over the immediate pleasure. Scrolling your phone feels good now but robs you of the discipline and time that creates results later.
