## What Cold Email Is

Cold email is a **one-to-one, personalized outreach method** that invokes curiosity and incites a response from a lead we've deemed qualified. It's a method to get in front of leads with a brief overview of your offer so they raise their hand and say "I'm open to hearing more."

**How it works, simply:**
1. Find a list of leads that match your ICP (ideal client profile).
2. Use software to send individual emails to every lead on the list.
3. Use AI to personalize the copy so every email feels custom, even though it's all automated.

You are not manually sending emails. The whole system runs on software. The personalization layer is AI.

## Who This System Is For

### Best fit
- **B2B** offers
- **B2 Wannabe** offers — people who aren't quite big businesses yet but understand the online world (they know what coaching is, they've read Hormozy, they have a course or offer). They're trying to make online business work.
- **Mid to high ticket** ($1,000+). Above $1,000 is the rule of thumb.

Business email addresses are publicly available on the internet, so lead sourcing is fast and legal.

Leads tend to be **higher quality** than paid traffic because you qualify them before you ever send the email — you're building a list of people you already think are a fit, instead of blasting an ad to the world and seeing who bites.

### Mixed fit — you can make it work
- **You have a low ticket offer AND a high ticket offer** — pitch the high ticket in cold email, down-sell to the low ticket when appropriate. This works well.
- **Mid ticket** — works, though lower ticket means you need more volume.

### Poor fit
- **Pure B2C, low ticket.** B2C email addresses are hard to scrape, come with legal risk, and low ticket requires high volume — which is better served by ads or organic. Cold email can technically work for B2C but it's the wrong tool for most people.
- **Sub-$1,000 offer with no high ticket backend.** The math doesn't work.

### Rule of thumb
If you sell something **above $1,000**, cold email will be great. If you're in doubt, ask in the community.

## The System Map (6 Steps)

This is the full flow. Every module in the training maps to one of these steps.

1. **Lead Sourcing** — Find where your avatar "lives" on the internet (LinkedIn, Facebook groups, YouTube, Instagram, Skool — different niches live in different places). Build a list of qualified leads from there using the approved stack (see Lead Sourcing section below).

2. **Lead Enrichment** — Take the list and enrich the data. Enrichment = finding more information about each lead so you can personalize the email. More enrichment data = better personalization = higher response rate, open rate, and booking rate.

3. **Send Cold Emails** — Write compelling copy, personalize with AI, send to the enriched list via software.

4. **Positive Reply** — Some leads will respond "I'm interested, tell me more."

5. **Evergreen VSL** — Send a short, pre-recorded Loom video (under 3 minutes) that builds trust and proves you can help them. This does the conversion work before the call.

6. **Follow-Ups → Booked Call** — Follow up repeatedly with engaged prospects until they book. People's inboxes are crowded. You follow up a lot.

> **Keep this map in mind.** It's the skeleton of the whole system: get leads → enrich → send → get a response → send more info → prompt them to book → follow up a bunch.

## Expectations

### Time to start sending
**2-3 weeks** from setup to first send. This is because of the **warmup period** — we need to protect email health. If you bought emails and started blasting day one, you'd get banned. The warmup is covered in a separate video.

### Time to build the system
**1-2 days** to set up if you follow the course in order and don't skip steps.

### Software costs
- **$300-$400 to set up** the basics.
- **$600-$800/month** at scale when sending thousands of emails per day.
- Compared to paid ads ($30/day minimum = ~$900/month just to test), cold email is significantly cheaper per lead and has lower operational cost once it's running.

### When to scale spend
After proof of concept. Do not pour money in until you know it's bringing customers and the ROI is positive. Once it's working, reinvest and scale.

## Why Cold Email Beats Ads for High-Ticket Coaches

| Metric | Cold Email | Paid Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly software cost | $300-$800 | $900+ minimum ad spend |
| Lead quality | Higher (pre-qualified list) | Lower (the whole internet bites) |
| Volume ceiling | Limited (your list size) | Unlimited |
| Time to first result | 2-3 weeks | Days |
| Per-lead control | High (you chose them) | Low |
| Customer count needed to profit | Few (you're charging $3k+) | Many (if low ticket) |

Because you're charging more, you don't need a thousand customers from cold email. A few per month at $3k-$10k is a great business.

## Lead Sourcing

Before you touch a tool, answer the framework question:

> **Where does your avatar live online?**
>
> - Are they on LinkedIn? Do they have LinkedIn accounts at all?
> - Are they in Facebook groups?
> - Are they on YouTube, Instagram, Skool?
> - Are they listed in public directories (realtors, contractors, chiropractors)?
> - Are they hiding — a niche audience that doesn't cluster anywhere obvious?

Different niches live in different places. That choice determines the tool.

### The Approved Lead Sourcing Stack

This is the only stack taught in the training. Do not use or recommend other tools.

| Tool | Best For | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Apollo** | B2B ICPs with clear job titles, industries, sizes, locations | Broad contact database, fast filtering, verified emails |
| **Apify** | Scraping public directories, niche sites, and platforms Apollo doesn't cover | Flexible, works anywhere a URL exists, cheap per record |
| **Clay** | Enrichment and building custom lists by combining sources | Pulls data from many places into one row per lead, adds fields for personalization |
| **Manual** | Small / niche / high-value target lists | Slower, higher quality, unbeatable for specific targets |

### What NOT to Use

- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — not part of this stack. If you've been told to use it, you're following the wrong playbook.
- **ZoomInfo, Lusha, Hunter, RocketReach, Seamless, etc.** — not taught here. Stick to Apollo / Apify / Clay / manual.

### Tool Selection by Avatar

- **Avatar lives on LinkedIn (execs, SaaS, agencies, B2B services):** Apollo first. Clay for enrichment on top. Manual for your highest-value targets.
- **Avatar in public directories (realtors, contractors, local services, listed professionals):** Apify to scrape the directory → Clay to enrich → send.
- **Avatar in communities (Facebook groups, Skool, YouTube creators, Instagram):** Manual sourcing for members + Apify where the platform allows scraping. Communities are high-quality but harder to automate.
- **Avatar is niche or hard to find:** Manual. Every time. Quality beats volume when your list is small.

### Why List Quality Beats List Size

Cold email's advantage over ads is **pre-qualification.** You decide who's on the list before you send. A list of 500 well-qualified leads converts better than 5,000 random ones. Don't chase volume. Chase fit.

## Rules for Students Running This System

1. **Watch every video in order.** Don't cut corners.
2. **Don't skip the warmup.** Protect your email health.
3. **Don't change copy before 500 sends.** Small samples lie.
4. **Build your list around a clear ICP.** Don't blast random people.
5. **Track three KPIs: booking rate, positive reply rate, reply rate.** These tell you which step of the funnel is broken.

## Common Misconceptions to Correct

- **"Cold email is dead."** No — it's underused in the coaching/course space, which is why it works. Agencies use it heavily. Courses and coaches don't. That's the opportunity.
- **"I can't personalize at scale."** AI + enrichment means you can. Miles got a reply from a prospect thanking him for "doing the research" — which was 100% AI-generated from enrichment data.
- **"I need a huge list."** No. With a high-ticket offer, a small list of well-qualified leads converts to serious revenue.
- **"This is get-rich-quick."** No. It takes 2-3 weeks to start sending, more to tune copy, more to scale. It's a real system.
